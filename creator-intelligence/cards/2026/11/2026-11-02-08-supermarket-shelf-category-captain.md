# Supermarkets outsource their own shelves

**The find:** Nearly every inch of a US supermarket shelf is mapped by a "planogram" built with the biggest manufacturer in each category, who is given "category captain" status to help decide where rivals and smaller brands get placed too.
**The surprise:** The store doesn't really control its own shelves. It outsources shelf strategy to the largest supplier in the category, who then shapes where its own competitors go.
**The idea:** [Category captain](../../../concepts/category-captain.md): letting the biggest player help design the "neutral" system you both compete in.
