# Announcing a Nudge Made It Work

**The find:** Researchers quietly reset 149 Belgian government employees' height-adjustable desks to standing position overnight; the silent default raised standing-work time 171%, but simply telling employees the desk had been changed on purpose more than doubled that result to 521%, and adding light coaching on top pushed it to 922%.
**The surprise:** Announcing the nudge out loud worked far better than hiding it — the opposite of the usual assumption that nudges only work if people don't notice them.
**The idea:** [The default effect](../../../concepts/default-effect.md): transparency about a nudge doesn't kill it, and can amplify it when stacked with a little explanation.
