# Airbnb's CEO calls himself terrible

**The find:** Brian Chesky, CEO of a company worth over $100 billion, tells Guy Raz: "I think I was pretty much naturally a great founder, and I was naturally a terrible CEO," then calls the standard advice to "hire great people and get out of their way" a "death sentence" for a young company.
**The surprise:** A famous founder openly says the most-repeated piece of Silicon Valley leadership wisdom nearly destroyed his own company.
**The idea:** [Founder mode](../../../concepts/founder-mode.md) — founder skills and manager skills aren't the same skillset, and delegate-and-disappear advice is tuned for mature companies, not young ones.
