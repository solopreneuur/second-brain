# A CEO admitted his own weak spot before a huge bet

**The find:** Announcing Constellation Software's 2013 acquisition of Total Specific Solutions, its biggest deal to date, CEO Mark Leonard wrote plainly to shareholders that "Constellation has not covered itself with glory outside of North America," noting the company's returns abroad had consistently run below its North American results.
**The surprise:** A famously press-shy, metrics-obsessed capital allocator volunteered a self-indictment of his own track record in exactly the market he was about to make his biggest-ever bet in, before anyone asked.
**The idea:** [Unfiltered admission](../../../concepts/unfiltered-admission.md): naming your own specific weak spot right when you're asking for more trust in that same spot is its own kind of leverage.
