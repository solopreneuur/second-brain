# Real-world nudges work 6x smaller than the lab says

**The find:** Comparing 126 government "nudge unit" field trials (23 million people) against nudge studies in academic journals: academic papers reported an average 8.7 percentage-point effect; the real government trials averaged only 1.4 points, about 84% smaller.
**The surprise:** The nudge literature that built an entire consulting industry overstates real-world impact roughly 6x by its own authors' reckoning, mostly from publication bias and underpowered academic samples.
**The idea:** [Publication bias](../../../concepts/publication-bias.md): the gap between a flashy single study's effect size and what actually happens at scale.
