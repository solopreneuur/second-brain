# Say "sari" instead of "kilt" enough times

**The find:** The illusory truth effect names the finding that repeated exposure to a false statement makes it feel truer, even to people who initially knew it was false. A 2015 study got participants who correctly knew what a kilt was to start doubting themselves after repeated false claims calling it a "sari."
**The surprise:** Even factual knowledge doesn't fully protect you — repetition alone manufactures a feeling of truth that is independent of logic, memory, or what you actually learned.
**The idea:** Familiarity gets mistaken for accuracy, so the fastest way to make a claim feel true isn't evidence, it's repetition ([Illusory truth effect](../../../concepts/illusory-truth-effect.md)).
