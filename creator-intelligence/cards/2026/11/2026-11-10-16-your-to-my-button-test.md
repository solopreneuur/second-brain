# Changing "your" to "my" doubled signups

**The find:** A copywriter changed a trial button from "Start YOUR free trial" to "Start MY free trial" — one possessive word — and got a 90% lift in sign-ups; the same swap repeated on other sites consistently lifted conversion 30-90%.
**The surprise:** The sentence says almost the same thing either way, but switching from the brand's voice to the visitor's own internal voice doubled conversion.
**The idea:** Self-referential copy — writing in the reader's own first-person voice mimics the decision already forming in their head, lowering the last friction to click.
