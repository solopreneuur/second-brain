# Netflix's "right" countdown was a guess

**The find:** The engineer who built Netflix's "next episode" autoplay says the team tested 5, 10 and 15-second countdowns before shipping. Ten seconds produced the biggest lift in hours watched of any feature they'd tested at the time.
**The surprise:** The team's own explanation was that 10 seconds "felt right" — long enough to digest an episode, short enough not to feel jarring. Netflix later cut it to 5 seconds and watch time rose again, so the "digestion time" story wasn't the real driver.
**The idea:** [Default effect](../../../concepts/default-effect.md): flipping the decision from "choose to continue" to "choose to stop" does almost all the work; the exact timer is secondary, and the reason given for it afterward is often just a story.
