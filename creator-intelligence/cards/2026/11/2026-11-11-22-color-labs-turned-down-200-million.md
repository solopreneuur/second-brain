# A startup turned down $200M before launch

**The find:** Color Labs raised $41 million before launch at a $167 million valuation, and reportedly turned down a $200 million acquisition offer from Google — before a single user had opened the app.
**The surprise:** It launched to ridicule, peaked at 440,000 users, and sold to Apple for $2-5 million about a year later — a fraction of the offer it had turned down before anyone could use the product.
**The idea:** [Premature valuation collapse](../../../concepts/premature-valuation-collapse.md) — money and belief can arrive so far ahead of any real usage that the eventual collapse has nowhere soft to land.
