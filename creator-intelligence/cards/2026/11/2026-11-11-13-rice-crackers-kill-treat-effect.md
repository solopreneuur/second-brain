# Rice crackers killed the treat-yourself effect

**The find:** A follow-up study on "treating yourself" found that letting people choose their own snack only made them more generous to others if the snack was a genuine indulgence. Swap the choice to rice crackers instead of chocolate truffles, and the generosity boost disappears completely — donations were statistically identical to a no-snack control.
**The surprise:** It isn't the self-chosen part of "treat yourself" that does the work — a boring snack picked for yourself buys you nothing.
**The idea:** [Hedonic treat requirement](../../../concepts/hedonic-treat-requirement.md) — self-compassion effects need an actual pleasure, not just the appearance of choice.
