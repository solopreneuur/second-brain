# Five feet decides if your house burns

**The find:** Berkeley's wildfire ordinance creates "Zone Zero," a non-negotiable zero-to-five-foot buffer around a house where almost nothing is allowed to grow, because that narrow ring is disproportionately what decides whether the house ignites.
**The surprise:** The single highest-leverage safety fix isn't broad vigilance over the whole property, it's ruthless control of a strip five feet wide — and removing it feels like a loss to residents attached to gardens planted against their walls.
**The idea:** The few inches or seconds closest to the point of ignition carry most of the risk; find and guard that narrow zone instead of trying to control everything.
