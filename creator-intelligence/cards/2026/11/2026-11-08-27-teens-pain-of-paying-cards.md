# Teenagers Feel Cards More Than Cash

**The find:** A Dutch central bank study of 2,497 people found cash hurts more to spend than card for most adults — but teenagers show the reverse, feeling more pain paying by debit card or phone than by cash, because handing over paper doesn't register as "real money" the way a changing number on a banking app does.
**The surprise:** Nearly every cashless-is-frictionless narrative assumes digital payment always hurts less; it flips entirely for the generation that grew up without a visceral "handing over paper" association.
**The idea:** [Pain of paying](../../../concepts/pain-of-paying.md) is a learned, generational construct tied to what feels like "real money" to you, not a fixed property of cash versus card.
