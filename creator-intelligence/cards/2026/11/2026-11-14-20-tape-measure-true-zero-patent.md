# The tape measure's "broken" hook

**The find:** The loose metal hook on the end of nearly every tape measure looks like sloppy manufacturing, but it's a patented mechanism: the hook is deliberately offset by exactly its own material thickness so it still reads a "true zero" whether you're hooking it outside an edge or butting it inside a corner.
**The surprise:** Generations of people have assumed the wobble was wear-and-tear or a defect; it's a patented dual-mode mechanism solving the same accuracy problem two opposite ways with one moving part.
**The idea:** The thing that looks broken or careless about a mass-produced object is sometimes the most engineered part of it, built to work invisibly in two directions at once ([dual-mode design](../../../concepts/dual-mode-design.md)).
