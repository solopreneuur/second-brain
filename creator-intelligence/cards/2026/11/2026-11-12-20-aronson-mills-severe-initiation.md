# A harder initiation made people love the group more

**The find:** Elliot Aronson and Judson Mills told women they had to pass an embarrassing "test" (reading obscene material aloud to a male experimenter) to join a discussion group. Women who went through the severe version rated the group, which was actually a boring, canned recording, far more favorably (97.6 on a liking scale) than women who had a mild initiation (81.8) or none at all (80.2).
**The surprise:** The group never even interacted with them live. The women suffered to get in, and that suffering alone was enough to make them talk themselves into loving a group that, objectively, bored everyone equally.
**The idea:** When effort and discomfort can't be justified by the thing you got, the mind resolves the tension by deciding the thing must have been worth it after all.
