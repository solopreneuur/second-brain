# A leaderboard tripled Duolingo's most engaged users

**The find:** When Duolingo rebuilt its competitive weekly leaderboards, total learning time on the app rose 17% — and the number of "highly engaged" learners, studying an hour a day five days a week, tripled. The team considered a 1% lift on a major metric a big win internally.
**The surprise:** A pure social-competition feature, no new content and no new product, beat what the team called a great result by roughly seventeen times over on one metric.
**The idea:** none
