# A court ruled "refusing to get rich" was legal

**The find:** Craigslist CEO Jim Buckmaster told a UBS analyst in 2006 that Craigslist ran on "a business model which, by any rational standard, is completely insane" — no display ads, no revenue-maximizing moves, on one of the highest-traffic sites in America. When minority shareholder eBay sued to force Craigslist toward profit-maximizing behavior, Delaware's Chancery Court ruled against eBay, affirming that directors aren't legally required to maximize shareholder wealth if that isn't the company's chosen model.
**The surprise:** A real court ruling validated "refuse to make more money" as a legitimate corporate strategy, not just a founder's eccentricity.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md): optimizing for user utility instead of monetization can itself be the moat, and refusing to grow revenue isn't a legal requirement to abandon, just a default.
