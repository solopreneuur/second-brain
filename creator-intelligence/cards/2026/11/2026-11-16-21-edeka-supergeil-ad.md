# The grocery ad that never mentions groceries

**The find:** In 2014, German supermarket chain EDEKA released a five-minute video of 58-year-old performance artist Friedrich Liechtenstein flamboyantly crooning "Supergeil" ("super awesome") about ordinary grocery items, breaking the standard rule that an ad must clearly sell the product with a call to action.
**The surprise:** With almost no paid media, the video reached 12.6 million people and 362 international write-ups for a grocery launch — the kind of reach normally bought, not earned, by conventional category advertising.
**The idea:** [Spectacle as feature](../../../concepts/spectacle-as-feature.md): an ad that refuses to look like an ad gets shared past the exact filter that would bury a conventional sell.
