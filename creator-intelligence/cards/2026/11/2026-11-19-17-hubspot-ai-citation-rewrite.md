# Same text, 642% more AI citations

**The find:** HubSpot rewrote key page content from flowing paragraphs into blunt subject-predicate-object sentences ("HubSpot can automate email marketing"), without adding or removing any actual information.
**The surprise:** AI engines citing HubSpot's pages jumped 642%, and brand mentions inside AI-generated answers rose 58% — from a grammar change alone.
**The idea:** Large language models reward sentences shaped like discrete facts over sentences shaped like prose, so the same information becomes more machine-citable just by resequencing it.
