# Hermès won't make a Birkin faster

**The find:** Hermès has kept leather-goods volume growth capped at 6-7% a year despite outstripping demand. CEO Axel Dumas says a Birkin takes exactly 15 hours to make and "I'm not going to start doing them in 13 hours," even when a shortfall cost the division a 5.4% sales decline in one quarter.
**The surprise:** The company chose to report a sales miss rather than speed up stitching, turning a hard manufacturing constraint into the scarcity that fuels its waiting lists.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md): a rule a company binds itself to, giving up short-term gain to protect long-term discipline and trust.
