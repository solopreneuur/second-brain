# Google's money-losing lucky button

**The find:** Google's "I'm Feeling Lucky" button skips search results — and the ad impressions that come with them — on every single use, costing Google ad revenue each time, yet it stayed on the homepage for over a decade. Employee #59 Doug Edwards says Sergey Brin insisted on keeping it as proof the algorithm was good enough to trust completely.
**The surprise:** A core homepage feature's entire purpose was to make the company less money, kept alive for over ten years as an ideological statement.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md): deliberately keeping something costly as proof of a principle.
