# A Second Button Steals Nothing

**The find:** Email-marketing tool GetResponse added a second "Free Trial" button beside its existing "Buy Now" button on the same page.
**The surprise:** The team expected the new option to cannibalize the paid one; instead free-trial clicks rose 158.6% while paid signups didn't drop at all.
**The idea:** A [lower-commitment option](../../../concepts/micro-commitment.md) doesn't steal from the high-commitment path — it just opens a lane that wasn't there before.
