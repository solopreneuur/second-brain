# AI is already winning literary prizes

**The find:** Four of the six 2025-2026 winners of the Commonwealth Short Story Prize were flagged as 88-100% AI-generated by detection tool Pangram, after thirteen consecutive clean years; other recent prize-winning stories and a prize-winning novel were flagged the same way, with one publisher dropping its author once the metadata was exposed.
**The surprise:** A 2024 Pittsburgh study found readers can barely tell AI poetry from human poetry — and even rate AI poems as more beautiful — meaning it's not that AI mastered literary craft, it's that any judged metric, once it's the thing being optimized for, becomes gameable.
**The idea:** [Goodhart's law](../../../concepts/goodharts-law.md): the moment a proxy for quality becomes the measured target, people (or models) learn to win the proxy instead of improving the underlying thing.
