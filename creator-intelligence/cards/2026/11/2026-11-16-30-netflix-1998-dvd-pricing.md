# Netflix started by charging the late fees it later killed

**The find:** Netflix's original 1998 site, preserved on the Wayback Machine, charged $4 for a first movie rental and $3 for each additional one, plus shipping, with a 7-day limit and late fees — essentially Blockbuster by mail. The flat-rate, no-due-dates subscription that defines Netflix today didn't launch until September 1999.
**The surprise:** The company famous for killing the late fee started out charging one.
