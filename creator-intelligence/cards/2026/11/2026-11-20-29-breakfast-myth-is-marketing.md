# A Harvard scientist outs breakfast myth

**The find:** David Sinclair, a Harvard genetics professor and longevity researcher, says on The Diary of a CEO that "three meals a day is craziness," and that the idea breakfast is the most important meal of the day was planted by early-20th-century cereal marketing.
**The surprise:** You expect a Harvard scientist to cite peer-reviewed studies on meal timing, not to casually name a century-old cereal-industry marketing line as the real origin of a "nutrition fact" most people treat as settled science.
**The idea:** A [manufactured tradition](../../../concepts/manufactured-tradition.md) — a marketing claim repeated long enough gets mistaken for scientific consensus.
