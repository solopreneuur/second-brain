# A historian names two kinds of hubris

**The find:** Naval War College strategy professor Dr. Sarah Paine coins two types of American hubris on air — "Type B," which "applies to some of the tech bros," is the belief that being great at one thing makes you great at everything.
**The surprise:** A historian talking geopolitics pivots into a clean, exportable leadership flaw — competence in one domain curdling into false authority in all domains, fixed only by people willing to contradict you.
**The idea:** Competence transfer illusion — expertise in one domain is mistaken for expertise in all domains, and surrounding yourself with agreement is how you lose the ability to see it.
