# Investors read your financial slide for 4 seconds

**The find:** Papermark tracked 358,672 real investor views across 24,541 pitch decks. The average full view lasted 4 minutes, but the median view was just 77 seconds. Inside that median view, the team slide got 5.7 seconds of attention and the financials slide got 3.9 seconds.
**The surprise:** Founders spend weeks building the financial model, but the data shows investors statistically almost never read it; the deal gets decided mostly on the first slide, in a handful of seconds.
**The idea:** Where people say the decision is made and where it's actually made are often different slides entirely.
