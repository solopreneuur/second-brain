# Vanguard's index fund, "Bogle's Folly"

**The find:** Jack Bogle's 1976 index fund IPO tried to raise $150 million and got $11.3 million — so little the fund couldn't even buy full lots of all 500 stocks it tracked. Wall Street called it "Bogle's Folly"; rivals printed posters of Uncle Sam stomping index fund certificates.
**The surprise:** The fund mocked as a flop on day one now holds roughly $1.67 trillion, and index investing is the industry norm it was accused of betraying.
**The idea:** Being laughed at for refusing to try to beat the market can be the market-beating move, decades out ([contrarian conviction](../../../concepts/contrarian-conviction.md)).
