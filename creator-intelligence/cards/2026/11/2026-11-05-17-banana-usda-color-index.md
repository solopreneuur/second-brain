# Your banana has a government number

**The find:** Every banana sold in the US is tracked on an official USDA 1-7 Color Index and deliberately shipped out of sealed "ripening rooms" at stage 3-4 after 24-48 hours of ethylene gas exposure, timed so it finishes ripening in transit and lands on the shelf at stage 5-6.
**The surprise:** The yellow banana on the shelf isn't ripening naturally on its own schedule — it's a numbered industrial output, backward-engineered from a delivery truck's arrival time.
**The idea:** [Logistics-driven design](../../../concepts/logistics-driven-design.md) — the appearance of nature is itself a manufactured supply-chain spec.
