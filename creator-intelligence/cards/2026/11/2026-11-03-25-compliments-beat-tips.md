# A compliment outlasted a tip

**The find:** Researchers bought ice cream and döner kebabs across 36 vendors, either tipping the seller in advance or simply complimenting them. Both produced more product per transaction, but the compliment's effect grew over five repeat visits while the tip's boost stayed flat.
**The surprise:** A free compliment created more durable, compounding goodwill than actual money — once the cost of the tip is subtracted, the words outperformed the cash.
**The idea:** [Reciprocity](../../../concepts/reciprocity.md) — immaterial gifts can out-earn material ones over repeated exchanges, because the warmth, not the value, carries the obligation.
