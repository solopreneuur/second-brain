# The fridge with no door

**The find:** Open-front supermarket refrigerator cases have no door — instead they blow a continuous sheet of cold air from top to bottom, an "air curtain," and researchers found roughly 75% of a case's cooling load goes just to fighting warm store air leaking through that invisible wall.
**The surprise:** Most of the electricity those "doorless" fridges burn isn't cooling your milk — it's running a losing, nonstop mechanical battle just to fake having a door.
**The idea:** An "open," convenient design often hides a continuous, expensive process working hard to simulate the function a simple static barrier would give for free ([hidden affordance](../../../concepts/hidden-affordance.md)).
