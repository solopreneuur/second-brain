# Resentment means you betrayed yourself

**The find:** A former private-equity investor turned executive coach to Google, Uber and Twitter founders argues that resentment toward a partner or boss isn't about them — it's proof you broke your own boundary by saying yes when you meant no.
**The surprise:** Resentment is normally read as evidence someone else wronged you; he flips it into a self-diagnosis of your own people-pleasing.
**The idea:** "Why resentment? Because resentment means you've abandoned yourself. If you ask me to do something and I don't want to do it, and then I do it, enough times I'm going to resent you, period."
