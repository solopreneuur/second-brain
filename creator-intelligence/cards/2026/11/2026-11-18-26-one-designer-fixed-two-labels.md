# One designer fixed two pill labels

**The find:** Designer Deborah Adler, who redesigned Target's ClearRx prescription bottle after a family member's medication mix-up, spent five years working with CVS on a second system, ScriptPath, patenting a follow-up technology (AdlerRx, granted 2021) to tackle the same identical-looking-medication problem for a different national pharmacy chain.
**The surprise:** The same designer re-ran her original incident-born design process twice, a decade apart, for two competing companies.
**The idea:** A design career can be built on repeating one playbook — turn a specific personal incident into a redesign brief — for client after client ([Incident-born design](../../../concepts/incident-born-design.md)).
