# Thirteen deaths wrote ten fire rules

**The find:** After the 1949 Mann Gulch fire killed 13 smokejumpers — echoing a nearly identical 1937 Blackwater fire disaster — the U.S. Forest Service codified ten non-negotiable rules firefighters must check before engaging any fire.
**The surprise:** It took a second, near-identical disaster, eight years after the first, before the Forest Service actually mandated rules it had already written down.
**The idea:** Written protocols don't prevent the first catastrophe — they're assembled from its wreckage, and often need a second one before anyone enforces them ([Rules written in blood](../../../concepts/rules-written-in-blood.md)).
