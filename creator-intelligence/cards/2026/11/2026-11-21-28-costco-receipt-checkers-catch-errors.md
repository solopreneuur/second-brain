# Costco's Receipt Checker Catches Cashiers

**The find:** A 12-year Costco employee, describing the job on Hacker News, says the person checking receipts at the exit stopped theft "a grand total of zero times" during their tenure — but catches cashier scan or pricing errors roughly twice an hour.
**The surprise:** The checkpoint everyone assumes is loss-prevention theater aimed at customers is actually a quality-assurance audit on the registers.
**The idea:** [Disguised function](../../../concepts/disguised-function.md) — the visible purpose of a ritual and its real job can be two different things entirely.
