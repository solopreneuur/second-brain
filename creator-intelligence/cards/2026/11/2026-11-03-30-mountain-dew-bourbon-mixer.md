# Mountain Dew was a bourbon mixer

**The find:** In the 1940s, Tennessee bottlers Barney and Ally Hartman couldn't find their favourite lemon-lime mixer for Old Taylor bourbon, so they bottled their own batches for personal use and friends, jokingly naming it "Mountain Dew" — Appalachian slang for moonshine.
**The surprise:** The soda now associated with hyper-caffeinated gamer culture started as an unlabeled whiskey mixer handed out to friends, with a hillbilly-with-a-rifle label drawn by a teenage neighbour as a joke — and later owners kept that branding because it had already become the product's real identity.
**The idea:** A product's voice can outlive and outsell the thing it was originally built to serve.
