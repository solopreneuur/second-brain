# Telling radiologists to look broader

**The find:** Fifty radiologists reviewed chest CTs for lung nodules; a final scan had an obvious breast mass and enlarged lymph node. Searching only for nodules, 67% missed the breast mass. Told to look broadly for any abnormality on the same scans, the miss rate collapsed to 3%.
**The surprise:** It's not that radiologists can't see a tumor — the task framing itself blinds them to it, nearly 20 times better detection from changing only what they were told to hunt for.
**The idea:** [Inattentional blindness](../../../concepts/inattentional-blindness.md): a narrow search instruction can blind an expert to something in plain sight.
