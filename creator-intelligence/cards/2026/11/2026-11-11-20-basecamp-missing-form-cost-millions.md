# An un-tested redesign cost Basecamp millions

**The find:** Basecamp removed its homepage signup form during a 2014 rebrand for aesthetic reasons and never tested the decision. Seventeen months later, a belated A/B test found that putting the form back raised signups 16%.
**The surprise:** Basecamp's own analyst estimated the unexamined redesign had quietly been costing the company millions of dollars the entire time nobody checked.
**The idea:** [Untested redesign tax](../../../concepts/untested-redesign-tax.md) — a design change made once, for taste, and never re-tested can silently tax a metric indefinitely.
