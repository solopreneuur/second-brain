# Groupon's ancestor was protest software

**The find:** Andrew Mason's 2007 site "The Point" pitched itself as a collective-action platform for boycotts and civic causes — built, by his own account in Groupon's SEC filing, to "solve the world's unsolvable problems." It barely worked until users repurposed the mechanic to crowd-buy a pizza discount; Groupon launched as a side project in November 2008.
**The surprise:** The multi-billion-dollar daily-deals company's actual insight was that people didn't want to save the world together — they wanted to save money together.
**The idea:** An [accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): the tool built for one purpose finds its real use in users' hands, not the founder's pitch.
