# Doing Good Makes Products Taste Better

**The find:** Researchers found that companies seen as socially responsible get a "benevolent halo" that spills into unrelated product judgments — a cookie is rated better-made and tastier if the box says 1% goes to charity, same cookie.
**The surprise:** Doing good doesn't just buy goodwill, it silently reprices the product itself, in ways that have nothing to do with the cause.
**The idea:** The [Noble Edge Effect](../../../concepts/noble-edge-effect.md), from Chernev & Blair's "Doing Well by Doing Good" (2015).
