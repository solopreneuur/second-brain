# The dome on your coffee lid is patented ergonomics

**The find:** The raised dome bump on a to-go coffee lid — Solo Cup's 1986 "Solo Traveler" design — is a patented shape engineered specifically to cradle your lip and nose, so you can drink safely from a moving vehicle without spilling or burning yourself.
**The surprise:** The detail people assume is just a cheap plastic quirk was deliberately shaped around the human face, with the patent itself spelling out exactly which part of your mouth it's designed to fit against.
**The idea:** A mass-produced object can be engineered around a specific human body part without ever telling you that's what it's doing ([Hidden affordance](../../../concepts/hidden-affordance.md)).
