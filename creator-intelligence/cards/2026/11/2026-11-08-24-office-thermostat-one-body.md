# Office Thermostats Assume One Body

**The find:** The ASHRAE 55 standard that sets office building temperatures worldwide, first published in 1966, is still calibrated around the metabolic rate of a hypothetical 40-year-old, 70kg man — a single body type used as the "average" occupant for an entire industry's climate-control math.
**The surprise:** A 2015 Nature Climate Change study found women's actual resting metabolic rates run meaningfully below that baseline, meaning the "neutral" office temperature most buildings target was never neutral — it was one body's comfort point, quietly exported to every building that cites the standard.
**The idea:** [Path dependency](../../../concepts/path-dependency.md): a decades-old technical default gets mistaken for a biological fact simply because nobody re-checks the assumption the standard was built on.
