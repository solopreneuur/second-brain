# Twitter launched as "an Odeo thingy"

**The find:** Twitter's earliest archived homepage, built inside the failing podcasting startup Odeo in 2006, didn't call itself a social network or a microblog — it described itself, almost apologetically, as a small side project with no clear category of its own.
**The surprise:** The product that would define a new communication format launched with no claim to one, inside a company whose actual business (podcasting) was about to be made obsolete by Apple adding podcasts to iTunes.
**The idea:** A side project built to kill time while the main business dies can end up outliving and outgrowing the company that housed it.
