# Hermès' bag-buying "lottery" survives court

**The find:** A 2024-2025 antitrust suit alleged Hermès runs a hidden allocation scheme forcing Birkin buyers to first purchase scarves, shoes, fragrance and homeware before being allowed to buy the bag; a judge dismissed the case twice, ruling that reserving bags for the highest-spending customers isn't illegal even if deliberate.
**The surprise:** The court didn't deny the scheme exists, it ruled that engineered unfairness toward the biggest spenders simply isn't the kind of harm antitrust law covers.
**The idea:** Tying scarcity to proven spending turns an exclusivity complaint into a [quality signal](../../../concepts/quality-signalling.md) the brand never has to admit to running.
