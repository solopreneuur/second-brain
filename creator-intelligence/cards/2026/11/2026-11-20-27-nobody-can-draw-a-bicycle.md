# Nobody can actually draw a bicycle

**The find:** Since 2009, designer Gianluca Gimini has asked hundreds of strangers to draw a bicycle from memory for his project "Velocipedia" — only about a quarter of roughly 376 respondents produced a mechanically correct frame; most drew bikes that physically couldn't be ridden, despite having seen and ridden bicycles thousands of times.
**The surprise:** People fail in the exact same handful of ways — frame geometry that defies physics — which means the gap isn't random ignorance, it's a specific, shared blind spot about a machine everyone is sure they understand.
**The idea:** The [illusion of explanatory depth](../../../concepts/illusion-of-explanatory-depth.md): familiarity with using a thing feels identical to understanding how it works, right up until you're asked to reconstruct it.
