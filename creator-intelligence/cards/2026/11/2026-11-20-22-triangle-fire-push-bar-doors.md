# A "fireproof" building bred fatal complacency

**The find:** The 1911 Triangle Shirtwaist Factory fire killed 146 workers partly because exit doors were locked and opened inward against the fleeing crowd. The aftermath produced the "free egress" standard and the panic-bar hardware now mandatory on every commercial exit door — built so escape requires zero special knowledge under panic.
**The surprise:** The building was marketed and regarded as "fireproof," and that reputation is exactly what gave management an excuse to skip real escape planning — the safety label bred the complacency that made the fire fatal.
**The idea:** [Design for panic](../../../concepts/design-for-panic.md), not for the calm user — an exit, a cancellation flow, or an off-ramp has to work without instructions, memory, or a key, because the person using it under stress can't be trusted to have any of those.
