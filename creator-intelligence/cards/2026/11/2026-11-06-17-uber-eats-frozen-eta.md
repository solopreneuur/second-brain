# Uber Eats froze its own ETA

**The find:** Uber Eats added a second, fixed delivery-time estimate that locks the moment you order and never updates, layered on top of the real-time ML estimate underneath. Design lead Andy Szybalski said the more-accurate live number made users feel "gaslit" every time it ticked backward, so the team froze a less-accurate number on top of it just to manage the feeling.
**The surprise:** The more honest, more accurate number was the one that felt like a lie — so the fix was to intentionally show a worse one.
**The idea:** A frozen anchor: a fixed, slightly wrong reference point can feel more trustworthy than a constantly-updating accurate one.
