# Spotify's founder bet 20x the going seed

**The find:** Daniel Ek, who built Spotify into a service with 550 million users, says he and his co-founder personally put roughly $10 million of their own money into the company at seed stage in 2007 — when a typical seed round at the time was about $500,000.
**The surprise:** He calls it "unheard of": before any outside investor believed streaming could work, he bet about twenty times the normal seed-stage amount on a company most assumed the record labels would simply kill.
**The idea:** [Betting past the line](../../../concepts/betting-past-the-line.md) — a founder putting in far more personal capital than the going rate is itself a signal, made before anyone else is willing to believe the bet.
