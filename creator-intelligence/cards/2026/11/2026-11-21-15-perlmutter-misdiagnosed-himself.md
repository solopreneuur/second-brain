# The Gluten Doctor Doubts Himself

**The find:** David Perlmutter, the neurologist whose bestseller "Grain Brain" made "gluten is bad for your brain" a household belief, admitted on-mic that his own self-diagnosis was probably wrong.
**The surprise:** "I actually used to go around saying I was gluten-free, but having learned a few things on this podcast, I think I might actually be fructan intolerant" — the man who built a career on the claim mislabeled his own condition for years.
**The idea:** [Unfiltered admission](../../../concepts/unfiltered-admission.md) — a credentialed expert naming the exact mistake his own authority was built on, with no hedge.
