# The hot hand "myth" was itself a myth

**The find:** Gilovich, Vallone & Tversky's 1985 finding that basketball "hot streaks" are a cognitive illusion became textbook canon for 30 years — until economists Miller & Sanjurjo found a hidden selection-bias flaw in the original method that, once corrected, revealed a small but real hot-hand effect in the same data.
**The surprise:** The tidy, famous "debunking" of a popular belief was itself a less tidy, under-examined statistical artifact — the myth-busting became the myth.
**The idea:** [Narrative fallacy](../../../concepts/narrative-fallacy.md): a clean, satisfying story about human bias can displace a messier truth just as easily as a clean story about human genius can.
