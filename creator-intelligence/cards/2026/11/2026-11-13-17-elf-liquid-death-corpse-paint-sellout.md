# e.l.f. and Liquid Death sold out in 45 minutes

**The find:** e.l.f. Beauty's own investor materials confirm that its "Corpse Paint" collaboration with Liquid Death — makeup branded like a canned-water company — sold out in 45 minutes.
**The surprise:** Two brands with nothing functionally in common (cosmetics and canned water) ran a joint drop deliberately priced and stocked to disappear almost instantly, turning scarcity itself into the headline metric worth disclosing to investors.
**The idea:** A hard deadline on supply can be worth more as a marketing number than the product itself ([Scarcity anchor](../../../concepts/scarcity-anchor.md)).
