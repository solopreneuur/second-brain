# Ancient warfare's 17-to-1 lineage gap

**The find:** Evolutionary psychologist David Geary says population-genetics data show that roughly 5,000-7,000 years ago, organized male-kin-group warfare crashed male lineage diversity so hard that at one point there were 17 surviving female ancestral lineages for every 1 surviving male lineage.
**The surprise:** Nearly all living humans trace back to a tiny fraction of male ancestors but almost all female ancestors — a fingerprint of ancient warfare still visible in human DNA.
**The idea:** A male lineage bottleneck: large-scale group conflict can leave a measurable, lopsided mark on a population's gene pool for thousands of years.
