# Tesla's 12-page loan needed zero pages

**The find:** Jon McNeill recalls asking Tesla's lawyer how many of the 12 pages in a standard auto-loan contract were actually legally required. The answer: none. The entire legally necessary content boils down to one paragraph — price, rate, term, payment.
**The surprise:** An entire industry's paperwork ritual, signed by every car buyer for decades, turns out to be zero percent legally mandated.
**The idea:** [Ritual without understanding](../../../concepts/ritual-without-understanding.md) — an institution keeps performing a lengthy procedure long after anyone can say why most of it exists.
