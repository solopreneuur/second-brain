# 217 investors said no to Starbucks

**The find:** When the original Starbucks founders rejected Howard Schultz's idea to bring Italian-style espresso bars to America, he quit and spent nearly two years raising money to build it himself — pitching 242 investors, 217 of whom said no.
**The surprise:** An 89% rejection rate sat behind the idea that became a $100+ billion coffee empire.
**The idea:** The rate at which people reject an idea measures how unfamiliar it is to them, not how good it is ([Rejection math](../../../concepts/rejection-math.md)).
