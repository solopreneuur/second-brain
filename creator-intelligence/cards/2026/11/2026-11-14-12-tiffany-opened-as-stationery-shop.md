# Tiffany opened as a stationery shop

**The find:** Tiffany & Co. opened in 1837 as "Tiffany & Young," a stationery and "fancy goods" store in New York; its first day of sales totaled $4.38. It didn't become a dedicated jewelry house until 1853.
**The surprise:** The brand synonymous with diamond engagement rings made less than five dollars on day one selling paper goods.
**The idea:** Category leadership is rarely the founding category — it's often discovered 15-plus years in.
