# Naming the payoff beat the ask

**The find:** ContentVerve's Michael Aagaard rewrote BettingExpert.com's sign-up form — "Join BettingExpert" / "Sign Up+" became "Get FREE Betting Tips" / "Sign Up & Get the Best Daily Tips" — and sign-ups rose 31.54% over a 13,560-visitor test.
**The surprise:** Naming what the user gets, instead of naming the action they have to take, moved the needle almost as hard as swapping a single word.
