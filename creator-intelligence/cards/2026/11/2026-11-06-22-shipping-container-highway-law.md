# A highway law sized shipping containers

**The find:** Malcom McLean's 1956 shipping container was built to 35 feet because that was the trailer-length limit on a since-irrelevant Pennsylvania highway. That arbitrary number locked Sea-Land out of the 1965-68 ISO 20/40-foot container standard for nearly three decades.
**The surprise:** The size of the box that moves most of the world's goods traces back to one state's old trucking regulation, not any engineering reason.
**The idea:** [Path dependency](../../../concepts/path-dependency.md): an early, arbitrary choice can outlast the reason for it and still constrain everyone downstream.
