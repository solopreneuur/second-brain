# A supermarket that shuts down to grow

**The find:** Chinese supermarket chain Pang Dong Lai hit $3.27B revenue in 2025 from just 14 stores — more per-store than Walmart — while founder Yu Donglai deliberately capped growth: mandatory weekly closures every Tuesday, a 40-hour workweek cap, "unhappy leave" days, and even shutting down profitable locations to protect culture and service quality.
**The surprise:** The founder treats expansion itself as a threat to the business and actively throttles demand rather than chasing it.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md) — growth becomes a problem to be managed down, not up.
