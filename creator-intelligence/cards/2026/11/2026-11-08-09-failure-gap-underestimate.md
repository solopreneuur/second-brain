# Everyone Underestimates How Often Things Fail

**The find:** Across 30+ real-world domains, researchers Eskreis-Winkler, Woolley, Kim and Polimeni found people systematically think failure is rarer than it is: for every 5 weapons the TSA actually fails to detect (a true rate near 70%), people guess only 1 slips through.
**The surprise:** The gap isn't about rare, exotic risks — it shows up in ordinary things like miscarriage rates, product returns, and patent rejections, because success simply gets reported and discussed far more often than failure does.
**The idea:** [Failure blindness](../../../concepts/failure-blindness.md): media's heavy tilt toward reporting success quietly recalibrates everyone's sense of how often things actually go wrong.
