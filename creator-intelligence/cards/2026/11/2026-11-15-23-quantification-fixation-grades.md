# A number beats a better option

**The find:** Across 21 experiments with over 23,000 people, the same job candidate was picked by 84% of evaluators when a grade was shown as a number (93-97%) but by only 69% when the identical performance was shown as a letter grade ("A").
**The surprise:** Reformatting the exact same information as a number instead of a category swung real hiring decisions by 15 points, with zero new information added.
**The idea:** [Quantification fixation](../../../concepts/quantification-fixation.md): numeric formats get weighted more heavily than equivalent categorical ones, even when they carry identical information.
