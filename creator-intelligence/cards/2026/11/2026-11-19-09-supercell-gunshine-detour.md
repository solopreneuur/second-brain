# Supercell's first game was a total flop

**The find:** Before Clash of Clans, Supercell spent eight months building Gunshine, an ambitious browser and Facebook multiplayer shooter that launched in 2010, peaked at about 500,000 monthly players, and was killed in November 2011 once the team concluded it was too complex and couldn't compete with Zynga's economics.
**The surprise:** The studio that would go on to define minimalist, instantly-graspable mobile games started by building the opposite: a complex, hardcore multiplayer shooter that had to fail completely before the team found its actual formula.
**The idea:** A founding team's first instinct is often to build the sophisticated version of what they already know; the breakthrough can come only after abandoning sophistication for radical simplicity.
