# Germany's Bottle Deposit Backfired Into Success

**The find:** Germany's mandatory €0.25 bottle deposit was raised in 2003 specifically to push shoppers toward reusable bottles — disposables kept gaining share anyway — but the country now returns 98% of eligible containers through 135,000 return points, because people stopped treating the deposit as an environmental fine and started treating it as free money waiting to be collected.
**The surprise:** A policy can fail completely at its stated behavioral goal while succeeding completely at a different one, because reward framing drives behavior more reliably than punishment framing.
**The idea:** [Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md): the same €0.25 means something different depending on whether it's labeled a fine you're avoiding or money you're owed.
