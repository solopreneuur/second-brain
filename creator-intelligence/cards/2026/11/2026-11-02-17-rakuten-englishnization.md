# Rakuten's two-year English ultimatum

**The find:** In 2010, Rakuten CEO Hiroshi Mikitani told 10,000 mostly Japanese employees that within two years every meeting and document, even the cafeteria menu, would be in English only, with minimum test scores required for managers.
**The surprise:** A Japanese company forced its own Japanese-speaking workforce to abandon Japanese internally, with real career consequences for failure, and roughly 90% of employees met the bar by the deadline.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md): a hard, dated, measurable rule forces organisational change that aspirational language never does.
