# Twitter deleted 15 million visitors

**The find:** After Twitter acquired blogging platform Posterous — which had around 15 million monthly visitors — it shut the product down entirely in 2013, folding the team straight into Twitter and discarding years of hosted content along with the user base.
**The surprise:** A platform with real, substantial traffic was killed outright by its own acquirer, not despite its popularity but regardless of it — the users were never the asset being bought.
**The idea:** When a buyer's business model only values your team, your user base isn't protected by how large it is — get a continuity commitment in writing if that's the kind of deal you're in ([Talent acquihire](../../../concepts/talent-acquihire.md)).
