# Analysts called Son's $32B chip bet incoherent

**The find:** In 2016, SoftBank paid $32 billion — a 43% premium — for chip designer ARM Holdings, piling another $10 billion of debt onto an already $112 billion-indebted company. Analysts called it strategically incoherent for a telecom-and-internet investor.
**The surprise:** Masayoshi Son himself called it one of his "crazy ideas" and quoted Yoda to skeptical investors to defend it. ARM went on to sit at the center of the AI and mobile chip boom, and Son has since said if forced to choose between his ARM bet and his hugely profitable Nvidia stake, he'd pick ARM "without hesitation."
**The idea:** A confident bet that looks financially reckless to outside analysts can be a correct read of where an entire industry is about to move ([Contrarian conviction](../../../concepts/contrarian-conviction.md)).
