# Chatting with AI inflates false understanding

**The find:** Researchers had students answer "how/why" questions (how glass is made, why leap years exist) after either chatting with ChatGPT, passively reading its output, or doing nothing; the chat group rated themselves most confident afterward but gave the lowest-quality explanations when asked to actually explain the mechanism.
**The surprise:** Talking to AI doesn't just fail to teach you, it actively inflates your illusion that you already knew it.
**The idea:** The [illusion of explanatory depth](../../../concepts/illusion-of-explanatory-depth.md), now showing up in a conversation with a chatbot instead of a zipper or a toilet.
