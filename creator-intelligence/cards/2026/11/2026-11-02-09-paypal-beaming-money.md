# PayPal's original pitch was beaming money

**The find:** PayPal's own 1999 press release, then named Confinity, announced itself as a way to "beam money" via infrared between Palm Pilots and pagers. CEO Peter Thiel called it "the next killer application for web users." Email payments were a throwaway backup for people without a Palm Pilot.
**The surprise:** The feature that actually made PayPal huge was built as a secondary fallback bolted onto a product that never caught on.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): teams often can't tell which part of the product is the real one until users decide for them.
