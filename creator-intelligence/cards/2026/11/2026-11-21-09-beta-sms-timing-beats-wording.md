# Timing Beat Clever Wording

**The find:** Australia's Behavioural Economics Team tested welfare income-reporting reminder texts: a plain SMS sent the afternoon before the deadline lifted on-time reporting from 53.1% to 66.6%.
**The surprise:** Wording variants (gain-framed, personalized copy) added a little more, but almost all the gain came from when the text arrived, not what it said.
**The idea:** A [salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md) — timing is the nudge; content is decoration.
