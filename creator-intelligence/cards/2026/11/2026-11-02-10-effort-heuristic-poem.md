# An 18-hour poem is rated better

**The find:** 144 students rated the identical poem "Order." Half were told it took 4 hours to write, half 18 hours. The "18-hour" group rated it higher quality and worth $95 to a magazine, versus $50 for the same poem labelled 4 hours.
**The surprise:** You can change how good something is judged to be, with zero change to the thing itself, just by changing a number in its backstory.
**The idea:** [Effort heuristic](../../../concepts/effort-heuristic.md): using perceived effort as a stand-in for quality, strongest when true quality is hard to judge directly.
