# A behavior analyst says 90% never leave childhood roles

**The find:** Chase Hughes, a behavior analyst who trains US military and intelligence personnel in profiling, says: "90% of us are walking around with this exact triangle governing our life" — referring to a childhood-formed behavioral pattern he says still runs most adults.
**The surprise:** He frames ordinary adult office behavior — someone reacting the same way in every meeting — as a direct replay of a role fixed in childhood, treating it as close to universal rather than an occasional quirk.
