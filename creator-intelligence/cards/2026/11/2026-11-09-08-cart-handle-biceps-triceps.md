# The cart handle that flexes "want"

**The find:** Researchers found standard shopping-cart handles make shoppers push with their triceps — the same muscle used to shove away things you dislike. A cart redesigned with vertical handles that engage the biceps instead, the "pull toward you" muscle, raised sales of both vice and virtue products in real stores.
**The surprise:** Retailers could nudge buying just by changing which arm muscle a handle activates, with no change to price, layout, or product.
**The idea:** [Embodied cognition](../../../concepts/embodied-cognition.md): the body's posture and muscle activity feed back into judgment and desire before the mind has an opinion.
