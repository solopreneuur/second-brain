# The stork with a spear through its neck

**The find:** In May 1822, a German count shot a white stork on his estate and found it already had a foreign spear lodged clean through its neck. About 25 more "Pfeilstorch" (arrow storks) later turned up the same way, finally proving storks wintered in sub-Saharan Africa rather than hibernating in mud or turning into mice, as was seriously believed.
**The surprise:** For centuries the leading theories for "where do storks go in winter" were hibernation underwater or transformation into other animals — until a wounded bird, carrying physical evidence of its own journey, settled it.
**The idea:** Sometimes a question resists theory for centuries until one piece of brute physical evidence makes the answer undeniable ([decisive evidence](../../../concepts/decisive-evidence.md)).
