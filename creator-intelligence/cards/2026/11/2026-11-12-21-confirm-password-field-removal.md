# Removing the "confirm password" box raised signups

**The find:** A registration form removed its "confirm password" field, replacing the masked re-entry box with a simple show/unmask toggle on the original field instead. Form starts rose 14.3%, completion rose 35.5%, in-form correction events fell 23.9%, and overall conversion rose 56.3%, while password-reset requests stayed flat, disproving the fear that dropping the field would cause more lockouts.
**The surprise:** The field existed specifically to prevent typos in a masked password box, and removing it reduced errors instead of causing the flood of lockouts everyone assumed it was preventing.
**The idea:** A safeguard added to solve one problem can create a bigger one of its own (friction, abandonment) that nobody re-measures once the original fix becomes standard practice ([Untested redesign tax](../../../concepts/untested-redesign-tax.md)).
