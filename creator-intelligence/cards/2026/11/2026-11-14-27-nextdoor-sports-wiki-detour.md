# Nextdoor's founders first built a sports wiki

**The find:** Before Nextdoor, Nirav Tolia, with Sarah Leary and Prakash Janakiraman, raised $5M from Benchmark Capital to build Fanbase, a crowdsourced Wikipedia-style almanac of pro and college athletes meant to rival ESPN's stats pages. It grew to millions of users, then stalled, and the same founders shut it down and pivoted straight to Nextdoor in 2010.
**The surprise:** There's no thematic thread between a sports-stats wiki and a "lost cat, found cat" neighborhood app — the detour and the hit share nothing but the founding team and leftover venture cash.
**The idea:** A founding team's edge is sometimes just execution and capital discipline, not a consistent "vision" — the idea itself can be thrown away completely and still lead somewhere big.
