# Belgium tested kindness vs. threat on 6.5M people

**The find:** Belgium's tax authority tested rewritten reminder letters on about 6.5 million taxpayers between 2014 and 2016. Shorter letters that explicitly mentioned fines and enforcement raised late-payment compliance by 26% and filing by 17% versus the standard reminder.
**The surprise:** A warmer, civic-pride version of the letter, appealing to taxpayers' sense of social duty, did nothing at all — and sometimes made compliance slightly worse — while the colder, more concrete "here's what happens if you don't" letter is what moved a population of millions.
**The idea:** Clarity plus concrete stakes beats moral appeals at scale; people often default to inaction when a letter is vague, not when they lack civic spirit.
