# Cereal mascots are engineered to stare down

**The find:** A Cornell study measured the eye-gaze angle of 86 cereal-box spokescharacters across 65 brands. Adult-cereal mascots look straight ahead; children's-cereal characters, like the Trix rabbit, are drawn gazing down at an average of -9.67 degrees — precisely the angle to meet a child's eyes from a lower shelf.
**The surprise:** The illustrated "stare" of a cartoon rabbit was engineered down to specific degrees, like a lighting cue in a film, purely to intercept a child's line of sight from a cart seat.
**The idea:** Eye contact works as persuasion even when the "looker" is a two-dimensional drawing on a box.
