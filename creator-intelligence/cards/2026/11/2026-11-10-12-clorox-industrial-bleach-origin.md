# Clorox wasn't built for laundry

**The find:** Clorox began in 1913 as the Electro-Alkaline Company, founded by five Oakland businessmen — a coal dealer, a bookkeeper, a lawyer, a banker and a miner — who ran electricity through San Francisco Bay brine to make industrial bleach for paper and textile mills.
**The surprise:** The bottle under nearly every American sink was invented by a team that was 80% non-chemists selling to paper mills, not households.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md) — a mass-market winner can start as a narrow industrial input, invented by outsiders to the category.
