# Oprah's Demotion Became Her Format

**The find:** At 22, Oprah Winfrey was pulled from the WJZ-TV Baltimore evening news anchor desk after about 8 months — management called her "too emotional" for hard news — and reassigned to a low-status daytime talk show, "People Are Talking," in 1978.
**The surprise:** The format she was demoted into as a consolation prize is exactly the live, personality-driven talk format her entire later empire was built on.
**The idea:** [Misfit as signal](../../../concepts/misfit-as-signal.md) — being pushed out of the wrong box can be the first evidence of the right one.
