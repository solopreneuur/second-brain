# Top of queue beat a reminder

**The find:** Two nationwide RCTs texted nearly 4 million English adults about COVID vaccine eligibility. Simply wording the text as "Top of queue" — implying priority — nudged vaccination rates up by a small but statistically robust 2% relative lift across over a million people in each of two age-group trials.
**The surprise:** A four-word framing tweak, tested at a scale of millions, is what actually decided a national text-message policy — not a new incentive, discount or deadline.
**The idea:** [Salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md) — feeling chosen or first in line is a lighter lift than financial incentives, but still moves real-world health behavior at population scale.
