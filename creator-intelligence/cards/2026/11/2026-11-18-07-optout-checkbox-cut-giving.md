# The opt-out checkbox that cut giving

**The find:** Economists ran a door-to-door charity fundraiser field experiment where some households got a flyer the day before announcing the exact time a solicitor would come by, with an easy "not interested, don't knock" checkbox to opt out in advance.
**The surprise:** Simply offering an easy, guilt-free way to say no in advance cut small-dollar giving by about 30% compared to households with no opt-out.
**The idea:** A lot of small charitable giving isn't driven by generosity — it's driven by the social discomfort of refusing someone face to face, and removing that discomfort removes the giving too ([Social pressure giving](../../../concepts/social-pressure-giving.md)).
