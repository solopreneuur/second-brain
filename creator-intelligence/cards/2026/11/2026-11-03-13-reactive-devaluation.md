# The same offer, rated opposite ways

**The find:** Stanford researchers showed people an identical nuclear arms-reduction proposal. 90% rated it "favorable to the US" when told Reagan proposed it, versus just 44% when told Gorbachev did — same terms, different name attached.
**The surprise:** We don't evaluate offers on their merits; we discount them automatically the instant we learn who made them.
**The idea:** [Reactive devaluation](../../../concepts/reactive-devaluation.md) — a hidden tax on every compromise offered by someone you distrust, which is why negotiations stall even when a real concession is on the table.
