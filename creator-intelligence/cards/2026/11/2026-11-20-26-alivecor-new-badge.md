# One "New" badge lifted revenue 30%

**The find:** Heart-monitor maker AliveCor added a single "New" product badge to its KardiaMobile Card listing and detail pages, lifting conversion rate 25.17% and revenue per user 29.58% across desktop and mobile, with 99.4% statistical confidence.
**The surprise:** A badge that cost nothing to build outperformed most full redesigns, just by making an existing product visible as new.
**The idea:** A [salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md) — people buy what they notice, not merely what exists on the page.
