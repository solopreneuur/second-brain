# Deleting share buttons raised sales

**The find:** Finnish retailer Taloon.com A/B tested removing Facebook/Pinterest share buttons from product pages, and removing them raised "Add to Cart" clicks by 11.92%.
**The surprise:** Share buttons are supposed to build trust through social proof, but buttons showing near-zero share counts broadcast "nobody wants this" — deleting the trust signal outperformed keeping it.
**The idea:** Negative social proof — a weak number is worse than no number at all.
