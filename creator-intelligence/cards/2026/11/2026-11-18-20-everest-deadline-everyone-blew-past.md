# The Everest deadline everyone blew past

**The find:** Expedition leader Rob Hall set a hard 1–2pm turnaround time for the 1996 Everest summit attempt, but guides and clients blew past it by two hours chasing the summit. Business scholars now use the case to study escalation of commitment and sunk-cost traps inside organizations.
**The surprise:** The deadline meant to save climbers' lives was set by the same leader who broke it — and died doing so.
**The idea:** Pre-committing to a quit point before emotion or sunk cost can veto it only works if the commitment is treated as non-negotiable in the moment, not just on paper ([Escalation of commitment](../../../concepts/escalation-of-commitment.md)).
