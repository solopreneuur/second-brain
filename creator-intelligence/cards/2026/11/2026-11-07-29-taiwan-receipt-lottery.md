# Every receipt in Taiwan is a lottery ticket

**The find:** Since 1951, almost every retail receipt in Taiwan has doubled as a numbered ticket in a bimonthly national lottery run by the Ministry of Finance, with a top prize around NT$10 million. It was created to fight cash-business tax evasion by turning every customer into an unpaid auditor who now demands a receipt the merchant would rather skip. Reported business tax revenue jumped 75% in the first year alone.
**The surprise:** The government solved an enforcement problem not by auditing more businesses, but by making customers want the paper trail for themselves.
**The idea:** [Civic honesty](../../../concepts/civic-honesty.md): the cheapest way to get a transaction monitored at scale is to give the person on the other side of it a personal stake in the record existing.
