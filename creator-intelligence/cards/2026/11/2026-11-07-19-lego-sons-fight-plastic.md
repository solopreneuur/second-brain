# LEGO's own sons fought the plastic brick

**The find:** After founder Ole Kirk Kristiansen bought an expensive plastic injection-molding machine in the late 1940s, his own sons, all working in the company, saw plastic as ruinous and pushed him to go back to wood. Godtfred told a newspaper "you can make nice, cute things in plastic — but wood is a stronger material." Kristiansen refused, saying "if we get this right, we can sell these bricks all over the world."
**The surprise:** The people trying to kill LEGO's most valuable asset today weren't outside skeptics — they were the founder's own sons and co-workers.
**The idea:** [Formula lock-in](../../../concepts/formula-lock-in.md): the loudest caution against the bet that defines a company can come from the people closest to the cost of changing, not from anyone outside it.
