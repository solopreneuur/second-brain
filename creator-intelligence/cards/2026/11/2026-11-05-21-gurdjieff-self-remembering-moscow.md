# He walked across Moscow asleep

**The find:** Gurdjieff's student Ouspensky practiced "self-remembering" — staring at an ordinary object and telling himself "I am remembering this moment" — until one day he came to and realized he'd walked clear across the city with zero memory of the trip.
**The surprise:** The breakthrough moment in a practice meant to increase presence was a demonstration of its opposite — total absence, revealed only by contrast.
**The idea:** [Self-remembering](../../../concepts/self-remembering.md) — most waking life runs on autopilot, and its absence is invisible until something forces a contrast point.
