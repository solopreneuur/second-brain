# She lifted the stones no woman had lifted

**The find:** In 1979, Jan Todd traveled to Scotland and became the first woman ever to lift the Dinnie Stones, two linked granite boulders totaling over 700 pounds that had sat as an unofficial strength test since the 1860s. No woman matched her for the next 39 years.
**The surprise:** She didn't stop at the record — she went on to become the first woman to total over 1,000 pounds across squat, bench and deadlift, then turned researcher to help write the rulebook for a sport that, before her, had none.
