# Schwab's contrarian cut on May Day

**The find:** When the SEC deregulated trading commissions on May 1, 1975, Wall Street's major firms were expected to raise fees — and did. Charles Schwab, after being told "wait until Merrill Lynch decides to go into your business, you're going to be crushed," cut his commissions 50-60% instead. Merrill Lynch raised its fees that same day, handing Schwab the opening.
**The surprise:** Schwab's whole bet was that deregulation would scare the industry into raising prices, not lowering them — and he was right on day one.
