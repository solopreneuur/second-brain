# Writing it yourself beats reading it

**The find:** A classic 1978 study found that words people generated themselves — by filling in a blank from a simple rule — were remembered far better than identical words they simply read.
**The surprise:** The memory boost has nothing to do with seeing the word; it comes purely from the effort of producing it yourself, even for words you didn't invent and that were right there on the page either way.
**The idea:** [The generation effect](../../../concepts/generation-effect.md) — producing information yourself embeds it in memory deeper than reading the exact same information does.
