# Odd numbers make tougher negotiators

**The find:** In negotiation experiments, an opening offer like $19,873 pulled counteroffers less than a round one like $19,900 for the same item, because recipients read the precision as a sign the offerer had done real homework and adjusted less.
**The surprise:** The exact same price is a stronger anchor just for having more digits in it, though the digit count carries no real information about value.
**The idea:** Precision heuristic: specific numbers read as evidence of informed reasoning and resist adjustment more than round numbers of identical size, a close cousin of [anchoring](../../../concepts/anchoring.md).
