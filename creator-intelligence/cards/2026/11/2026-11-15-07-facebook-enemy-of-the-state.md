# Zuckerberg's first job title

**The find:** TheFacebook.com's 2004 About page listed Mark Zuckerberg's own title as "Founder, Master and Commander, Enemy of the State," with cofounders given joke titles and the footer reading "a Mark Zuckerberg production."
**The surprise:** The company now defined by tightly managed PR launched by having its CEO publicly call himself an enemy of the state, as a joke.
**The idea:** none
