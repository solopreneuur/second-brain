# That hole in your pan handle has a second job

**The find:** The hole drilled into a frying pan or saucepan handle, usually assumed to be for hanging storage, is also patented as a built-in spoon rest — a 2000 patent describes resting a stirring spoon in it so drips fall back into the pan instead of onto the counter.
**The surprise:** The same hole most people have only ever used (or ignored) for one purpose was engineered to do two jobs at once, and nobody tells you the second one.
**The idea:** A single, unremarkable design detail can be quietly doing more than one job, with no label explaining the second one ([Hidden affordance](../../../concepts/hidden-affordance.md)).
