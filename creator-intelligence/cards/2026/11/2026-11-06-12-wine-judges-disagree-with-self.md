# Wine judges can't agree with themselves

**The find:** Robert Hodgson served the same wine three times, hidden in blind flights of 30, to the same 70 expert judges at a major U.S. wine competition. Only about 10% gave it the same medal all three times; another 10% ranged it from bronze to gold.
**The surprise:** Trained, credentialed palates can't reliably tell a wine from itself.
**The idea:** Rater unreliability: expertise doesn't guarantee a stable judgment, even from the same judge on the same subject.
