# Nucor ran $4B on 22 people

**The find:** When Nucor approached $4 billion in revenue in the late 1990s, its entire headquarters staff was 22 people in rented offices in Charlotte, with only four layers of management between the CEO and a frontline steelworker.
**The surprise:** A Fortune 500 company running a multibillion-dollar business chose a head office smaller than a single department at most rivals, as deliberate philosophy rather than cost-cutting.
**The idea:** Headquarters size is a visible choice about where a company believes real decisions should be made, a form of [decentralised control](../../../concepts/decentralised-control.md).
