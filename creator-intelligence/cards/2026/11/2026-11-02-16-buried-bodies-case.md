# The lawyer who had to keep the secret

**The find:** In 1973, attorneys Frank Armani and Francis Belge learned from their client exactly where he'd hidden two murder victims' bodies. Bound by attorney-client privilege, they kept it secret for roughly five months until the bodies were found independently.
**The surprise:** The law required silence that looked, to the public, exactly like complicity. Doing the legal and ethical thing cost Armani death threats, his law practice, and a heart attack.
**The idea:** [Professional duty conflict](../../../concepts/professional-duty-conflict.md): a confidentiality obligation can force a person into a choice that looks morally wrong from the outside while being procedurally correct.
