# One sign closed the handwashing gap

**The find:** Observers watching public restrooms found men used soap only 38% of the time, well below women's rate. After a sign about the health consequences of not washing went up, men's usage jumped to 77%, nearly matching women's.
**The surprise:** The gap wasn't about habit or hygiene knowledge. It closed almost entirely the moment one sign stated the consequence out loud.
**The idea:** [Salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md): people already know the right behavior; what's missing is often just a reminder of why it matters, right at the moment of the decision.
