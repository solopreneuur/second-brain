# 98% of France recognizes one train-station jingle

**The find:** French rail operator SNCF has played the same four-note jingle before every station announcement since 2005. Composed by Michaël Boumendil of Sixième Son, it's now recognized by 98% of French people — most can identify it as SNCF from just the first two notes.
**The surprise:** The brand built near-universal recognition with four notes and zero visuals, a level of recall most logos never reach, entirely through a sound nobody consciously tries to remember.
**The idea:** A sound repeated in the same context thousands of times a day can become a more reliable brand marker than a logo, without anyone deciding to memorize it ([Distinctive assets](../../../concepts/distinctive-assets.md)).
