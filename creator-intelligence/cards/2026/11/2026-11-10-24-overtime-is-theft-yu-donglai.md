# A retail tycoon calls overtime theft

**The find:** In a 2023 speech, Pang Dong Lai founder Yu Donglai said making staff work overtime is "unethical" because it is "an expropriation of other people's opportunities for growth" — not a wellness complaint, but a claim about who the overtime actually steals from.
**The surprise:** He frames unpaid overtime not as exploitation of the person working it, but as theft from everyone who didn't get hired to do it instead.
**The idea:** Zero-sum reframing — recasting a common practice as a transfer from an unseen third party, not a cost to the person doing it, changes who looks like the victim and makes the practice harder to defend.
