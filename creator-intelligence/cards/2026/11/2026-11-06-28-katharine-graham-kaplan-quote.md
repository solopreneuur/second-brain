# The $3B deal approved with a shrug

**The find:** Washington Post Co.'s 2006 annual report quotes Katharine Graham's unfiltered reaction, from her own memoir, to buying test-prep company Stanley Kaplan: "I don't give a shit about it, but if you think it will be profitable, let's do it." Kaplan later became a bigger profit engine for the company than the newspaper itself.
**The surprise:** The acquisition that ended up propping up the entire company was approved with a shrug, not a vision statement.
