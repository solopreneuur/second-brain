# Shell Oil is named for seashells

**The find:** In 1833, London dealer Marcus Samuel began importing ornamental seashells from the Far East for beach-souvenir trinket boxes. His sons later turned the trading business into shipping oil through the Suez Canal, naming the 1897 company "Shell" and its tankers after mollusks in tribute to the original trade.
**The surprise:** One of the world's largest oil companies is named for seaside tourist-trap trinkets, not petroleum.
**The idea:** A [manufactured tradition](../../../concepts/manufactured-tradition.md): the name reads as deliberate branding now, but it started as a literal, sentimental leftover from the founder's first business.
