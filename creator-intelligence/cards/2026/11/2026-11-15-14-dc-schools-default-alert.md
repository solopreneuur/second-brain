# One default saved failing grades

**The find:** Washington DC schools got under 11% of parents to opt in to failing-grade text alerts when parents had to actively sign up; automatically enrolling them, with an opt-out text, pushed adoption to 95% and measurably cut course failures.
**The surprise:** The district's own administrators didn't believe switching who has to take action would move adoption by 90 points — but it did.
**The idea:** The [default effect](../../../concepts/default-effect.md): whatever option requires no action becomes the option almost everyone ends up with.
