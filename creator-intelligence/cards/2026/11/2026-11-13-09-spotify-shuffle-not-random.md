# Spotify's shuffle is deliberately not random

**The find:** In 2014, Spotify's engineers replaced their mathematically correct shuffle algorithm with a less-random one that deliberately spreads songs by the same artist evenly across a playlist.
**The surprise:** The fix wasn't a bug patch — it was Spotify admitting their *correct* randomness was the problem. True random shuffle often plays two songs from the same artist back to back, and users read that as rigged, so Spotify made the algorithm less random to make it feel more random.
**The idea:** Statistical randomness and the subjective feeling of randomness are different targets, and systems built for humans have to design for the feeling ([Subjective randomness](../../../concepts/subjective-randomness.md)).
