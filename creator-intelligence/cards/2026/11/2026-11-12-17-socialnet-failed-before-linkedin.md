# Reid Hoffman built LinkedIn once already, and it flopped

**The find:** In 1997, five years before LinkedIn, Reid Hoffman founded SocialNet, a site combining online dating, roommate-matching and professional networking in one product. It struggled for roughly two and a half years, one ad partnership produced just six signups in a month, and it shut down.
**The surprise:** Hoffman didn't stumble into LinkedIn's idea later; he tried to build essentially the same idea once already and failed outright, years before the market or the culture was ready for it.
**The idea:** Being right too early is its own form of failure, and the lesson from a dead company can be exactly what makes the second attempt work.
