# "Request pricing" beat "Request a quote"

**The find:** Veeam Software's product pages kept getting visitor feedback asking for pricing it wasn't willing to publish, since it sells through partners with variable discounts. Instead of adding real numbers, it reworded its call-to-action link from "Request a quote" to "Request pricing" and A/B tested it.
**The surprise:** No price was ever shown and nothing about the offer changed — renaming the button to match customers' own language lifted click-through from 0.54% to 1.40%, a 162% relative jump.
**The idea:** [Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md): matching the words customers already use for what they want, without changing what you're willing to give them.
