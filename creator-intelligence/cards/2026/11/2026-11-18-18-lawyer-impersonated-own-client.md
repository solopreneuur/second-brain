# A lawyer impersonated his own client

**The find:** Beyond the famous "fight club blindness" police case, researchers Christopher Chabris and Daniel Simons cite a real courtroom incident: lawyer Roger Christianson approached the bench pretending to be his own client, and the ticketing officer testifying against that client failed to notice he had the wrong man.
**The surprise:** A lawyer deliberately exploited a known blind spot in human perception to discredit a witness in open court — and the stunt worked well enough that he was later disciplined for it.
**The idea:** Professionals can weaponize the same cognitive blind spots that normally just cause innocent mistakes, turning a perception failure into a courtroom tactic ([Inattentional blindness](../../../concepts/inattentional-blindness.md)).
