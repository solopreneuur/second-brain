# One sentence in a text cut missed visits

**The find:** Adding a single sentence — "Not attending costs the NHS approximately £160" — to routine hospital appointment reminder texts cut missed appointments from 11.1% to 8.4%, a 23% relative drop, confirmed across two randomised controlled trials.
**The surprise:** One free, factual sentence outperformed the entire existing reminder system it was bolted onto.
**The idea:** [Concrete cost framing](../../../concepts/concrete-cost-framing.md) — an abstract inconvenience ("please show up") becomes motivating the moment it's priced in real money.
