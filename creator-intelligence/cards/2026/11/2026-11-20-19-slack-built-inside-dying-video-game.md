# Slack was built inside a dying video game

**The find:** Stewart Butterfield's Tiny Speck spent years building Glitch, a whimsical browser MMO that launched in September 2011 and was permanently shut down by December 2012. The internal chat tool the small remote team built just to coordinate work on Glitch was spun out and relaunched as Slack in August 2013.
**The surprise:** One of the most valuable workplace software companies in history — bought by Salesforce for $27.7 billion — began life as scaffolding around a video game that failed to find an audience.
**The idea:** A [forced detour](../../../concepts/forced-detour.md): sometimes the infrastructure built to make a failed product works better as the standalone business than the product it was built to support.
