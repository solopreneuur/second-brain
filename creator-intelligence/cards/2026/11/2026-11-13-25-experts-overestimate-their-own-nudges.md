# Behavioral scientists overrate their own nudges 3x

**The find:** A large megastudy compared behavioral scientists' own forecasts of their nudge interventions against the real results. Experts predicted an average 4.98 percentage-point lift; the actual average effect was 1.61 percentage points — about a third of what they expected.
**The surprise:** These are the people who design nudges for a living, forecasting their own field's interventions, and they were still off by roughly 3x in the optimistic direction.
**The idea:** Expertise in building an intervention doesn't protect you from overestimating how much it will actually move the needle ([Expert forecast inflation](../../../concepts/expert-forecast-inflation.md)).
