# Google won't build the scenic route

**The find:** Former Google Maps UX researcher Kasey Klimes says the team built and tested a "scenic route" option for walking navigation, found it technically easy, and killed it anyway.
**The surprise:** It died not because it failed, but because routing foot traffic toward prettier, wealthier streets would quietly divert footfall and tax revenue away from the poorer neighborhoods the default route already passed through.
**The idea:** Quiet redistribution — a product decision can look like neglect from outside while actually being a deliberate refusal to let a convenience feature redistribute real-world money.
