# Discord's dead video game

**The find:** Discord's founders first sold a gaming SDK for $104 million, then built a multiplayer battle-arena game called Fates Forever that flopped in 2014 — the voice chat its small player base used during development became Discord.
**The surprise:** Discord wasn't a messaging idea at all, it was a side effect of a failed video game, and even the $104 million exit before that wasn't the end of the road.
