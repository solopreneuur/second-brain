# Chouinard killed his best-selling product

**The find:** By the late 1950s, pitons were the best-selling product at Yvon Chouinard's climbing-gear company, roughly 70% of revenue — but he'd watched them scar climbing routes that had stood untouched for millennia. In 1972 he pulled them from his own catalog with no working replacement yet invented.
**The surprise:** He discontinued the product paying most of his bills before he had anything ready to sell instead, forcing the pivot to non-damaging aluminum chocks that eventually funded the Patagonia label.
**The idea:** A [self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md): cutting off the easy, profitable option before the better one exists, so there's no way back.
