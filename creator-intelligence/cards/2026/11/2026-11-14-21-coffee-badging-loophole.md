# Workers found the badge-reader loophole

**The find:** Owl Labs' 2023 survey of 2,000 US hybrid workers found 58% admitted to "coffee badging" — swiping into the office just long enough to be seen, grabbing a coffee, then going home to work the rest of the day.
**The surprise:** The badge reader was built as a security checkpoint, but companies repurposed its beep as an attendance metric — and workers instantly found the loophole, because the sensor only proves you walked through a door, not that you stayed.
**The idea:** Any proxy metric gets gamed the moment people realize what it's actually being used to measure ([Goodhart's Law](../../../concepts/goodharts-law.md)).
