# Rivals called Revolut's bet "niche"

**The find:** On stage at TechCrunch Disrupt Berlin in December 2017, Monzo's Tom Blomfield called Revolut's multi-currency and crypto features "a 1% niche problem," and N26's Valentin Stalf said only 5 to 10% of customers used foreign-exchange features. Revolut doubled down on being the everything-app anyway.
**The surprise:** The feature both rivals dismissed as a rounding error became the engine of Revolut's growth to over 80 million customers and a $115 billion valuation by late 2026, while the companies that mocked it stayed far smaller.
**The idea:** Competitors judge your edge case against their own average customer — what looks niche to a generalist can be the wedge that compounds into the whole business.
