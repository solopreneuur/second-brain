# Netflix killed the opening credits

**The find:** Netflix noticed that about 15% of the time, viewers were manually fast-forwarding through a show's opening credits in the first five minutes; its product team shipped "Skip Intro" in 2017, and it's now pressed roughly 136 million times a day.
**The surprise:** One quietly measured behavior — viewers' own thumbs skipping ahead — killed the TV title sequence as an art form faster than any streaming strategy memo could.
**The idea:** none
