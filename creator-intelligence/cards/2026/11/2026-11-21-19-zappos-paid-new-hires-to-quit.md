# Zappos Paid New Hires to Quit

**The find:** Tony Hsieh's Zappos offered new hires, after initial training, a one-time $2,000 payment to quit on the spot, no questions asked.
**The surprise:** A company paid cash specifically to lose employees, on purpose — and by Hsieh's own account, under 1-3% ever took it.
**The idea:** A [self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md) used as a culture filter: anyone who'd take free money to leave wasn't committed enough to keep.
