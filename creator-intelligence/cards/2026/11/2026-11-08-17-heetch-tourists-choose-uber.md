# Heetch Told Tourists to Choose Uber

**The find:** During the 2024 Paris Olympics, French ride-hailing challenger Heetch ran ads in 12 languages at airports and tourist hotspots reading "Dear tourists, choose Uber!" — complete with a QR code linking to Uber's app — to keep its own drivers free for loyal local Parisians.
**The surprise:** A company paid real media money to send its single biggest demand spike of the year straight into a rival's hands.
**The idea:** [Identity signalling](../../../concepts/identity-signalling.md): publicly sacrificing a windfall customer segment to show loyalty to your core tribe builds more brand equity than capturing the windfall would.
