# Game Boy's worse screen, on purpose

**The find:** Nintendo's Gunpei Yokoi deliberately shipped the 1989 Game Boy with a cheap monochrome screen while rivals Atari Lynx and Sega Game Gear launched with color, overruling internal pressure to match them.
**The surprise:** The "worse" screen was the point: color LCDs of that era drained batteries in hours, and both color rivals flopped partly because their screens killed batteries fast — kids cared more about long battery life and a low price.
**The idea:** [Withered technology](../../../concepts/withered-technology.md) — Yokoi's own term for deliberately using cheap, mature tech in new ways instead of chasing the newest spec.
