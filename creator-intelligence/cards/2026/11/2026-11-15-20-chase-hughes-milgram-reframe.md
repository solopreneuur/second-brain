# A profiler rewrites the Milgram study

**The find:** Behavior analyst Chase Hughes, who has trained US special-operations personnel, told Steven Bartlett that psychology's most famous obedience experiment, Milgram's 1962 shock study, has been misread for 60 years — he argues the lab coat and "authority" weren't the real driver, novelty-driven focus was, with authority riding on top of it.
**The surprise:** A working behavior profiler used a mainstream podcast to directly contradict the standard reading of the most famous experiment in the psychology of authority.
