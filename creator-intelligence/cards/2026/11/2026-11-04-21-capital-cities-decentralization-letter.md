# One paragraph, printed every year

**The find:** Every Capital Cities Communications annual report carried the same paragraph on its inside cover: "Decentralization is the cornerstone of our philosophy... All decisions are made at the local level." Headquarters had no VP of marketing, no PR department, and no corporate counsel; CEO Tom Murphy's secretary fielded press calls.
**The surprise:** The company that went on to buy ABC for $3.5 billion ran its empire with a headquarters thinner than almost any department most conglomerates consider mandatory.
**The idea:** A company can make its organizational philosophy a literal, repeated line item instead of a slogan, another face of [decentralised control](../../../concepts/decentralised-control.md).
