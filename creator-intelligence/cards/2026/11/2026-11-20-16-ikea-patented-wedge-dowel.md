# IKEA's real moat is a hidden patent

**The find:** US Patent 9,447,804 B2 covers IKEA's wedge-dowel furniture joint — the snap mechanism that lets flat-pack furniture click together without an Allen key, cutting assembly time by an estimated 50-80%. IKEA doesn't patent how its furniture looks; it patents the joinery hidden inside the particleboard.
**The surprise:** IKEA's actual competitive advantage isn't the Scandinavian look any factory can copy — it's an un-copyable patented snap-joint nobody sees until they're already assembling the product.
**The idea:** A [process-patent moat](../../../concepts/process-patent-moat.md): the invisible manufacturing or assembly method can be worth protecting more than the visible product it produces.
