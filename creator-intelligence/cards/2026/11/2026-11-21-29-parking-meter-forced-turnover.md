# The Parking Meter Wasn't About Money

**The find:** Oklahoma City newspaperman Carl Magee invented the parking meter in 1935, installed July 16, because downtown merchants were losing customers to commuters who grabbed a spot at 8am and sat on it all day.
**The surprise:** The device everyone assumes is a city cash grab was actually a merchant-lobbied fix for a retail-traffic problem — revenue was incidental to the original design.
**The idea:** [Turnover engineering](../../../concepts/turnover-engineering.md) — forcing rotation through a scarce resource, not pricing it, was the actual goal.
