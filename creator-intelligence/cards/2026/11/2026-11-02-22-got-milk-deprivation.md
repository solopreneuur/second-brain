# "Got Milk?" was born from a cruel focus group

**The find:** In 1993 planner Jon Steel asked focus-group participants to abstain from milk for a full week before the session. Their stories of disappointment convinced the agency that deprivation, not health benefits, was the real emotional trigger, despite 96% of consumers already believing milk was healthy.
**The surprise:** The industry's most famous slogan came from deliberately inflicting a small deprivation on real people and watching them break, not from asking what they liked about the product.
**The idea:** [Deprivation marketing](../../../concepts/deprivation-marketing.md): absence creates more emotional salience than presence; sell the gap where a product should be.
