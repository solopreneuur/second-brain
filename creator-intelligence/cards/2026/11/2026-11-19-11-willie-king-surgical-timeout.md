# Every document agreed, and it was still wrong

**The find:** In a 1995 Florida hospital, surgeons amputated Willie King's healthy left leg instead of his diseased right one. The wrong leg was written correctly nowhere — it was wrong on the OR blackboard, the hospital computer and the schedule, and every person who prepped the room still prepped the wrong limb.
**The surprise:** The case led to the mandatory surgical "time-out," a forced full stop right before cutting where the entire team verbally reconfirms the patient, site and procedure out loud together, from scratch, independent of any paperwork.
**The idea:** When a fact has propagated into every system — a deck, a brief, a calendar invite — cross-checking those same systems won't catch the error; only a moment where the team says the critical fact out loud from scratch will.
