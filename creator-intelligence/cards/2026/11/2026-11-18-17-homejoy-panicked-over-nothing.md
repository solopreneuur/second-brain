# A $150M startup panicked over nothing

**The find:** Homejoy, a $150M on-demand home-cleaning marketplace, shut down in 2015 after rushing to expand into 30+ cities, including Europe, to outpace a smaller, far less funded rival — burning cash before its unit economics were solid.
**The surprise:** It wasn't beaten by a bigger competitor. It panicked over a weaker one and overextended trying to out-race a threat that was never actually winning.
**The idea:** FOMO-driven overexpansion can kill product-market fit faster than any real competitor does ([Execution panic](../../../concepts/execution-panic.md)).
