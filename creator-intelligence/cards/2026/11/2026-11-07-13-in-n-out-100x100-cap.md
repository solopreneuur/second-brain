# One order forced In-N-Out's burger cap

**The find:** Until 2004, In-N-Out would build a burger of any size a customer asked for. On Halloween night that year, a group that included future Zappos CEO Tony Hsieh talked a Las Vegas location into building a "100x100" — 100 patties, 100 slices of cheese, nearly 20,000 calories, eaten by eight people over two hours. By the end of 2006, In-N-Out had capped every burger nationwide at 4x4, no exceptions.
**The surprise:** In-N-Out's entire identity was "we'll make it however you want it." The policy that undid that promise was triggered by one group of friends proving the open-ended version could be taken to an absurd, kitchen-breaking extreme.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md): sometimes a company's strictest rule is written the day after its most permissive one gets stress-tested in public.
