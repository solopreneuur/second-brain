# Jack in the Box blew up its mascot

**The find:** In 1980, Jack in the Box aired an ad literally exploding its own beloved clown mascot on TV to signal the brand was changing; it later resurrected "Jack" as a sly executive for a 1994-95 relaunch after a deadly E. coli crisis.
**The surprise:** Detonating the icon that built the brand scored 72 on an ad-recall scale against a category average of 27, and the character went on to win 10 Effie Awards.
**The idea:** Killing your own symbol can be the fastest way to prove you've actually changed, when words alone won't be believed ([destroying your own symbol](../../../concepts/symbolic-demolition.md)).
