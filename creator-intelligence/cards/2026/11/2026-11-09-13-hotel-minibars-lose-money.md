# Hotel minibars often lose money

**The find:** Hospitality trade press reports that hotel minibars often run on thin or negative margins despite their steep sticker prices, because daily restocking, inventory checks, and billing-dispute resolution cost more than the markup recovers — even outsourced operators with sensors only cut guest billing errors from 15% to 2-4%.
**The surprise:** The absurd prices look like pure gouging but are often the hotel's attempt to merely break even against labor costs that still exceed the revenue.
**The idea:** No named concept — the price tag hides a cost structure, not a profit margin.
