# An ad critic says nobody sees ads

**The find:** Ad critic Bob Hoffman ("The Ad Contrarian"), around the 31-minute mark of the Uncensored CMO podcast, says: "Most advertising is invisible, most advertising has no attention paid to it. The skill in the advertising business is to be able to create something that does get attention — if you can't do that, forget about it, save your money."
**The surprise:** A veteran ad-industry insider flatly states that the default output of the advertising business is invisible and worthless — delivered as plain, boring truth most agencies won't say to clients.
**The idea:** Attention is the actual product being sold in advertising, not reach or impressions — a message nobody notices has zero value regardless of spend.
