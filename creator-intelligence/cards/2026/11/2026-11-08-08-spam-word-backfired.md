# A Reassurance Word That Backfired

**The find:** Conversion researcher Michael Aagaard (ContentVerve) tested an opt-in form reading "100% privacy — we will never spam you!" against one with no privacy statement at all; adding the reassurance cut signups 18.7%, purely because the word "spam" itself primed distrust.
**The surprise:** Trying to calm a fear by naming it, even to deny it, backfired harder than saying nothing at all — removing just that one trigger word, in a rewritten line, then lifted signups 19.47% over the no-statement baseline.
**The idea:** [Salience backfire](../../../concepts/salience-backfire.md): naming a feared outcome to deny it still activates the fear.
