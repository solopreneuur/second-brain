# Confident on the hardest questions

**The find:** Lichtenstein and Fischhoff had people judge whether children's drawings were made by European or Asian kids; on this hard task, people scored only 53% right but reported 68% confidence — overconfident on exactly the questions they were worst at.
**The surprise:** The harder the task got, the more sure of themselves people became, inverting what you'd expect.
**The idea:** The hard-easy effect — confidence and competence move in opposite directions at the extremes of difficulty, which is why people under-prepare for hard exams and over-prepare for easy ones ([the hard-easy effect](../../../concepts/hard-easy-effect.md)).
