# The Wiffle Ball's curve was a side effect

**The find:** The 1957 Wiffle Ball patent states its purpose plainly: a ball that "will not travel any relatively long distance" and won't injure anyone or break a window, even if struck hard. The perforated plastic shell was designed for suburban safety, not for its signature crazy curve.
**The surprise:** The feature everyone loves the Wiffle Ball for — the unpredictable swerve — is a side effect of a liability fix, not the stated design goal.
**The idea:** [Unintended side effect](../../../concepts/unintended-side-effect.md) — the feature that makes a product famous is sometimes a byproduct of solving a completely different, duller problem.
