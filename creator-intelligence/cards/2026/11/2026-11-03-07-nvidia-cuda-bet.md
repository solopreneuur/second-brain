# Nvidia's decade-long "mistake"

**The find:** In 2006, Jensen Huang built general-purpose computing (CUDA) into every consumer Nvidia graphics card, not just expensive workstation ones, raising manufacturing costs for a market that barely existed yet.
**The surprise:** Wall Street punished the bet for a decade — Nvidia's stock fell as much as 85% between 2007 and 2008, with board members privately fearing an activist takeover — before CUDA became the backbone of the entire AI boom.
**The idea:** Building the platform before the market exists means absorbing years of looking wrong on paper.
