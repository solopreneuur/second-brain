# Feastables made people click 500 times

**The find:** Feastables, MrBeast's chocolate brand, sent an email offering "the first five people" 50% off — but only if they clicked a button 500 times first. The campaign drove 2 million clicks from one email.
**The surprise:** Making the reward harder to get, not easier, is what made people engage so hard.
**The idea:** [Labor illusion](../../../concepts/labor-illusion.md): visible effort toward a reward makes the reward feel more earned and worth chasing.
