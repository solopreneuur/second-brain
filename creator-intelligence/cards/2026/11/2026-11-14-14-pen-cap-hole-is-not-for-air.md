# The hole in a pen cap isn't for air

**The find:** The small hole in a pen cap isn't there to stop the ink drying out. BIC's early-1990s patent describes it as an air channel so that if a child swallows or inhales the cap, air can still pass around it — a response to international choking-safety standards after child deaths.
**The surprise:** Almost everyone assumes the cap hole is about ink; the patent itself is explicitly a choking-hazard vent.
**The idea:** A detail that looks purely functional can be a liability fix hiding in plain sight ([hidden affordance](../../../concepts/hidden-affordance.md)).
