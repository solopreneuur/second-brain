# One in three secretly hate fidgeting

**The find:** UBC researchers surveyed the general population about their reactions to watching other people fidget — leg-shaking, pen-clicking, gum-chewing — and measured who felt a genuine negative emotional reaction.
**The surprise:** About a third of ordinary people reported real irritation or anger at watching someone else's small repetitive movements, a condition that had almost no public name before this study.
**The idea:** Misokinesia, the visual twin of misophonia — an involuntary aversion to seeing repetitive motion, not just hearing repetitive sound ([Misokinesia](../../../concepts/misokinesia.md)).
