# Google's memo called users ignorable

**The find:** A 2017 internal Google "communications training" document from VP of Finance Michael Roszak, entered as a trial exhibit in US v. Google, states search-ad economics are so favorable the company "can mostly ignore the demand side... and only focus on the supply side," comparing the business to "cigarettes or drugs."
**The surprise:** Google specifically fought to keep this exhibit sealed — it shows a senior finance executive training colleagues to treat users as irrelevant to the business model, while the public story is all about serving user needs.
**The idea:** Internal communications-training documents are a rare unfiltered window into how a company really models the people it says it serves.
