# An airport made the walk to bags longer

**The find:** Houston's airport cut baggage-claim complaints to almost nothing, not by speeding up the bags, but by moving arrival gates farther from the claim area — making passengers walk six times longer before standing still to wait.
**The surprise:** The fix for "the wait is too long" was to make the whole trip take longer, as long as people were moving instead of standing.
**The idea:** [Labor illusion](../../../concepts/labor-illusion.md): occupied time feels shorter than idle time of the same length, so the perceived wait, not its clock duration, is the thing worth engineering.
