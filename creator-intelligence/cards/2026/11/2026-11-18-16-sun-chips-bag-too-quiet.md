# The chip bag that was too quiet

**The find:** Oxford psychologist Charles Spence's Pringles study found people rate a chip as noticeably fresher and crisper when its crunch sound is louder or higher-pitched — a finding snack companies use when choosing stiff, crinkly packaging film. When Frito-Lay's biodegradable Sun Chips bag was measured at 95 decibels, consumer backlash forced the company to redesign it back to a quieter bag.
**The surprise:** A famous eco-friendly packaging redesign failed not because it was bad for the planet, but because it was too quiet.
**The idea:** Perceived quality is multisensory — manipulating one unrelated sense (sound) changes judgments of another (freshness or taste), for better or worse ([Sonic seasoning](../../../concepts/sonic-seasoning.md)).
