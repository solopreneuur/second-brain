# The consultant said Japan would never buy it

**The find:** When Howard Schultz wanted to open Starbucks in Tokyo in the mid-1990s, Starbucks's board forced him to commission an outside market study first. The consultant's report said flatly that no one in Japan would ever walk down the street with a cup of coffee — they would "lose face." Schultz opened there anyway, through a joint venture.
**The surprise:** The exact "culturally impossible" behaviour the report ruled out became a visible street phenomenon in Tokyo, and launched Starbucks's entire global-expansion playbook.
**The idea:** Paid expert research can be confidently wrong about culture; founder conviction plus a strong local partner can beat a consultant's model.
