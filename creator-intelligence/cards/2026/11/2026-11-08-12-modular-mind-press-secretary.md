# The Mind's "Press Secretary" Doesn't Know

**The find:** Psychologist Rob Kurzban argues consciousness works like a "press secretary" module whose job is managing how we look to others, not accessing the truth — illustrated by a terminally ill patient who books a cruise months out despite his doctor's prognosis, because one mental module keeps making long-term plans while another can act on the real odds.
**The surprise:** This isn't denial in the usual sense — the mind is strategically, functionally holding two incompatible positions at once because each one serves a different purpose.
**The idea:** [The modular mind](../../../concepts/modular-mind.md): hypocrisy and inconsistency aren't moral failures, they're what you'd expect from an architecture where the "spokesperson" part evolved to persuade, not to know.
