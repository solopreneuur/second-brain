# A photocopied note doubled spending

**The find:** A South Korean online beauty retailer randomized customers to get no note, a printed note, a handwritten note, or a photocopy of a handwritten note with their order. No-note customers spent $25.97 on average; photocopy-of-handwritten-note customers spent $52.07 — about the same as a real handwritten note.
**The surprise:** A cheap photocopy of handwriting worked exactly as well as the real thing — authenticity didn't matter, only the appearance of a human hand.
**The idea:** [Effort heuristic](../../../concepts/effort-heuristic.md): perceived effort changes how something is valued, regardless of the effort actually spent.
