# The seatbelt holding your gearshift hostage

**The find:** Researchers fitted 101 vehicles with a device blocking the gearshift for up to 8 seconds unless the driver buckled their seatbelt — buckling instantly cancelled the delay.
**The surprise:** No fine, no alarm, no lecture — just a few controllable seconds of not being able to drive — raised seatbelt use from roughly 48% to 67% in US drivers and higher in Canada, more than most awareness campaigns manage.
**The idea:** Negative reinforcement — a removable irritant that disappears the instant you comply can beat a moral appeal.
