# You need half as many "no"s as you think

**The find:** Vanessa Bohns' research found people dramatically underestimate how often strangers will say yes to a request. In one study, people expected to ask about 10 strangers to borrow a phone before one agreed; it actually took about six, with every other person saying yes.
**The surprise:** In a separate study, people predicted only 28% compliance with an uncomfortable, ethically dicey request (vandalizing a library book); actual compliance was 64% — more than double the guess, in the wrong direction people assume protects them.
**The idea:** People consistently overestimate how much resistance they'll meet, which quietly suppresses how often they ask for anything at all ([Underestimating compliance](../../../concepts/underestimating-compliance.md)).
