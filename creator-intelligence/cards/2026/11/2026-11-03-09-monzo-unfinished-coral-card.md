# Monzo's bank card was a placeholder

**The find:** Monzo's first designer picked a neon coral colour for the beta debit card, borrowed from a gift card he'd spotted at the manufacturer, specifically to signal "this is a prototype" — the same trick concept cars use at auto shows.
**The surprise:** The company planned to replace the colour once branding was finished, but strangers kept asking about it in restaurants, and the "unfinished" placeholder became the brand's permanent, trademark identity.
**The idea:** An [accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md) — the throwaway signal meant to mark something as temporary becomes the thing people remember.
