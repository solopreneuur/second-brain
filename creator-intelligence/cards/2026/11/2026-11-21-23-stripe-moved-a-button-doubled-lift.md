# One Button, Moved, Doubled Itself

**The find:** Stripe's holdback experiments found offering Apple Pay at checkout lifted conversion 22.3% on average.
**The surprise:** Moving the same button earlier in the checkout flow, instead of near the bottom, doubled that lift again — identical feature, same merchants, 2x different outcome, purely from where it sits on the page.
**The idea:** A [salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md) — position changes what a feature is worth before anyone reads a word of copy.
