# Why every LEGO brick still fits

**The find:** A 2x4 LEGO brick molded in 1958 still snaps onto one made this morning on a different continent, because LEGO holds its mold tolerances to roughly 0.01mm across billions of parts made every year.
**The surprise:** That precision isn't a nostalgia choice — any looseness compounds brick over brick until a tall model collapses from "tolerance stack-up."
**The idea:** Precision isn't a luxury feature, it's the thing that makes modularity possible at scale: loosen tolerance anywhere and the whole combinatorial promise falls apart ([Tolerance stacking](../../../concepts/tolerance-stacking.md)).
