# Brazil prices everything by the month

**The find:** In Brazil, splitting a purchase into interest-free monthly installments ("parcelamento sem juros") is the default way prices are displayed above about BRL 200, not a special financing offer; about 62% of cardholders use it monthly, and 70% of installment buyers say they would not have bought at all without it.
**The surprise:** The total price never changes, but reframing it around the smallest recurring number shown to the buyer measurably creates demand that a straight price tag does not.
**The idea:** Buyers respond to the number they see most, not the number that's true — another face of [pain of paying](../../../concepts/pain-of-paying.md), where price-per-month framing moves willingness-to-buy independent of any real financing cost.
