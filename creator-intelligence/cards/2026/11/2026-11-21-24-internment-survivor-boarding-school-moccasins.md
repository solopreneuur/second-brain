# One Moccasin, Two State Institutions

**The find:** A pair of beaded moccasins was given to June Suzuki by a student at a Native American boarding school where she taught in the 1940s — right after her own release from a Japanese-American internment camp during WWII.
**The surprise:** The object looked purely sentimental for decades, framed in a family dining room; the real story is two separate systems of American institutional control intersecting in one keepsake, unremarked on until someone asked why it existed.
**The idea:** An object can carry two unconnected histories until someone actually asks what it's doing on the wall.
