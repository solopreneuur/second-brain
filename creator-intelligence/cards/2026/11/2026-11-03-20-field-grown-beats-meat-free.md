# "Field-Grown" outsold "Meat-Free"

**The find:** Researchers field-tested café menu language across real locations. Simply renaming a dish from "Meat-free Breakfast" to "Field-Grown Breakfast" — removing the word that defines a dish by what it lacks — lifted sales of that exact same dish by 18%.
**The surprise:** The food didn't change and the recipe didn't change; just not calling it "meat-free" made people order it far more.
**The idea:** [Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md) by what something is, rather than what it isn't, changes demand without changing the product.
