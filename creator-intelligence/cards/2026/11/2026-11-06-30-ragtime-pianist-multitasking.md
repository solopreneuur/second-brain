# The pianist who broke multitasking limits

**The find:** Neuroscientist Kerstin Bettermann put an orchestra conductor and ragtime pianist Bob Milne through simultaneous-task tests. The conductor failed almost immediately; Milne played a demanding piece while holding a full conversation and joking.
**The surprise:** Decades of a very specific practice can push a skill entirely out of working memory, defeating a limit neuroscience treats as near-universal.
**The idea:** Skill automaticity: enough deeply practiced repetition can move a skill out of the attention system that normally limits multitasking.
