# Crosswalk buttons that do nothing

**The find:** In most dense US downtowns, most pedestrian crosswalk buttons are disconnected. Pressing one does nothing — the lights run on a fixed timed cycle built for traffic flow, not for anyone pressing a button.
**The surprise:** Cities never removed the dead buttons. Keeping a control that changes nothing still measurably calms impatient pedestrians and makes the wait feel less pointless.
**The idea:** [Illusion of control](../../../concepts/illusion-of-control.md): a visible, costless action gives people a sense of influence over an outcome they can't actually change, and that feeling alone makes waiting easier.
