# A neurologist calls alcohol "directly toxic"

**The find:** Asked how alcohol harms the brain, board-certified neurologist and New York Times bestselling author David Perlmutter didn't hedge: "It is directly toxic to neurons."
**The surprise:** He goes further, saying alcohol also "helps polarize our microglial cells away from being supportive" — framing a substance most people treat as a normal social habit in the same register as a direct neurological toxin.
