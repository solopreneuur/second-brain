# SunChips' bag was loud for one reason

**The find:** PepsiCo's 2010 compostable SunChips bag measured 80-85 decibels at the ear — about as loud as a subway train — and a backlash forced a redesign. Packaging engineer Brad Rodgers found the fix wasn't a new material or shape: switching the adhesive bonding the bag's plastic film layers to a more rubber-like compound cut the noise to about 70 decibels.
**The surprise:** The "eco bag is just inherently crinkly" assumption was wrong — the loudness was a glue problem, not a plastic problem.
**The idea:** [Small detail decides the outcome](../../../concepts/small-detail-decides-outcome.md) — a single hidden component, not the headline material, was driving the whole complaint.
