# Trader Joe's replaced its PA with a bell

**The find:** Trader Joe's never uses PA announcements. Staff ring a ship's bell instead, tied to the stores' maritime theme: one ring means a register needs opening, two rings mean a customer needs help, three call a manager.
**The surprise:** A sound regular shoppers have heard hundreds of times without thinking about it is actually an operating signal system, built specifically to replace the generic intercom every other chain uses.
**The idea:** [Signal encoding](../../../concepts/signal-encoding.md): a distinct, on-brand sound can carry real operational instructions while the generic version of it gets tuned out entirely.
