# Duolingo's forgiveness ceiling

**The find:** Duolingo's retention team tested giving new users 1, 2, or 3 "streak freezes" — passes on missing a day of practice without losing their streak. Two freezes was a massive retention win over one, but three performed no better than two.
**The surprise:** More slack in the habit mechanic didn't keep helping — past a precise point, extra forgiveness just taught users it was fine to skip, with zero added benefit.
**The idea:** Habit-forming mechanics have a specific threshold where leniency stops paying off and starts teaching the wrong lesson.
