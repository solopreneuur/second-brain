# Your name beat the amount owed

**The find:** The UK Courts Service and the Behavioural Insights Team tested four SMS reminders for overdue court fines. A text with just the recipient's name — no amount, no threat — raised the response rate from 5% to 33%, beating the version that also stated how much was owed (29%).
**The surprise:** Adding financial detail to the reminder made it slightly less effective than simply using someone's name.
**The idea:** A [salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md) — being addressed personally makes a message feel unavoidable in a way raw facts don't.
