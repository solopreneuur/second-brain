# Marathoners sprint to dodge a round number

**The find:** Across 9.5 million marathon finish times, runners bunch heavily just below round-number barriers like 4:00:00 — the pattern implies a loss-aversion coefficient nearly identical to Kahneman and Tversky's original lab estimate.
**The surprise:** Loss aversion isn't just a lab artifact measured with tokens or dollars — it shows up in real physical pain, in the last mile of a marathon, pushed by nothing but an arbitrary clock digit.
**The idea:** [Loss aversion](../../../concepts/loss-aversion.md) — people will suffer real cost to avoid "losing" against a number that has no actual stakes attached.
