# Telltale enforced a formula that was already failing

**The find:** A former Telltale Games designer describes CEO Kevin Bruner enforcing a rigid creative formula, killing any scene that deviated from it, even as sales had been declining "since almost the very beginning" despite hit titles like The Walking Dead.
**The surprise:** Outsiders blamed licensing costs for Telltale's 2018 collapse; insiders describe a slow, willfully ignored decline caused by suppressing creative variation.
**The idea:** [Formula lock-in](../../../concepts/formula-lock-in.md): mistaking a one-time hit's surface pattern for the actual cause of success, then enforcing it past the point of diminishing returns.
