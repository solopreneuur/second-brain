# Linktree swapped its logo for a button

**The find:** Linktree replaced the small company logo at the bottom of every public profile page with an actual "Create a Linktree" call-to-action button. Per Linktree's CPO Jiaona Zhang, this single swap nearly doubled signups.
**The surprise:** Millions of visitors were staring at a passive brand mark when it could have been a door the entire time — the fix cost almost nothing and nearly doubled the company's biggest growth loop.
**The idea:** Every passive brand touchpoint on a high-traffic page is an unclaimed conversion opportunity hiding in plain sight.
