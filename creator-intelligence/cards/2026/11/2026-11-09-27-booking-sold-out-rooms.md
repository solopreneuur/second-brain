# Booking.com sells more showing sold-out rooms

**The find:** Booking.com tested interspersing "Sold out" hotel listings among available ones in search results — a change that wastes screen space on rooms users can't buy — and overall bookings went up.
**The surprise:** Showing unavailable inventory increased sales because it made "only 1 room left" urgency claims elsewhere on the page feel credible, instead of like a trick.
**The idea:** [Quality signalling](../../../concepts/quality-signalling.md): scarcity claims need visible proof that scarcity is real somewhere on the page, or people stop believing any of them.
