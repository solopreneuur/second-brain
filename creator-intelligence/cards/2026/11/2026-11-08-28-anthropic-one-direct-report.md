# Anthropic's CEO Has One Direct Report

**The find:** Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has only one direct report, his chief of staff, while cofounder and company president Daniela Amodei (his sister) manages essentially the rest of the 2,000+ employee company — an arrangement Dario calls "incredibly freeing."
**The surprise:** It's the structural opposite of the usual "founder mode means staying deeply involved everywhere" reading — here founder mode means a hand-picked cofounder partition that frees the founder to stay concentrated on research strategy.
**The idea:** [Founder mode](../../../concepts/founder-mode.md) isn't one fixed org-chart shape — it's about choosing deliberately where a founder stays personally involved, not a generic "flatter is always better" rule.
