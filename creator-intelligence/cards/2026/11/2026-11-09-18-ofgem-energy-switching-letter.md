# One letter tripled energy switching

**The find:** Ofgem sent disengaged UK households a plain one-page letter listing the three cheapest tariffs available to them. In a 137,876-customer trial, switching rose from a 1% baseline to 3.4% — but only when the letter appeared to come from their own supplier rather than from the regulator.
**The surprise:** The exact same information, the exact same offer, tripled behavior just by changing who seemed to be sending it.
**The idea:** The [messenger effect](../../../concepts/messenger-effect.md): who delivers a message changes how much people act on it, independent of the message itself.
