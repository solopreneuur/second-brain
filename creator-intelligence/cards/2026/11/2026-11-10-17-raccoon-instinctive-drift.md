# The raccoon that broke behaviorism

**The find:** Two of B.F. Skinner's own students trained raccoons, using his reward methods, to drop coins into a toy bank for an ad — but over time the raccoons stopped depositing coins and instead dunked and rubbed them, the exact motion raccoons use to wash food.
**The surprise:** The animals had been reinforced thousands of times for the "right" behavior and still reverted to instinct — reward alone couldn't out-compete evolution, even for Skinner's own protégés.
**The idea:** [Instinctive drift](../../../concepts/instinctive-drift.md) — incentive systems that fight an existing instinct or habit tend to decay back toward that instinct no matter how well they're reinforced.
