# Oatly built a site of its scandals

**The find:** In 2023 Oatly launched a standalone site, "F*ck Oatly," listing every scandal in its history — a lost trademark suit, selling oat waste to pig farmers — with its own replies, and built a second site, "F*ck F*ck Oatly," just for people who hate the first one.
**The surprise:** A food brand built a whole second website whose only purpose is cataloguing the worst things ever said about it.
**The idea:** [Radical honesty](../../../concepts/radical-honesty.md) — naming the ugly stuff yourself before critics do removes their leverage over the story.
