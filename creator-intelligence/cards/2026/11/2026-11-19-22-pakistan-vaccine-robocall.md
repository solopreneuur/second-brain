# A phone call lifted vaccination 30%

**The find:** In Quetta, Pakistan, five of eleven government health districts received automated 3-minute "audio drama" phone calls twice a week for seven weeks, mixing parental-responsibility messaging with clinic-location reminders. A difference-in-difference analysis found a 30% increase in fully-vaccinated children relative to the areas that didn't get the calls.
**The surprise:** No clinic changed, no vaccine changed, no money changed hands — a recorded phone call nudged a population-level health outcome by nearly a third, in a setting where literacy and smartphone access are both low.
**The idea:** Removing "I forgot" or "I didn't know where" through repeated, low-cost reminders can unlock intent to comply that was already there, especially when the reminder channel matches what people can actually access.
