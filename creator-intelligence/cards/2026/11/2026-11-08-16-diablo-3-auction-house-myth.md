# Diablo 3's Most-Hated Feature Made Nothing

**The find:** Former Diablo 3 lead designer Jay Wilson revealed the game's widely mocked real-money auction house, assumed to be a cynical cash grab, existed mainly to fight item-duping hackers from Diablo 2 — he estimated it earned Blizzard only $10-15 million total, a sum World of Warcraft made "every few hours."
**The surprise:** The feature players hated most actually made the company almost nothing, and it stayed alive over a year past the point the team wanted it gone because it was printed on the retail box and lawyers feared a lawsuit.
**The idea:** [Idea-value blindness](../../../concepts/idea-value-blindness.md): a widely despised feature's stated purpose and its real purpose can be entirely different things, and reversing a written-down promise is a legal problem before it's a product one.
