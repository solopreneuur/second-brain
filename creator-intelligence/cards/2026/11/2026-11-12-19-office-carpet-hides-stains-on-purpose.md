# Office carpet patterns are a stain camouflage system

**The find:** Office and casino carpets use small, busy, high-contrast repeating patterns on purpose: the eye reads the dominant pattern before it registers a stain, so a new mark lands on top of an existing line or color block and gets visually absorbed into the design instead of standing out.
**The surprise:** The exact same camouflage logic shows up in two very different buildings, the office breakroom and the casino floor, chosen not for taste but because it buys more time between cleanings.
**The idea:** Visual noise can be a maintenance strategy, designed to overwhelm a viewer's pattern-recognition so a specific unwanted signal can't surface above it.
