# A chocolate bar that excluded women

**The find:** In 2002, Nestlé's Yorkie bar redesigned its wrapper with a "no women" prohibition icon and the slogan "It's Not for Girls," with posters reading "Not available in pink" and "King size, not Queen size."
**The surprise:** Nielsen recorded Yorkie sales up 28.7% in the year to February 2003 while the category grew only 2%, and rival Mars fell 5.4% despite a bigger ad budget; the campaign ran a decade and survived 97 unupheld complaints.
**The idea:** [Identity signalling](../../../concepts/identity-signalling.md) — deliberately narrowing a stated audience can sharpen brand identity enough to grow total sales, even while alienating the excluded group.
