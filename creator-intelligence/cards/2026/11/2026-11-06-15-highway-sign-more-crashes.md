# The highway sign that causes crashes

**The find:** Texas only displays its digital "X deaths this year" highway signs one week a month, letting researchers compare crash rates on the same roads with the sign on vs off. Crashes rose 4.5% in the 10km downstream when the sign was on — an estimated 2,600 extra crashes and 16 extra deaths a year.
**The surprise:** A safety campaign built to scare drivers straight makes the road measurably more dangerous.
**The idea:** Salience backfire: making a risk more vivid can distract from the task instead of improving caution.
