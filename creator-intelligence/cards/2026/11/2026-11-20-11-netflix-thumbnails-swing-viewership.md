# Netflix thumbnails swing viewing 20-30%

**The find:** Netflix ran an explore/exploit A/B test generating multiple thumbnail-art variants per title and serving whichever image performed best to future viewers.
**The surprise:** Swapping only the artwork — same show, same description, same algorithm recommending it — moved viewing of that title by 20% to 30%.
**The idea:** The image that governs the first half-second of [attention](../../../concepts/attention-sequence.md) can out-influence the actual content behind it.
