# Nintendo ran taxis and love hotels

**The find:** Before video games, Hiroshi Yamauchi pushed Nintendo into a taxi company, individually-portioned instant rice, and a chain of love hotels in the 1960s and 70s, all of which failed, before the company pivoted to toys and then games.
**The surprise:** The company now synonymous with Mario spent over a decade as a failed taxi-and-love-hotel conglomerate before anyone there had made a single video game.
**The idea:** [Narrative fallacy](../../../concepts/narrative-fallacy.md): the "pivot" story only reads as destiny in hindsight; at the time it was just trial and error.
