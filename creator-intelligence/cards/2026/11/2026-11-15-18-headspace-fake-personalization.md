# The quiz that wasn't personal

**The find:** Headspace, with Irrational Labs, more than doubled meditation-course starts (31% to 63%) just by adding a short "personalization" quiz before recommending a course — even though the recommended course was often the same for everyone.
**The surprise:** The feeling of being understood drove the entire lift; actual personalization barely mattered, and it didn't meaningfully increase how many days people actually meditated.
**The idea:** none
