# eBay's Pez dispenser myth was invented

**The find:** The famous story that Pierre Omidyar built eBay to help his fiancée trade Pez dispensers was invented five years after founding by PR chief Mary Lou Song, who admitted she fabricated it because "no one wants to hear about a 30-year-old genius who wanted to create a perfect market."
**The surprise:** The company's own publicist confirmed, on record, that she lied to journalists to manufacture the legend — and it worked for years, including in major papers.
**The idea:** [Narrative fallacy](../../../concepts/narrative-fallacy.md) — a clean founding myth gets manufactured and repeated as fact because it's more satisfying than the real, boring motive.
