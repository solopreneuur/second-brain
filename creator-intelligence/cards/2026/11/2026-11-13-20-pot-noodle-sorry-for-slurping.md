# Pot Noodle apologized with 49 versions of the insult

**The find:** After viewers complained about the slurping sound in a Pot Noodle ad, the brand's "apology" wasn't to remove the sound — it was to recut the ad into 49 different slurp variants, each targeted to a different audience sensitivity.
**The surprise:** The standard move after a complaint is to delete the offending element. Pot Noodle instead multiplied it and personalized it, and the campaign still drove 4.7 million incremental pots sold and a 10x return.
**The idea:** You can answer a complaint by escalating and personalizing the very thing people objected to, instead of removing it ([Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md)).
