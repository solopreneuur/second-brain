# Norway's snooping fell 88% in a year

**The find:** Norway has published every citizen's tax return since 1863; in 2013, 920,896 people ran 17 million searches on neighbors and exes. In 2014, Norway added one feature — the person you look up can now see who searched for them — and searches collapsed 88% overnight.
**The surprise:** A century and a half of radical transparency hadn't changed snooping behavior at all; making the watcher visible to the watched did it in one policy tweak.
**The idea:** [Reciprocal surveillance](../../../concepts/reciprocal-surveillance.md): people will do things anonymously that they stop doing the moment the other side can see them doing it.
