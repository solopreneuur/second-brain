# Singapore has a word for fear of missing out

**The find:** Singapore has a decades-old, dictionary-entered word — "kiasu," Hokkien for "afraid to lose" — for the specific anxious competitiveness that drives people to queue for limited offers and over-buy during sales, not to get a good price but to avoid the feeling of missing out.
**The surprise:** What Western marketers treat as a vague, slightly embarrassing manipulation tactic ("FOMO") is a named, culturally mainstream personality trait in Singapore with its own academic literature and self-aware local slang.
**The idea:** The same loss-aversion lever works everywhere, but a culture that names and owns the bias lets marketers pull it far more openly and effectively ([kiasu](../../../concepts/kiasu.md)).
