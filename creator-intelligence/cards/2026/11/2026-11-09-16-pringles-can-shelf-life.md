# Pringles' can shape is a shipping fix

**The find:** P&G's 1970 patent for the Pringles "saddle" shape exists to solve stacking, shipping, and shelf-life problems, not taste — uniform hyperbolic-paraboloid chips nest with almost no empty space inside a sealed tube.
**The surprise:** Inventor Fredric Baur was so attached to the design that part of his cremated remains were buried in a Pringles can, per his family.
**The idea:** [Logistics-driven design](../../../concepts/logistics-driven-design.md): the shape of the chip was engineered for the truck, and the taste followed.
