# The ad everyone hated sold out in days

**The find:** Protein World's 2015 London Underground ad "Are You Beach Body Ready?" triggered a 70,000-plus-signature petition and a Hyde Park protest for body-shaming. The company reportedly made roughly £1 million in direct sales in the four days following the backlash, against a £250,000 campaign spend.
**The surprise:** The company never pulled the ad or apologised — the outrage cycle itself became the distribution channel, turning a quarter-million-pound billboard buy into a reported seven-figure sales spike almost overnight.
**The idea:** [Outrage as distribution](../../../concepts/outrage-as-distribution.md): a backlash that spreads a brand's name for free can outperform the ad's intended message, if the brand is willing to absorb the anger instead of apologising it away.
