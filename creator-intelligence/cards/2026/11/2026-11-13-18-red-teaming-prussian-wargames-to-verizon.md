# Red teaming began as a 19th-century war game

**The find:** "Red teaming" — assigning a group to attack your own plan as if they were the enemy — started as Prussian kriegsspiel war-gaming, was formalized by RAND and the Pentagon during the Cold War, and got institutionalized department-wide after the 9/11 intelligence failure. Verizon has since built it into its own strategic-planning approval process.
**The surprise:** The people who built a plan are structurally the worst people to find its flaws — so the practice had to invent a separate team with the explicit job of trying to break it, rather than trusting objections to surface on their own.
**The idea:** An organization can't reliably stress-test its own plan using the people who built it; it needs an assigned opposition with a mandate to attack ([Structured dissent](../../../concepts/structured-dissent.md)).
