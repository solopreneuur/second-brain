# F1's "undercut" fails if you half-commit

**The find:** Measuring 81 real undercut attempts across the 2025 Formula 1 season — pitting for fresh tires one or two laps before a rival to gain position — pitting one lap early worked only 34% of the time, while pitting two laps early worked 69%.
**The surprise:** One lap isn't a smaller version of the same move, it's a worse one: the cold-tire out-lap eats the entire advantage, and the payoff only shows up on the second flying lap.
**The idea:** A half-measure in a competitive move can be strictly worse than either staying put or going further, because the cost of the move is paid up front while the payoff arrives late.
