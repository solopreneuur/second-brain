# The flight deck that never panics

**The find:** Researchers studied why nuclear aircraft carrier flight decks, one of the most dangerous workplaces on Earth, have excellent safety records. The answer: low-ranking crew are trusted to override rank and speak up the instant something looks wrong.
**The surprise:** Safety doesn't come from stricter rules or more supervision. It comes from authority migrating to whoever has the most relevant information in the moment, regardless of rank.
**The idea:** [High reliability organization](../../../concepts/high-reliability-organization.md): "preoccupation with failure" and deference to expertise over hierarchy, now taught in hospitals, nuclear plants and banks.
