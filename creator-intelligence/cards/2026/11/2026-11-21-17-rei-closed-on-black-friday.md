# REI Closed Shop on Black Friday

**The find:** REI closed all 143 stores and froze online purchase processing on Black Friday 2015, paying 12,000+ employees to stay home on the single biggest shopping day of the year.
**The surprise:** Online traffic rose 26% that day — people showed up to a site that couldn't even take an order — and REI's digital sales grew 23% for the year.
**The idea:** [Counter-positioning](../../../concepts/counter-positioning.md) — refusing to compete on the industry's own terms can be the stronger competitive move.
