# A Google exec's notes compared search ads to cigarettes

**The find:** In personal notes for an internal 2017 communications-training exercise, Google finance VP Michael Roszak called search advertising "one of the world's greatest business models ever created," compared its economics to "illicit businesses (cigarettes or drugs)," and wrote that Google could "tear the economics textbook in half" by ignoring demand-side economics.
**The surprise:** The notes surfaced as a trial exhibit in the DOJ's antitrust case against Google. Roszak testified they were deliberately hyperbolic "Gordon Gekko cosplay" written for a training exercise — not his real view — but the document still became public, permanent evidence regardless of his explanation.
**The idea:** Material created for a private, hypothetical exercise can later become the one document that defines how an outsider reads your actual business ([Unfiltered admission](../../../concepts/unfiltered-admission.md)).
