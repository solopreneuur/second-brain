# The letter line that beat social proof

**The find:** Singapore's Land Transport Authority ran a trial on about 1 million vehicle owners' road-tax renewal notices: a loss-aversion line ("Avoid Late Renewal Fees") lifted on-time renewal from 88.4% to 90.1%, beating a social-norms line ("9 out of 10 renew on time"), which only reached 88.9%.
**The surprise:** The famous "everyone else does it" social-proof nudge, the one most playbooks lead with, actually underperformed a plain loss-aversion line, at the scale of a million people, with nothing changed but two sentences.
**The idea:** Social proof isn't a universal best nudge — for compliance and avoidance behaviors, naming the cost of inaction can beat naming the crowd's behavior ([loss framing](../../../concepts/loss-framing.md)).
