# The square milk jug nobody asked for

**The find:** In 2008 Sam's Club and Walmart switched to flat-topped, stackable square gallon milk jugs. Trade coverage reports the flat top let trucks carry about 9% more milk per load, cutting an estimated 11,000 truckloads a year and crate-washing water use 60-70%.
**The surprise:** Shoppers hated it immediately, harder to pour, more spills, and the retailer pushed ahead anyway because the pallet math was that favourable.
**The idea:** [Logistics-driven design](../../../concepts/logistics-driven-design.md): when supply-chain savings are big enough, a company will ship a worse everyday experience on purpose.
