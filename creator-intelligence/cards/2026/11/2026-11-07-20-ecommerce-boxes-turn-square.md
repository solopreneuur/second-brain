# E-commerce boxes are turning square for robots

**The find:** Warehouses are reshaping e-commerce packaging into squarer, boxier forms, not to save cardboard, but because the suction-cup grippers on warehouse robots need a flat, rigid, predictable surface to pick up — irregular or flimsy shapes make the robot drop the box.
**The surprise:** The customer never sees a robot, yet the box in their hands changed shape because of one.
**The idea:** [Logistics-driven design](../../../concepts/logistics-driven-design.md): a visible consumer product detail can be dictated entirely by a machine three steps upstream that the customer will never meet.
