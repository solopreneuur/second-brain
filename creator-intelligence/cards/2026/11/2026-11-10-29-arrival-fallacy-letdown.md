# The letdown after you make it

**The find:** Harvard positive-psychology lecturer Tal Ben-Shahar coined "arrival fallacy" for the flat, anticlimactic feeling after a major achievement — finishing a degree, landing the job, hitting the number — where people expect euphoria and instead feel empty.
**The surprise:** It inverts the core assumption behind goal-setting culture, that arriving is the reward, and names why even people who "made it" often report feeling worse right after their biggest win.
**The idea:** The [arrival fallacy](../../../concepts/arrival-fallacy.md).
