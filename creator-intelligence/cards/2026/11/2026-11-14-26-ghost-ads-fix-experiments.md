# Placebo ads fixed broken ad experiments

**The find:** Following up on their own famous finding that most ad experiments are too underpowered to prove anything, the same researchers ran a 3-million-user Yahoo field experiment and found a real, significant 3.6% sales lift — not by gathering more data, but by using "ghost ads" (placebo-ad exposure logs) to strip out noise unrelated to the ad itself, buying the equivalent of 31% more statistical power.
**The surprise:** The fix for "advertising can't be measured" wasn't a bigger sample — it was deleting the right kind of noise, and demographic targeting data, the industry's favorite lever, barely helped at all (only 5% more power).
**The idea:** When a measurement is too noisy to trust, the highest-leverage move is often removing irrelevant variance, not adding more data ([statistical power blindness](../../../concepts/statistical-power-blindness.md)).
