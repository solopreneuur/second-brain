# China's wallet-return rate wasn't a honesty problem

**The find:** In the original global lost-wallet study, China had the lowest reporting rate in the world for wallets with no money inside, under 10%. A 2025 replication across Shanghai and Beijing found that printing a QR code to WeChat, instead of asking finders to email a stranger, pushed the rate up to 59.3%.
**The surprise:** The "dishonest country" finding was partly a data artifact of using the wrong contact method. Honesty didn't need fixing; the contact channel did.
**The idea:** [Civic honesty](../../../concepts/civic-honesty.md): measuring a behaviour through a channel people won't use looks exactly like the behaviour not existing.
