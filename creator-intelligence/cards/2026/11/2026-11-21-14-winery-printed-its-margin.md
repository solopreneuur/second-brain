# A Winery Printed Its Own Margin

**The find:** Alit Wines, founded by Hollywood producer Mark Tarlov, priced a Pinot Noir at $27.45 and published the full cost breakdown alongside it — $5.66 farming, $2.14 labor, about $15.10 total production cost.
**The surprise:** The label states plainly this means a 45% margin, $12.35 profit per bottle, in an industry built entirely on the customer never knowing true cost.
**The idea:** [Radical honesty](../../../concepts/radical-honesty.md) — voluntarily printing the exact number everyone else hides.
