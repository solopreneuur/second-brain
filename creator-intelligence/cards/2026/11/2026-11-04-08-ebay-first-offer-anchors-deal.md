# eBay's first offer wins the deal

**The find:** Across 26,454,176 real eBay "Best Offer" negotiations, final sale price tracked the buyer's own first offer almost linearly rather than settling near the midpoint; the single best average opening bid was 80% of the seller's list price.
**The surprise:** Everyone assumes haggling lands near the middle; the data shows the deal gravitates toward whoever anchors first, and bidding lower than 80% mostly just raises the odds the seller walks.
**The idea:** [Anchoring](../../../concepts/anchoring.md): the first number spoken pulls the final number toward it far more than "meeting in the middle" folk wisdom predicts.
