# The mattress tag was never about you

**The find:** The "under penalty of law" tag sewn into mattresses dates to early-1900s anti-fraud rules forcing manufacturers to disclose stuffing, after some filled mattresses with recycled rags and horsehair; the law targeted resellers passing off used mattresses as new, not the person sleeping on it.
**The surprise:** A warning label millions of people have quietly obeyed for decades was never written for them at all.
**The idea:** A disclosure rule aimed at one party in a supply chain can be misread by the public as a rule about them, and the misreading can outlive the original problem by a century.
