# Marlboro used to be a women's cigarette

**The find:** Marlboro launched in 1924 as a "mild as May" luxury cigarette for women, with ads promising it wouldn't smear lipstick. In the 1950s Philip Morris hired Leo Burnett to re-gender it, testing sea captains and weightlifters before settling on the cowboy.
**The surprise:** The most masculine brand icon of 20th-century advertising was a deliberate rebrand of a struggling women's brand, not a creation from scratch.
**The idea:** [Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md): the same product can be repositioned to a completely different audience with imagery alone, no formula change needed.
