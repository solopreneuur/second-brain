# Chick-fil-A Never Calculated the Cost

**The find:** Chick-fil-A has closed every Sunday since 1946; analysts estimated the closure cost over $1.2 billion a year in forgone sales back in 2019, yet CEO Andrew Cathy told Fortune in October 2026 he has never once calculated what opening would earn — "it's never something that we're entertaining" — while the chain posted $23.9 billion in 2025 sales on six days a week.
**The surprise:** The leader of the company treats the foregone-revenue math as not worth running at all, not because the number doesn't matter but because answering it would open a negotiation he refuses to have.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md): refusing to even calculate the cost of a value-driven rule is itself a strategy — it removes the rule from the realm of ROI debate entirely.
