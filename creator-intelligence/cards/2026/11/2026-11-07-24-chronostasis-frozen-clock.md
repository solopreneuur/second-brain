# The second hand that seems to freeze

**The find:** "Chronostasis" names the moment you glance at an analogue clock and the second hand seems to hang frozen before it ticks on. Researcher Kielan Yarrow showed the brain backfills the instant after a rapid eye movement with the image it's about to see, stretching the first "tick" by up to half a second.
**The surprise:** Your eyes aren't seeing a longer first second. Your brain is retroactively editing what it thinks you saw during the eye movement itself.
**The idea:** [Chronostasis](../../../concepts/chronostasis.md): perception isn't a live feed, it's a reconstruction stitched together after the fact, and the stitching sometimes leaves a visible seam.
