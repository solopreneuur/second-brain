# Blinkist Named the Fear Upfront

**The find:** Blinkist designer Jaycee Day rewrote the free-trial paywall to state, before signup, the exact objection users had ("I'll forget to cancel and get billed") instead of hiding it in fine print — trial signups rose 23%, complaints fell 55%, and push-notification opt-ins jumped from 6% to 74%.
**The surprise:** Naming the scary, embarrassing objection out loud, rather than burying it, made more people sign up, not fewer.
**The idea:** [Radical honesty](../../../concepts/radical-honesty.md): stating the thing a user fears most removes the ambient anxiety blocking conversion.
