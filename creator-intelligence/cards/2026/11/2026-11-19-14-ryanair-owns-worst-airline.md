# Ryanair trolls itself as "worst airline"

**The find:** After being voted "worst airline in Europe" by consumer group Which? six years running, Ryanair's social media team didn't run a reputation-repair campaign — they leaned into the hate, posting self-deprecating TikToks that troll its own passengers and critics.
**The surprise:** Confirming the insult instead of denying it turned legitimate customer grievances into one of the highest-engagement airline accounts on the platform, at close to zero media spend.
**The idea:** When your bad reputation is already common knowledge, owning it louder than anyone else disarms it faster than any denial or PR campaign could.
