# People pay to add, not subtract

**The find:** Researchers gave people a wobbly Lego platform to stabilize, charging 10 cents per brick added while removing a block — which worked just as well — was free. Only 41% of participants found the free fix; most paid to add instead.
**The surprise:** Even with money on the line and an equally good free option sitting in plain sight, most people default to building up rather than stripping down.
**The idea:** [Subtraction neglect](../../../concepts/subtraction-neglect.md): the mind systematically overlooks removing something as a way to improve it.
