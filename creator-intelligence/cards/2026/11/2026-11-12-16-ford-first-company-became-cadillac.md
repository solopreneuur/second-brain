# Henry Ford's first car company became Cadillac

**The find:** Henry Ford's second company, the Henry Ford Company (1901), collapsed when he clashed with investors over racing cars versus production cars. He walked away in 1902 with nothing but his name and $900. The investors brought in engineer Henry Leland to liquidate the company; Leland reorganized it instead, renaming it the Cadillac Automobile Company.
**The surprise:** Two of the most storied names in American car history, Ford and Cadillac, grow out of the exact same failed venture, split only by who was left holding it when Ford walked.
**The idea:** The same failure can seed two completely different legacies depending on who's left to pick it up afterward.
