# Ferrari Began as a Legal Workaround

**The find:** Barred by a four-year non-compete from racing under his own name after leaving Alfa Romeo in 1939, Enzo Ferrari founded Auto Avio Costruzioni, a Modena machine-tool and aircraft-parts maker for Italy's wartime government, and only secretly built two race cars in 1939-40 that legally couldn't carry the Ferrari name.
**The surprise:** The marque now built entirely around its founder's name began life as a defense-contracting machine-tool company, created specifically because its founder was legally forbidden to put his name on a car.
**The idea:** [Forced detour](../../../concepts/forced-detour.md): a constraint imposed from outside pushed Ferrari through an unrelated industrial business that ended up funding and masking the real ambition until the ban expired.
