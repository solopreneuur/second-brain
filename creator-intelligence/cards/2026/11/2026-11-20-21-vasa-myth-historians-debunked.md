# The warship sank over an email no one sent

**The find:** Everyone "knew" Sweden's 1628 warship Vasa sank because King Gustav Adolf micromanaged the design and forced on a late second gun deck — but the gun order was placed two years before launch, long before the hull existed. Vasa Museum research shows it actually sank because Vice Admiral Fleming watched a stability test fail after just 3 of a planned 20 passes, then never told the king, continuing to write him letters without once mentioning the ship was unstable.
**The surprise:** The textbook "tyrant king ruined it by meddling" story museums repeated for twenty years turns out to be invented — the real cause was a subordinate too afraid to deliver bad news to a feared boss.
**The idea:** [Psychological safety](../../../concepts/psychological-safety.md) — the best-performing teams aren't the ones that make the fewest errors, they're the ones where people feel safe admitting the errors they do make.
