# SpaceX flew rockets on ATM computers

**The find:** Early SpaceX avionics ran on the same class of roughly $5,000 computer as a bank ATM, tripled up for redundancy, instead of $1 million radiation-hardened aerospace processors. The Merlin engine's pintle injector design dates to the 1960s Apollo lunar module, chosen for cost and stability over a modern design.
**The surprise:** The rockets that out-innovated the aerospace industry ran on deliberately decades-old, cheap parts.
**The idea:** [Withered technology](../../../concepts/withered-technology.md): choosing mature, well-understood tech over the newest spec.
