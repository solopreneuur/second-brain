# The man who invented hope

**The find:** L.L. Zamenhof published the constructed language Esperanto in 1887 under the pseudonym "Doktoro Esperanto" ("Doctor Hopeful One"). Readers adopted the pseudonym as the language's actual name. He included tear-out pledge cards in the book; mailed-back cards became the first adopter directory.
**The surprise:** The language's name was never meant to be its name. It was the inventor's pen name, adopted by the fanbase before there was a network to join.
**The idea:** [Bootstrapped network effect](../../../concepts/bootstrapped-network-effect.md): a mail-in commitment device can manufacture demand and community before the thing they're joining exists yet.
