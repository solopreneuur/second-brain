# Snack bags are mostly air

**The find:** A 2025 engineering test found Cheetos bags run about 59% empty space (nitrogen gas, not air), Doritos about 48%, Popchips about 50% — while Fritos sit at just 19% and Pringles 28%. There's no legal cap on this "slack fill" as long as a brand can argue it's "functional," which is why PepsiCo is currently being sued over PopCorners.
**The surprise:** Nearly identical snack categories differ by 40 percentage points in how empty the bag looks, entirely legally, because the only rule is a vague "functional" justification with zero numeric ceiling.
**The idea:** Regulatory vagueness as a business asset — when a rule says "must be functional" without defining a limit, companies push the definition as far as the lawsuit threshold allows.
