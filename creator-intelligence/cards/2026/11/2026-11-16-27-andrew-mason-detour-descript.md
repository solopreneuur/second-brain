# The tool built to make the product outlived it

**The find:** After Groupon, Andrew Mason built Detour, an audio walking-tour app. While producing tours, his team built an internal tool that edited audio by editing its transcript. Detour itself sold to Bose in 2018 for an undisclosed sum — but the internal editing tool became Descript, a standalone editor Mason later returned to as executive chairman.
**The surprise:** The side tool built only to serve the real product outgrew and outlasted the product itself.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): the infrastructure built to make a thing can end up worth more than the thing.
