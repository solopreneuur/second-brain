# One model edited another's brain directly

**The find:** MIT CSAIL had one model directly edit a smaller, stuck model's weights through natural-language instructions, with no retraining loop — changing just 131,072 weights (0.016% of all parameters) and lifting its MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1%.
**The surprise:** A model's capability jumped sixfold from touching a sliver of a percent of its parameters, directly, the way a surgeon operates rather than the way a trainer coaches.
**The idea:** Most of a system's failure often traces to a small, specific, locatable set of weights or habits, not a diffuse general weakness — the fix can be surgical instead of a full retrain.
