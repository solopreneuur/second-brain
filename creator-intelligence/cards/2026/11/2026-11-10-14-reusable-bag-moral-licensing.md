# Reusable bags license junk food

**The find:** Analyzing two million grocery transactions, researchers found shoppers who brought reusable bags bought more organic food — but also more ice cream, cookies and chips; a follow-up study found the effect vanished when stores required the bags instead of letting shoppers choose to bring them.
**The surprise:** The reusable bag, sold on its environmental virtue, quietly functions as a permission slip to buy more junk — and only works that way if bringing it felt voluntary.
**The idea:** [Moral licensing](../../../concepts/moral-licensing.md) — virtue bought in one aisle gets spent in the next.
