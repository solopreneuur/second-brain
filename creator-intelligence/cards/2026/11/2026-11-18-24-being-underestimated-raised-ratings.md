# Being underestimated raised performance ratings

**The find:** Organizational psychologist Samir Nurmohamed's research found that employees who felt underestimated by others went on to receive higher job-performance ratings from their own supervisors than peers who didn't feel underestimated.
**The surprise:** Being doubted by your boss can statistically predict that the same boss will later rate you more highly.
**The idea:** The underdog effect — feeling written off can function as fuel rather than discouragement, pushing people to work harder specifically to prove the doubt wrong ([Underdog effect](../../../concepts/underdog-effect.md)).
