# Skittles spent nine years apologizing

**The find:** When Skittles brought back its lime flavor in 2022, nine years after swapping it for green apple, the relaunch campaign apologized individually to all 138,880 people who had publicly complained online, via a live-streamed "press conference" admitting the mistake.
**The surprise:** Instead of a normal relaunch, the brand spent its budget apologizing to strangers for a decade-old decision, and it produced the brand's highest core US sales in its history.
**The idea:** Reviving a specific, named grievance and performing contrition at scale, a kind of [radical honesty](../../../concepts/radical-honesty.md), can out-market a straightforward comeback story.
