# The spoon that overdoses you

**The find:** Cornell researchers had 195 people measure a dose of cold medicine using different-sized kitchen spoons; the medium spoon made people under-pour by 8.4%, the large spoon made them over-pour by 11.6% — and everyone felt confident they'd measured correctly.
**The surprise:** Nobody actually measures liquid medicine, they estimate "a spoonful," and the spoon's own size silently recalibrates what counts as a full dose.
**The idea:** [Unit bias](../../../concepts/unit-bias.md): people treat whatever container or unit something comes in as "one correct amount," regardless of its actual size.
