# The "good" default that cut real donors

**The find:** Across 24 countries from 2000-2023, switching organ donation to opt-out raised deceased-donor rates by a statistically insignificant 7%, but cut living-donor rates by 29%, because people assumed the new default had already solved the shortage.
**The surprise:** The behavioral economics textbook's favorite "good" default quietly suppresses a different, harder-to-replace kind of giving it never measured.
**The idea:** [The default effect](../../../concepts/default-effect.md) can crowd out voluntary behavior: engineering a default to lift one behavior can shrink a related behavior by changing what people believe still needs doing.
