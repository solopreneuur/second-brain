# Air fresheners, the new secondhand smoke

**The find:** On Modern Wisdom, air-quality entrepreneur Michael Feldstein says synthetic fragrances — Febreze, plug-ins, scented candles — will one day be looked back on the way we now look back on secondhand smoke, dismissed as harmless for decades before being taken seriously.
**The surprise:** The comparison isn't to a chemical or a disease, it's to a social turning point — the moment a totally normal household habit quietly became unacceptable.
**The idea:** A habit doesn't get flagged as harmful because of new evidence alone; it gets flagged once enough people decide it's no longer acceptable to dismiss the evidence, the same arc secondhand smoke went through.
