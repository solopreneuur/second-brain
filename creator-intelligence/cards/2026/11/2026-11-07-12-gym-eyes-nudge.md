# A photo of eyes made gym-goers wipe down

**The find:** Researchers taped a simple picture of a pair of watching eyes onto the sanitizer and paper-towel dispensers at two Oslo gyms. Equipment-wiping compliance rose sharply whenever the eyes were up, and fell again once they came down.
**The surprise:** No one was actually watching. A flat photo of eyes, with nobody behind it, was enough to make gym members behave as if they were being supervised.
**The idea:** [Salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md): a cue that merely resembles being watched activates the same self-monitoring as a real observer.
