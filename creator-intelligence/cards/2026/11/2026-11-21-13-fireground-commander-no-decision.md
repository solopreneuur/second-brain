# The Commander Who Made No Decision

**The find:** In Gary Klein's foundational fireground study, a commander pulled his crew from a quiet, too-hot "routine" kitchen fire on a hunch — seconds later the floor collapsed into a hidden basement fire.
**The surprise:** Asked how he decided, he said: "I don't make decisions. I don't remember when I've ever made a decision." That absence of deliberation is the marker of expertise, not its opposite.
**The idea:** [Recognition-primed decision making](../../../concepts/recognition-primed-decision.md) — experts act on the first workable pattern-match under time pressure; they don't compare options.
