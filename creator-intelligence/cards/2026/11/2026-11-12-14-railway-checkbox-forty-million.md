# A railway found $40 million in one checkbox

**The find:** A European national railroad switched seat reservations from opt-in (an extra click to add one, for a small fee) to opt-out (bundled by default, with a checkbox to remove it). Reservation attach rate jumped from 9% to 47%, adding an estimated $40 million a year, from a single flipped default.
**The surprise:** No price change, no new feature, no marketing spend behind it, the entire gain came from which state the checkbox started in.
**The idea:** People read whatever is pre-selected as the normal or recommended choice, and will actively opt out of a default far less often than they would have opted in to the same option ([Default effect](../../../concepts/default-effect.md)).
