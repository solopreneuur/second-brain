# Duolingo's one red dot

**The find:** Duolingo's growth lead added a single red notification dot to the app icon, signaling something unresolved — six lines of code, built in about 20 minutes. It raised daily active users 6%, and a refined version added another 1.6%.
**The surprise:** The cheapest, dumbest-sounding change outperformed Duolingo's deliberately designed gamification bets like badges and streaks.
**The idea:** The [Zeigarnik effect](../../../concepts/zeigarnik-effect.md): an unfinished or unresolved task pulls at attention until it's closed.
