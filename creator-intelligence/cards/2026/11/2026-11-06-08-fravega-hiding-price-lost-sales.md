# Hiding the price lost the sale

**The find:** Economists ran a field experiment with Frávega, Argentina's major appliance retailer, on 771,583 customers: one email version hid the price, one showed it. The no-price version got more clicks per customer but 11% fewer purchases (2.31% vs 2.61%).
**The surprise:** Hiding the price is the classic curiosity-gap growth hack — and it reliably won the click while losing the sale.
**The idea:** Curiosity-gap backfire: a tactic built to drive engagement can work against the very purchase it's meant to produce.
