# A Billing Formula Shrank Your Boxes

**The find:** In January 2015, FedEx and UPS extended "dimensional weight" pricing to all ground packages — billing by a box's volume, not its actual weight — and FedEx Ground's billed weight rose 28.9% above actual weight that year, an estimated $550 million hit to shippers that set off a retailer scramble to cut box size and air-fill.
**The surprise:** The "greener, less wasteful packaging" story most brands tell is substantially a reaction to a 2015 carrier billing-policy change almost no shopper ever heard of, not a sustainability initiative.
**The idea:** [Logistics-driven design](../../../concepts/logistics-driven-design.md): a pricing-formula change inside a supplier's billing system can rewrite product packaging worldwide faster than any design brief.
