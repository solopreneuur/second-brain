# Specsavers made its own ads look bad

**The find:** Specsavers' "Lost Ladder" campaign deliberately placed its own billboards crookedly, half-hidden behind trees, or blocked by ladders — bad outdoor-media placements — to punchline its 22-year-old tagline, "Should've gone to Specsavers."
**The surprise:** An advertiser spent real media budget to make its own ads look like mistakes, and econometric modeling credited the stunt with roughly 582,000 incremental eye tests and £44 million in revenue, a 3.6:1 return.
**The idea:** [Anti-branding](../../../concepts/anti-branding.md): deliberately breaking your own brand's polish to prove the joke, and the brand, is in on it.
