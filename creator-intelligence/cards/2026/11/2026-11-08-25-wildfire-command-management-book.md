# Wildfire Command Borrowed a Management Book

**The find:** After California's catastrophic 1970 fire season, Congress funded FIRESCOPE to design what became the Incident Command System — now the backbone of almost all emergency response worldwide — and its designers built it explicitly around Peter Drucker's 1954 business concept "Management by Objectives" and the "span of control" principle from management theory.
**The surprise:** The usual direction of borrowing is "military discipline teaches business" — here it ran backward, with a civilian management-consulting idea from a bestselling business book becoming the operating skeleton firefighters and FEMA use to run life-or-death incidents.
**The idea:** [Cross-domain transfer](../../../concepts/cross-domain-transfer.md): the best operating system for chaos may already exist in an unrelated field's management literature.
