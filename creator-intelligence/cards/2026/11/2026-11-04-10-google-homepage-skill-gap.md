# Google's blank homepage was a skill gap

**The find:** In a 2000 interview, Sergey Brin said Google's famously blank homepage happened because "we didn't have a webmaster," producing a bare page they then kept on purpose once they realized people wanted to search fast, not browse a portal.
**The surprise:** The minimalism now taught as deliberate UX genius started as the founders lacking the skill and staff to build anything more complicated.
**The idea:** A celebrated design principle can be the retrofit justification for a constraint, not its origin.
