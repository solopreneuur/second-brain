# A pie chart saved a government survey

**The find:** Australia's Behavioural Economics Team added nothing to a government survey except a gradually-filling pie-chart progress indicator. Completion rose from 84.7% to 91.5% — cutting non-completion by nearly half — with no new questions, incentives, or shortening.
**The surprise:** The fix wasn't to make the survey easier or shorter, just to show people, visually, how close to done they already were.
**The idea:** [The endowed progress effect](../../../concepts/endowed-progress-effect.md) — showing existing progress toward a goal makes people more likely to finish it, even when nothing about the task itself has changed.
