# A champagne house destroyed a vintage

**The find:** After a full year of growing, harvesting and fermenting, Dom Pérignon's cellar master judged the entire 2023 base wine too diluted from a record-hot July and rejected the whole vintage — no 2023 Dom Pérignon will ever exist, only the fourth time this century.
**The surprise:** Nothing external forced this — no recall, no complaint, no regulator — just one person's internal taste bar destroying a year's harvest on nobody's authority but his own, then turning the loss into a public museum exhibit instead of hiding it.
**The idea:** Voluntarily discarding your own output over a standard only you can perceive is a quality signal that's nearly impossible to fake, because nobody was forcing the disclosure ([quality signalling](../../../concepts/quality-signalling.md)).
