# Friendster died rather than cut a feature

**The find:** Friendster invented the social-network format, but pages took up to 40 seconds to load at its peak — the site recalculated each user's "degrees of separation" count on nearly every page view. Founder Jonathan Abrams called the feature central to Friendster's magic and refused to simplify it, even as the board responded by just buying more servers.
**The surprise:** The company that invented the category didn't lose to a better idea — it lost to its own founder's refusal to cut his favorite feature.
**The idea:** [Feature worship](../../../concepts/feature-worship.md) — a feature can become so tied to a founder's identity that fixing its cost becomes unthinkable, even as it kills the product.
