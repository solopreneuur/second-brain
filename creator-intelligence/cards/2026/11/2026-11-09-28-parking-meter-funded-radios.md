# A parking meter funded police radios

**The find:** Oklahoma City traffic commissioner Carl Magee built the first parking meter in 1935 specifically to force quick turnover of curb spots for retailers. It generated $3,541 in its first year, which the city used to buy the police department new radios.
**The surprise:** A device retailers initially feared as anti-customer instead proved that forced turnover grew foot traffic — and it bankrolled city infrastructure besides.
**The idea:** No named concept — a parking restriction paying for itself twice over, once in retail traffic and once in city revenue.
