# Mountaineers ranked the summit third

**The find:** After nearly starving on the first American expedition to K2 in 1938, climber Paul Petzoldt coined "expedition behavior," a code where the summit ranks third, after everyone returning alive and everyone returning friends. Adam Grant later used it to show why workplace "givers," who serve the team's goals ahead of their own status, outperform over time.
**The surprise:** The explicit ranking — survival first, relationships second, the goal itself only third — came out of a near-disaster, not team-building theory, and most "high-performing team" advice still gets that order backwards.
**The idea:** [Cross-domain transfer](../../../concepts/cross-domain-transfer.md): teams that openly rank relationship-preservation above the stated objective paradoxically hit the objective more often, because nobody is hoarding effort to protect their own standing.
