# The Warning Light Nobody Installed

**The find:** The ferry Herald of Free Enterprise capsized in 90 seconds off Zeebrugge in 1987, killing 193 people, because its bow doors were left open — the fifth known instance for that operator.
**The surprise:** Captains had already asked management for a simple bridge warning light showing the doors' status; the request was refused as unnecessary. The fix that became global maritime law (the IMO's mandatory International Safety Management Code) was something frontline staff had proposed years earlier and been turned down for.
**The idea:** An [ignored warning](../../../concepts/ignored-warning.md) — small, repeated deviations compound while the fix someone already proposed sits in an inbox.
