# A Ticketmaster exec texted "robbing them"

**The find:** In the DOJ's antitrust trial against Live Nation/Ticketmaster, unsealed internal text messages show a ticketing executive calling VIP-upgrade customers "so stupid," writing "I almost feel bad taking advantage of them," and bragging "Robbing them blind, baby" about markups on add-on fees at one show.
**The surprise:** The company's own staff described their most profitable pricing lever — fees added on top of the advertised ticket price — using the exact language of a confidence scam, in writing, to each other.
**The idea:** [Ancillary monetization](../../../concepts/ancillary-monetization.md): the real profit often sits in the add-ons layered after the headline price, not the price itself — which is also why it's the part most worth hiding.
