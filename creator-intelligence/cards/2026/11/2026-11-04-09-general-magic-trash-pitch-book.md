# The pitch book found in a dumpster

**The find:** Before General Magic became a company, the entire pitch to Apple's John Sculley was a physical concept book called "Paradigm," with metal covers and mocked-up devices and ads. A General Magic employee later found a surviving copy in a company dumpster during an office clear-out.
**The surprise:** The document that pitched a smartphone-like device nearly two decades early survived mainly because one employee happened to dig through the trash.
**The idea:** A single physical artifact built to sell a vision can outlast the company it was built to convince.
