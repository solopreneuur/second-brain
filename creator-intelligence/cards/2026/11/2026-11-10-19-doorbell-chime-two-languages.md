# Doorbells speak two languages

**The find:** Classic wired doorbell chimes use two tone bars and two buttons: the front-door button strikes both bars for a two-note "ding-dong," the back-door button strikes only one bar for a single "ding" — so the household knows which door to answer without looking.
**The surprise:** A sound heard a thousand times was built to carry a second piece of information for free.
**The idea:** Signal encoding — packing extra information into the shape of a signal itself, rather than adding a separate channel for it.
