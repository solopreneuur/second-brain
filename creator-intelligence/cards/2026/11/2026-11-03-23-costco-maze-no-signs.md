# Costco's co-founder calls his own layout "manipulative"

**The find:** Robert Price, co-founder of Price Club (which merged into Costco in 1993), told NPR's Planet Money on tape that he was "adamant" Costco would never have aisle signs telling shoppers where things are — so customers would be forced to wander every aisle and stumble into unplanned purchases.
**The surprise:** Asked to describe the choice, the founder himself said: "I think it was... manipulative."
**The idea:** Deliberately engineered wayfinding [friction](../../../concepts/friction.md) increases basket size by forcing more exposure to the shelves.
