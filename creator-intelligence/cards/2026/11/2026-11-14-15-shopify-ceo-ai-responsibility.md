# Shopify's CEO would hand it to AI

**The find:** Asked by Shane Parrish, "If AI got better than you, would you actually let it run Shopify?", Shopify founder and CEO Tobi Lütke answers without hesitation: "Oh yeah, of course" — then says the one thing AI still can't do is "take responsibility."
**The surprise:** A sitting billion-dollar-company CEO casually agreeing he'd hand over the keys to AI is not the answer you expect from the person whose entire job and identity is being CEO.
**The idea:** Judgment can be delegated; responsibility can't — that's the one irreducible job of a leader ([judgment vs. responsibility](../../../concepts/judgment-vs-responsibility.md)).
