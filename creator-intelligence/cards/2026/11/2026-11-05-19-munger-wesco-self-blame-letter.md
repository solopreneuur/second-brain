# Munger blamed only himself

**The find:** In Wesco Financial's 1994 letter, Charlie Munger writes that a money-losing foray into the electrical-equipment business was "entirely the result of misjudgment by the writer," and insists "the writer caused Wesco's loss, not Glen Mitchel," naming and clearing the subsidiary's CEO by name.
**The surprise:** Rather than a vague corporate "lessons were learned," Munger names the exact mental habit that caused the loss and insists, in print, on taking 100% of the blame himself.
**The idea:** Precise self-blame — naming the exact cognitive error, not just "we made a mistake" — as a leadership credibility move.
