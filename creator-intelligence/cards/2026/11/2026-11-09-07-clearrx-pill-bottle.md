# A mislabeled bottle redesigned pharmacy

**The find:** Design student Deborah Adler's grandmother swallowed her husband's medication by mistake because both their prescription bottles were labeled "H. Adler" and looked identical. Adler's graduate thesis became Target's 2005 ClearRx system: the bottle turned so the label faces the patient, with color-coded rings for each family member.
**The surprise:** A near-miss caught by a design grad student, not a pharmaceutical company's safety team, rewired how a Fortune-500 retailer packages every prescription in its stores — and the bottle now sits in MoMA's permanent collection.
**The idea:** An [accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): the fix for one family's mistake became the industry's new default.
