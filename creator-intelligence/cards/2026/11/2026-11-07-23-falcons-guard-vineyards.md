# Falcons that never attack still clear vineyards

**The find:** Napa and Sonoma vineyards hire falconers whose birds never attack the pest birds raiding the grapes — the falcon just has to fly past. Vintners report 40-90% drops in starling damage within weeks, because the prey birds' fear of the raptor's silhouette never wears off, unlike with propane cannons or scarecrows.
**The surprise:** The falcon doesn't catch or kill a single bird to work. Just showing up is the deterrent, and it never stops working because the fear is instinctual, not learned.
**The idea:** [Cross-domain transfer](../../../concepts/cross-domain-transfer.md): a threat that triggers a hard-wired fear response can't be habituated to the way a merely loud or novel one can — real deterrence outlasts manufactured deterrence.
