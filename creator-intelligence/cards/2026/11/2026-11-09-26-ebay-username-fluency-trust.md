# Easy usernames make strangers look honest

**The find:** Researchers showed 608 people fictional eBay sellers with either easy-to-pronounce or hard-to-pronounce usernames. The easy-to-pronounce sellers were rated far more trustworthy — 5.18 vs 3.97 out of 10 — even when their displayed reputation scores were identical.
**The surprise:** Warning participants about the bias beforehand didn't fix it; they still couldn't out-reason a name's mouth-feel.
**The idea:** [Cognitive fluency](../../../concepts/cognitive-fluency.md): what's easy to process gets read as more trustworthy, even when nothing about the substance has changed.
