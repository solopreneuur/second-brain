# Sonos added friction to checkout on purpose

**The find:** Around 2022, Sonos abandoned the industry-standard single-page checkout and switched to a three-step process (billing, shipping, order) for its speakers — cart completion rose 24%, conversion rose 4%, and attach-rate on multi-product bundles rose 26%, because the extra steps gave shoppers "a sense of space and context" for a high-ticket purchase.
**The surprise:** Every other e-commerce team was ripping friction out of checkout at the same moment Sonos was deliberately putting it back in — and it won.
**The idea:** [Friction](../../../concepts/friction.md) isn't always the enemy of conversion — for a considered, expensive purchase, a little more of it can read as care rather than obstruction.
