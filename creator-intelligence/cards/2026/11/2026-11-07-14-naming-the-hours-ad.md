# Naming the hours beat naming the benefit

**The find:** Podcaster Phill Agnew ran two identical $100 Reddit ad sets for his show — one just listed the lessons inside, the other added "I've spent 480 minutes listening to marketing experts." The version naming the hours got a 45% higher click-through rate.
**The surprise:** Admitting how long something took, instead of selling what you'd get out of it, won by a wide margin on a $100 test budget.
**The idea:** [Effort heuristic](../../../concepts/effort-heuristic.md): people value a thing more when they can see the labor behind it, and a specific number of minutes reads as more honest than a claimed benefit.
