# Southwest Ends Its Open Seating

**The find:** Southwest defended its open-seating, no-bag-fee, single-aircraft-type model for decades as the "maverick" identity fans loved — until activist investor Elliott built a $1.9B stake calling it outdated, and by January 2026 every seat was assigned and bags cost $35-45.
**The surprise:** Southwest's own internal research found 80% of its loyal customers actually wanted assigned seats — the egalitarian feature fans praised in theory was quietly costing the airline the fliers who avoided it in practice.
**The idea:** [Path dependency](../../../concepts/path-dependency.md): a defining differentiator can become a liability once customer preference shifts underneath it, and the outside pressure a company resents is sometimes what forces the overdue fix.
