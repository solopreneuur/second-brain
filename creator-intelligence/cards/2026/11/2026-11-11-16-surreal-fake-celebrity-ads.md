# Fake celebrities beat Kellogg's real one

**The find:** Cereal brand Surreal put up billboards quoting "celebrity" endorsements — except the names belonged to ordinary Brits who happened to share them, like a bus driver named Dwayne Johnson. Surreal's own lawyers flagged the idea as too risky to run.
**The surprise:** The joke ad, at a fraction of the budget, drew engagement from 13.3% of Surreal's much smaller following in four days — over 600 times the engagement rate Kellogg's got from an actual celebrity deal with Lizzo in the same period.
**The idea:** [Mock endorsement](../../../concepts/mock-endorsement.md) — a fake celebrity deal, played straight, can out-perform a real one bought at full price.
