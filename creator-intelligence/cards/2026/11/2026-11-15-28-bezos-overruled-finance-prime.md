# Bezos overruled his own CFO

**The find:** In 2005 Amazon's own finance team modeled unlimited two-day shipping for a flat $79 a year and found the numbers "horrifying"; Bezos overruled them and launched Prime anyway.
**The surprise:** The executive famous for demanding data overruled his own finance team's model and bet on changed customer behavior over the spreadsheet.
