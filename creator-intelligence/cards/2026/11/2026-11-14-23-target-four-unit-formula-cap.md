# Target capped formula at exactly four

**The find:** During the 2022 US baby formula shortage, Target capped online purchases at exactly 4 units per customer while leaving in-store purchases at the very same stores unlimited; CVS and Walgreens separately capped at 3.
**The surprise:** Same retailer, same product, same real shortage — but only the channel with a posted number gave shoppers a target to anchor toward, turning a rationing notice into a suggested "correct" quantity to buy up to.
**The idea:** A stated limit doesn't just ration demand, it tells people how much a reasonable person would want — remove the visible number and the anchor disappears even though the scarcity is identical ([scarcity anchor](../../../concepts/scarcity-anchor.md)).
