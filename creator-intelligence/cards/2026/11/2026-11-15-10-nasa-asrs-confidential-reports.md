# NASA runs aviation's confession line

**The find:** After a 1974 TWA crash revealed pilots had already reported the exact hazard internally with no one sharing it industry-wide, the FAA funded a confidential pilot-error reporting system — then handed it to NASA to run, specifically because NASA has no power to punish anyone.
**The surprise:** The regulator paid for a safety-reporting system and then deliberately gave it to a toothless agency, betting that removing its own power to punish was the only way to get pilots to tell the truth.
**The idea:** [Psychological safety](../../../concepts/psychological-safety.md): people only report their own mistakes honestly when doing so carries no personal cost.
