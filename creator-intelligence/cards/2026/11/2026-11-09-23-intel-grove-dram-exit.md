# Intel's CEO couldn't say the words

**The find:** Intel's 1986 annual report has CEO Andy Grove admitting that when deciding to exit the DRAM memory-chip business Intel itself had invented, "I had a hard time getting the words out of my mouth without equivocation" — the same year Intel lost $173 million, cut 7,200 jobs, and closed eight plants.
**The surprise:** The company now famous for ruthless strategic pivots documented, in writing to its own shareholders, its CEO's paralysis before making the call that saved it.
**The idea:** [Radical honesty](../../../concepts/radical-honesty.md): naming your own hesitation, not just the decision, as part of the record.
