# The 1917 patent behind impulse buying

**The find:** When Clarence Saunders opened the first self-service Piggly Wiggly in Memphis in 1916, the entry turnstile and maze layout weren't crowd control — his 1917 patent explicitly required the single-file path to force every shopper past every item in the store before reaching checkout.
**The surprise:** The patent text literally uses the word "required" — the forced-browsing path every supermarket still uses was legally codified as the mechanism of the store, not a marketing afterthought added later.
**The idea:** Choice architecture via forced exposure — constrain the path of movement to guarantee what people see, the ancestor of every "walk past the bakery to reach the milk" layout.
