# Hasbro spent two decades selling pencils

**The find:** The Hassenfeld brothers founded their company in 1923 selling textile remnants. When their own pencil-box supplier started competing with them, they began manufacturing pencils themselves — and didn't become primarily a toy company until 1942.
**The surprise:** One of the world's biggest toy companies spent its first two decades as a pencil and school-supply manufacturer, pushed into toys defensively rather than by design.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): companies often back into their eventual identity through a chain of defensive pivots against suppliers and competitors, not one visionary leap.
