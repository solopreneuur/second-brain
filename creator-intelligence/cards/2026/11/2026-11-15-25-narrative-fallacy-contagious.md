# Fabricated stories mislead the next person too

**The find:** NBER researchers showed subjects purely random, non-causal data, then had them write "advice" for future participants; many invented confident causal stories explaining the noise, and those fabricated narratives then measurably distorted the decisions of the next person who read them.
**The surprise:** The narrative fallacy isn't just self-deception — it's contagious. A made-up story about meaningless noise actively misleads a stranger who reads it later.
**The idea:** The [narrative fallacy](../../../concepts/narrative-fallacy.md): inventing a confident causal story for what is actually noise.
