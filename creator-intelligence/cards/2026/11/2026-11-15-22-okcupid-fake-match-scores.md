# OkCupid lied about your match

**The find:** In 2014 OkCupid data scientist Christian Rudder published results of an experiment where the site told some genuinely poor matches, actually 30% compatible, that they were a 90% match, just to see if belief alone would make strangers message each other.
**The surprise:** Telling people they were compatible made them act compatible — people like each other partly, Rudder concluded, "because they think they're supposed to."
**The idea:** The [expectancy effect](../../../concepts/expectancy-effect.md): believing something is true changes your behavior enough to help make it true.
