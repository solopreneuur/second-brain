# Oatly sold shirts mocking its ad

**The find:** Oatly's 2021 Super Bowl ad — its CEO singing an awkward jingle alone in a field — was widely mocked online as one of the worst commercials of the night. Oatly leaned into the backlash, selling "I Totally Hated That Oatly Commercial" T-shirts that sold out in under 24 hours.
**The surprise:** The brand turned its own worst reviews into a sold-out merchandise line within a day of the broadcast.
**The idea:** Public mockery is free distribution if the brand owns the joke before critics finish telling it ([Anti-branding](../../../concepts/anti-branding.md)).
