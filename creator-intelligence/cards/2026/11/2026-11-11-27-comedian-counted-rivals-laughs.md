# A comedian counted his rivals' laughs

**The find:** Katt Williams, a comedian with three decades in stand-up and 14 specials, says that as a young comic he wrote down the names of 300 working comedians ahead of him and literally counted the laughs each one got per 15-minute set — a baseline of nine — and wouldn't cross a name off his list until his own set beat theirs.
**The surprise:** He didn't treat comedy as a talent he had or didn't have — he treated it as a measurable number, laughs per minute, tracked rival by rival until he beat each one.
**The idea:** [Quantified deliberate practice](../../../concepts/quantified-deliberate-practice.md) — turning a subjective craft into a countable metric against named competitors, not just "getting better," is its own specific strategy.
