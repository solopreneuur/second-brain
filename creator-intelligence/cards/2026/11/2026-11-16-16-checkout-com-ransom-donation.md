# A hacked company said sorry and donated the ransom

**The find:** When the ShinyHunters hacking group stole data from an old, forgotten cloud system in November 2025, payments processor Checkout.com's public response read: "This was our mistake, and we take full responsibility... We are sorry." Instead of paying the ransom, it donated more than $1 million — the equivalent sum — to cybersecurity research at Carnegie Mellon and Oxford.
**The surprise:** Companies almost never say "sorry" after a breach, they say "unauthorized access was detected" — and this one turned the ransom demand into a donation instead of a payout to criminals.
**The idea:** [Radical honesty](../../../concepts/radical-honesty.md): the plain admission costs less reputational ground than the careful non-admission everyone expects.
