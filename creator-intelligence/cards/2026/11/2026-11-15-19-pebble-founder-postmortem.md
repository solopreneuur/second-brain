# Pebble's founder explains its death

**The find:** Pebble founder Eric Migicovsky's own postmortem says the smartwatch pioneer doubled spending in 2015 while revenue grew only 35%, missed its $100 million sales target by $18 million, and was left holding $15 million of unsold inventory before the fire-sale to Fitbit.
**The surprise:** His diagnosis isn't "we ran out of money" — it's that Pebble abandoned the product hackers clearly wanted for an "ill-defined" fitness device nobody had asked for.
