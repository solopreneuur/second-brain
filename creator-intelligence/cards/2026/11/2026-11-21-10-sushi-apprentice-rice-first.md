# Three Years of Rice First

**The find:** Traditional sushi apprenticeship follows a rule of "rice for three years, nigiri for eight" — novices spend years mastering only rice temperature, seasoning and texture before they're allowed to touch fish.
**The surprise:** The part every customer actually sees and photographs is taught last; years go into the invisible layer everything else sits on top of.
**The idea:** [Constraint-skill-transfer](../../../concepts/constraint-skill-transfer.md) — gate the unglamorous foundation first, since flaws there can't be covered by polish on top.
