# Flat-pack began with a table that wouldn't fit

**The find:** IKEA's fourth employee, designer Gillis Lundgren, couldn't fit a table into his car after a 1950s catalog photo shoot. His fix was to unscrew the legs and lay them under the tabletop — then he told founder Ingvar Kamprad: "I think we should unscrew the legs and put them under the table."
**The surprise:** Flat-pack self-assembly, the single most defining trait of IKEA's brand, wasn't a strategy about customer engagement, it was one designer solving a trunk-space problem on the drive home — and it let IKEA ship roughly ten times more furniture per truck.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): logistics created the behavior customers now call distinctive; the psychology explaining why they love it came later.
