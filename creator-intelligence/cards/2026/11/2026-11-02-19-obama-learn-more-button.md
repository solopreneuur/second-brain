# "Learn More" beat "Sign Up" by 40%

**The find:** The 2008 Obama campaign's analytics director ran a 24-combination test on its splash page. Staff favoured a video of Obama speaking; it was the worst performer. The winner was a plain family photo with a "Learn More" button, beating the original by 40.6%, worth about $60 million in extra donations.
**The surprise:** The highest-converting call to action asked for the least, and the team's confident creative instincts actively hurt conversion.
**The idea:** [Micro-commitment](../../../concepts/micro-commitment.md): a low-stakes ask often outperforms a direct one because people need an easy on-ramp, not a bigger push.
