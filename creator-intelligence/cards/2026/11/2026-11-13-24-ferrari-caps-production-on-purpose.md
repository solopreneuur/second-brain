# Ferrari's revenue grows while its car count barely does

**The find:** Ferrari shipped 13,752 cars in 2024, up just 0.7% year over year, while revenue rose 11.8% to €6.68 billion — growth that came almost entirely from pricing and mix, not from building more cars. Ferrari also cut China shipments by 328 units on purpose to protect scarcity, and its order book runs through 2027.
**The surprise:** A public company kept telling investors, in effect, that it is refusing the obvious lever (sell more cars) because selling more cars would make the product worth less.
**The idea:** Deliberately refusing to scale supply can be the thing that makes the price — and the brand — go up ([Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md)).
