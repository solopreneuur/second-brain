# One sentence collected 2.7 million pounds

**The find:** The UK's Behavioural Insights Team worked with Medway Council to add one line to council-tax reminder letters: "96% of Medway Council Tax is paid on time." Payment rates on the new letters rose 19% relative to the old version, projected to bring forward about £2.7 million a year.
**The surprise:** It wasn't a bigger threat or a bigger fine that moved non-payers, it was being told that almost everyone else already pays — reframing them from "being chased" to "the exception."
**The idea:** [Descriptive norm letter](../../../concepts/descriptive-norm-letter.md): naming the compliant majority can outperform escalating the threat to the holdouts.
