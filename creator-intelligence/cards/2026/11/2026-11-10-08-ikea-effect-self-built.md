# Self-built IKEA boxes sell higher

**The find:** People who assembled a plain IKEA storage box themselves bid 63% more for it than people who got the identical, already-built box.
**The surprise:** The box is physically identical either way — the only difference is who snapped it together.
**The idea:** The [IKEA effect](../../../concepts/ikea-effect.md) — labor on a thing makes you value it more, independent of its actual quality.
