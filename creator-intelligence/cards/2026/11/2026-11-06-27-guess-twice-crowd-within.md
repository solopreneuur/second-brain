# Asking yourself twice beats asking once

**The find:** Researchers found that asking yourself the same estimation question twice, three weeks apart, and averaging your two answers reduces error — capturing roughly a third of the benefit of a second independent person's opinion, versus almost none if you re-guess immediately.
**The surprise:** "Wisdom of crowds" doesn't require other people — waiting and asking yourself again works too.
**The idea:** The crowd-within effect: a single mind can simulate part of a crowd's accuracy if it waits and asks itself again.
