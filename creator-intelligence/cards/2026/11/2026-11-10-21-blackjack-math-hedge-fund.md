# Blackjack math built a hedge fund

**The find:** Ed Thorp used an IBM 704 to work out the math behind card counting, publishing it in 1962's "Beat the Dealer," then applied the same statistical-edge logic to markets, co-founding Princeton Newport Partners in 1969 — arguably the first market-neutral hedge fund.
**The surprise:** The same arithmetic that tilted blackjack odds by a few percent became a 19-year, near-unbroken winning streak on Wall Street, with only three losing months.
**The idea:** [Cross-domain transfer](../../../concepts/cross-domain-transfer.md) — a statistical edge doesn't care whether the table has cards or tickers.
