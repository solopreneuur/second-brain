# Avon was the free sample, not the book

**The find:** Avon's founder David McConnell was a door-to-door book salesman in the 1880s who gave female customers a free vial of homemade rose perfume to get them to open the door. When he noticed women cared more about the perfume than the books, he dropped the books and built a company around the free sample.
**The surprise:** The company that dominated door-to-door cosmetics for most of the 20th century began as a bribe to get a different product read.
**The idea:** The thing you give away to sell your real product can turn out to be the real product, another [accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md).
