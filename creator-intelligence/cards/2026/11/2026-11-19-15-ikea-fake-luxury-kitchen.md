# IKEA invented a fake rival to mock

**The find:** IKEA ran ads posing as a fictional, snobbish luxury kitchen brand called "Eureka Spiritsis," complete with an etiquette coach pitching pretentious jargon about kitchen design, before revealing at the end that it was IKEA all along.
**The surprise:** A budget retailer spent its media budget making fun of an entire category of marketing language — the premium-kitchen industry's — instead of talking about its own products at all.
**The idea:** You don't have to defend your price point if you can make the whole category's positioning look ridiculous first.
