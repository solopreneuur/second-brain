# Superhuman grew on purpose at 100 users a week

**The find:** Superhuman CEO Rahul Vohra capped new signups at 100 a week and required a mandatory 30-minute onboarding call with every single new user, while a waitlist swelled past 60,000 people. Critics called it leaving money on the table for a VC-backed startup under pressure to show growth.
**The surprise:** The usual "wrong decision" is pushing growth too hard; here the criticized decision was refusing to grow faster, and the throttle itself is what built the product's reputation.
**The idea:** Deliberately refusing to scale a specific part of the business, even under investor pressure to show bigger numbers, can be the decision that protects the thing making the product valuable in the first place ([Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md)).
