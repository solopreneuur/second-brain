# Zippo trademarked its own click

**The find:** Zippo's signature "click" is a byproduct of the precise metal-hinge tolerance needed for a windproof seal. In 2018, Zippo legally secured a sound trademark on that incidental noise, citing more than 2,000 film appearances as proof of brand recognition.
**The surprise:** An engineering side-effect nobody designed on purpose became protected intellectual property, on par with a logo.
**The idea:** An unplanned byproduct of how something is built can become a defensible, ownable brand asset once enough people start recognizing it on its own ([Byproduct as brand asset](../../../concepts/byproduct-as-brand-asset.md)).
