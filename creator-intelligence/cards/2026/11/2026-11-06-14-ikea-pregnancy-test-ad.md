# IKEA's ad was a pregnancy test

**The find:** IKEA Sweden's 2018 magazine ad for a crib doubled as a working pregnancy test — a positive result on the embedded strip revealed a code that halved the crib's price.
**The surprise:** The ad only worked, and only unlocked its own discount, if you urinated on it.
**The idea:** A functional ad: the ad medium performs the actual qualifying action, not just the message.
