# The milk carton was a self-refreshing billboard

**The find:** In 1984, a Des Moines dairy put missing paperboys Johnny Gosch and Eugene Martin on milk cartons because cartons sat on breakfast tables and got physically replaced every few days — a free, self-refreshing ad unit. The idea scaled to more than 700 dairies and up to 5 billion cartons nationwide within a year. Of roughly 200 children ever featured, only two were ever found alive; neither boy was.
**The surprise:** The medium's genius — constant forced refresh at the breakfast table — and its near-total failure at its one stated job sit side by side. It became one of the most recognized PSAs in American history mostly by failing at the thing it was built to do.
**The idea:** [Functional ad](../../../concepts/functional-ad.md): a distribution mechanism engineered for maximum reach and recall can succeed completely as an awareness vehicle while nearly failing as a results vehicle, and the two get conflated in the retelling.
