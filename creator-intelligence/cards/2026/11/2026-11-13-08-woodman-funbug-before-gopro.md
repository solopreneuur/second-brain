# GoPro's founder lost $3.9M before GoPro

**The find:** Before GoPro, Nick Woodman burned through $3.9 million in investor money, including $235,000 from his own father, on FunBug, a Java-games-with-cash-prizes platform that collapsed in the 2001 dot-com crash before it properly launched.
**The surprise:** Woodman has said the fear of repeating that exact failure — not confidence — is what drove his obsessive work ethic on GoPro.
**The idea:** A specific, humiliating failure can become the engine of the next venture, not by teaching a lesson but by installing a fear that won't let up.
