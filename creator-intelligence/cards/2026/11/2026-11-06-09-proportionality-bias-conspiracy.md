# Why dying presidents fuel conspiracies

**The find:** Researchers told people identical stories of a presidential assassination attempt, varying only the outcome: when the president died, people endorsed conspiracy explanations far more than when the same attack failed and he lived.
**The surprise:** We don't judge conspiracy claims mainly on evidence — we judge them on whether the outcome feels big enough to deserve a big cause.
**The idea:** Proportionality bias: a big effect pulls us toward assuming a big, deliberate cause behind it.
