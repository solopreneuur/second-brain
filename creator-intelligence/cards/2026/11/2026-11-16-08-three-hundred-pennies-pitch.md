# "300 pennies" sold twice as many cards

**The find:** In a 1999 door-to-door charity sale, sellers who said a set of note cards cost "300 pennies" instead of "$3.00," then immediately added "that's a bargain," sold to 80% of households — double the 40% rate of sellers who just said "$3.00, that's a bargain."
**The surprise:** The price was identical; restating it in a stranger unit nearly doubled sales on its own.
**The idea:** [Disrupt-then-reframe](../../../concepts/disrupt-then-reframe.md): a moment of confusion breaks a person's automatic "no," and whatever reframe follows immediately fills the gap.
