# One checkbox flip raised giving eightfold

**The find:** Home Retail Group's payroll-giving plan, which automatically raises a worker's donation by 3% a year, sat at 10% opt-in uptake. The Behavioural Insights Team switched new donors onto it by default, letting them opt out instead of opt in. Uptake jumped from 6% to 49% among new donors — eightfold.
**The surprise:** The giving plan itself never changed. Only who had to tick a box did, and that alone multiplied participation by eight.
**The idea:** [Default effect](../../../concepts/default-effect.md): the size of a default's pull isn't fixed — it scales with how little anyone cares enough to actively override it.
