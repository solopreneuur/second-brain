# The toothpaste tube's secret eye

**The find:** The small colored square at the sealed end of a toothpaste tube has nothing to do with ingredients, despite the long-running myth that it codes "natural" versus "chemical" — it's an "eye mark" printed so a light sensor on the packing line knows exactly where to fold and seal the tube.
**The surprise:** Millions of people have a confident, specific theory about what the mark means, and it was never meant for a customer to see at all.
**The idea:** [Accidental meaning](../../../concepts/accidental-meaning.md) — an object can carry a clear "meaning" the public invented, while its real purpose was only ever mechanical.
