# KFC sold more chips by limiting them

**The find:** KFC Australia and Ogilvy tested roughly 90 different ad lines for a $1 chips deal on Facebook. The line that won wasn't a better price or a better photo — it was a restriction: "maximum 4 packets per transaction." It lifted total chip sales 56%, with sales of exactly 4 packets up 84%.
**The surprise:** Telling customers they could only buy a little made them buy more of it.
**The idea:** [Scarcity anchor](../../../concepts/scarcity-anchor.md) — a stated purchase limit doesn't just ration demand, it signals how much a reasonable person would want and anchors people toward that number.
