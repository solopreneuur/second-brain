# Dating apps crushed women's selectivity

**The find:** Psychologist Paul Eastwick's speed-dating research finds that in person, men accept about 50% of matches and women about 35%, but on dating apps men's acceptance rate holds near 50% while women's collapses to around 5%; the same research found machine-learning models trained on rich self-reported traits and preferences could not predict which specific pairs would click.
**The surprise:** The medium, not the people, is what's warping selectivity, and compatibility between two specific people turns out to be unpredictable even from detailed data on each of them alone.
**The idea:** Chemistry is generated in the interaction, not contained in either person's individual traits — a limit on what any profile, survey or algorithm can predict about fit.
