# Two Words Beside the Button

**The find:** Professional-network Viadeo added "It's free!" next to its "Sign up" button — no redesign, no new button.
**The surprise:** Conversion rose from 14.5% to 18.6%, a 28% relative lift, from two words that killed an unstated fear about a hidden catch.
**The idea:** [Friction](../../../concepts/friction.md) isn't always visual — naming the price, even when it's zero, removes an ambiguity tax before it forms.
