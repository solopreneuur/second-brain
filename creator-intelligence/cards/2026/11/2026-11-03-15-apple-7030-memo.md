# Apple's own cut-it-later memo

**The find:** A 2011 internal Apple email, surfaced in the Epic v. Apple trial, shows marketing chief Phil Schiller telling Steve Jobs and Eddy Cue the App Store's 70/30 revenue split "will not last forever," and floating cutting it to 80/20 once App Store profit hit $1B a year — a full decade before any public pressure forced Apple's hand.
**The surprise:** The executive publicly defending the 30% cut as fair was privately scripting exactly when and how to lower it, timed purely to look like strength rather than concession.
**The idea:** Banking a concession internally for years, then releasing it only when it reads as strength rather than surrender.
