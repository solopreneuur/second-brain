# Menus drop the dollar sign on purpose

**The find:** A Cornell study found diners spent $5.55 more on average at restaurants that printed "32" instead of "$32.00" or "thirty-two dollars" on the menu.
**The surprise:** Removing a single punctuation mark, not changing any price, measurably changed how much people spent.
**The idea:** [Pain of paying](../../../concepts/pain-of-paying.md): a price's emotional cost depends on how explicitly it's dressed as money, not on the number itself.
