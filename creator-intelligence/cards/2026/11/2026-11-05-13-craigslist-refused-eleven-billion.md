# Craigslist turned down $11 billion

**The find:** For two decades, Craig Newmark refused banner ads, SEO plays and most monetization on Craigslist, even as tech press and bankers argued he was sitting on a near-monopoly worth roughly $11 billion if properly monetized.
**The surprise:** The "irrational" refusal to cash in kept Craigslist profitable, dominant and essentially un-displaced on a skeleton staff for over 20 years — durability through restraint rather than growth.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md) — deliberately capping monetization can function as a moat rather than a mistake.
