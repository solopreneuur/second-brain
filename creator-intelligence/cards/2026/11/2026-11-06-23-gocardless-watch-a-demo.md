# One word doubled GoCardless demos

**The find:** In 2014, UK fintech GoCardless changed its homepage button from "Request a demo" to "Watch a demo" — swapping a scheduled sales call for an instant recorded video. Conversions rose 139%.
**The surprise:** "Request" quietly worked as a commitment trigger, promising a future sales call; "Watch" promised zero-commitment instant access.
**The idea:** [Micro-commitment](../../../concepts/micro-commitment.md): the perceived size of the ask, not just the offer itself, decides whether people act.
