# One word lifted sales 90%

**The find:** Supplement brand Movexa A/B tested its landing-page headline "Natural Joint Relief" against the identical headline with one word appended: "Natural Joint Relief Supplement."
**The surprise:** Adding the single word "Supplement" lifted purchases by nearly 90%, significant at 98% confidence.
**The idea:** Visitors need the product category named explicitly or they bounce before reading far enough to understand what they're even looking at ([Category disambiguation](../../../concepts/category-disambiguation.md)).
