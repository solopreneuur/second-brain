# Domino's paid cities to fix potholes

**The find:** In 2018 Domino's "Paving for Pizza" offered cities $5,000 grants to fill potholes, stencilling its logo onto the repairs, because jostled delivery cars were ruining pizzas. It expected 20 cities; it got 137,000 nominations from 15,275 zip codes and expanded to all 50 states.
**The surprise:** A pizza chain became a literal infrastructure funder, and pothole repair turned out to be a better emotional hook than any pizza ad.
**The idea:** [Reciprocity](../../../concepts/reciprocity.md): solve a real, unglamorous problem adjacent to your product, and the goodwill dwarfs direct advertising.
