# RealPage coached landlords on "stretch"

**The find:** The Department of Justice's antitrust complaint against rental-pricing software maker RealPage quotes the company telling a landlord client that by pooling nonpublic competitor rate and occupancy data, "we may have a $50 increase instead of a $10 increase for that day" — language RealPage itself called "stretch and pull pricing."
**The surprise:** The software wasn't just forecasting what the market would bear — it was explicitly coaching landlords on how much further above market they could push rent before a tenant walked.
**The idea:** [Algorithmic price coordination](../../../concepts/algorithmic-price-coordination.md): pooling supposedly-independent competitors' private data into one shared pricing engine can recreate the effect of a cartel without anyone picking up a phone.
