# The bank that bragged about boring

**The find:** A year after the SVB and Signature Bank collapses, PNC Bank launched "Brilliantly Boring," a 2024 campaign built entirely on calling its own banking boring, including a spot where a Hollywood-style actor raves about how thrillingly dull PNC is.
**The surprise:** Leaning into the industry's biggest insult, right when "boring" should have been toxic, won PNC an Effie Gold and its highest-ever single-month new-account growth.
**The idea:** none
