# One error prompt halved mistakes

**The find:** Australia's Behavioural Economics Team redesigned a property-lien registration form's ID step with clearer layout and five real-time custom error prompts. Incorrect registrations fell from 22.3% to 12.8% — while a separate "confirm your details" review screen tested in parallel did almost nothing.
**The surprise:** The nudge that looks like it should help (a confirmation screen) does nothing; the unglamorous fix (specific error prompts) nearly halves the mistake rate.
**The idea:** [Friction](../../../concepts/friction.md): removing friction at the exact point of error beats a general-purpose nudge.
