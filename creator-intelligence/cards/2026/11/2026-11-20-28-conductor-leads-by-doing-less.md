# The conductor who leads by doing less

**The find:** Conductor Itay Talgam's analysis of 20th-century maestros like Bernstein, Karajan, and Kleiber shows the best conductors lead not by issuing commands but by creating conditions where musicians' own interpretive judgment produces the music — a model Talgam now teaches directly to corporate leaders.
**The surprise:** The conductor who waves the baton least and visibly does the least work often gets the richest, most unified performance, because he's delegated interpretation itself, not just execution of it.
**The idea:** [Decentralised control](../../../concepts/decentralised-control.md) — authority that creates space for others' expertise can outperform authority that directs every note.
