# Listerine was once a floor cleaner

**The find:** Listerine launched in the 1880s as a surgical antiseptic and spent decades being marketed as a floor cleaner, a foot soak, and a gonorrhea treatment before Lambert Pharmaceutical repositioned it as a mouthwash around the 1910s–1920s.
**The surprise:** Today's bathroom-cabinet staple spent its early career being pitched as a cure for a venereal disease and a way to mop floors.
**The idea:** The same chemical formula can be marketed into several unrelated categories before the one that finally sticks — the product doesn't change, the story around it does ([Category repositioning](../../../concepts/category-repositioning.md)).
