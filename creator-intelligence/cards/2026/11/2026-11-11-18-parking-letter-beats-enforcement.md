# One sentence beat years of fine threats

**The find:** Louisville, Kentucky added one true sentence to a parking-fine collection letter — "the majority of drivers who receive a parking fine in Louisville pay it within 13 days" — plus a "Pay Now" stamp. Payment likelihood more than doubled off an 8.1% base.
**The surprise:** A single statistic about what other people do outperformed years of standard enforcement language, with no new penalty and no new threat.
**The idea:** [Descriptive norm letter](../../../concepts/descriptive-norm-letter.md) — telling people what most people already do is often a stronger lever than telling them what they're supposed to do.
