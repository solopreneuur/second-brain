# Tesla fixed a blind-bolting problem overnight

**The find:** Jon McNeill, Tesla's former president, recalls on The Knowledge Project that workers assembling the Model X's Falcon Wing doors had to drill bolts in blind, reaching underneath the chassis with no way to see what they were doing. He asked Elon Musk if a guide jig had ever been considered. Musk's answer: "No, I haven't thought about that. Let's try that." The jig was built and in use by the next day.
**The surprise:** Workers had been blind-bolting a critical step for weeks with nobody flagging it, and the moment an outsider named the obvious fix, it was built and deployed before the next shift started.
