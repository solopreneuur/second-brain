# Everyone looks better in a group photo

**The find:** The same face is reliably rated more attractive when shown in a group photo than when shown alone, confirmed across five experiments.
**The surprise:** Your brain automatically averages a group into one composite "mean face," and since average faces read as more attractive, everyone in the photo borrows a little of that borrowed appeal.
**The idea:** The [cheerleader effect](../../../concepts/cheerleader-effect.md), named for a joke in a sitcom, is a documented bias in how the visual system encodes groups rather than individuals.
