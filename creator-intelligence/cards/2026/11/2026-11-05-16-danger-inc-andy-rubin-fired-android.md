# Fired from the smartphone he built

**The find:** Andy Rubin co-founded Danger Inc. in 1999 and built the T-Mobile Sidekick, an app-capable, push-data smartphone years before the iPhone, but was pushed out by Danger's own board in 2003.
**The surprise:** Rubin immediately founded Android Inc., which Google acquired in 2005 and turned into the dominant smartphone OS, while Danger itself sold to Microsoft in 2008 for roughly $500 million and died as the short-lived Kin — meaning the "loser" company's ousted founder built the category's eventual winner.
**The idea:** Getting fired from the right idea at the wrong company can be the best thing that happens to a founder.
