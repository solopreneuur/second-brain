# One header line moved a country's tax rate

**The find:** The Danish Tax Agency changed the header of a single reminder email, sent to roughly 119,000 companies, from a neutral "Remember to report tax before July 1st" to a loss-framed "Remember to report tax on time, to avoid a tax surcharge of up to 5,000 kr."
**The surprise:** On-time filing jumped from 65.3% to 74% year over year — nearly nine percentage points, from rewriting one line, with zero new enforcement or spending.
**The idea:** [Loss aversion](../../../concepts/loss-aversion.md) — people respond more strongly to the threat of a concrete loss than to a neutral factual deadline.
