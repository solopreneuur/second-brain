# A small-city doctor invented the childproof cap

**The find:** Dr. Henri Breault, a pediatrician in Windsor, Ontario, was treating roughly 1,000 child poisoning cases a year from household medicine bottles. In 1967 he partnered with a packaging company to invent the "Palm N' Turn" cap — a lid that only opens with a simultaneous push and twist, a motion a toddler's hands can't easily produce.
**The surprise:** It wasn't a lab engineer or a regulator who solved it, it was one local doctor who tracked the cases himself and built the first lid — and Windsor's hospital poisoning admissions dropped 91% within a decade, well before such caps became mandatory.
**The idea:** [Friction](../../../concepts/friction.md): the right kind of friction doesn't lecture anyone, it inserts a small physical obstacle calibrated to the exact capability gap you're trying to exploit.
