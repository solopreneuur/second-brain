# Hardee's quietly rewrote its own manual

**The find:** A 25-year Hardee's franchisee running 76 restaurants sued after Hardee's allegedly began charging new monthly fees and mandatory royalties never written into the signed franchise agreement, by quietly amending its internal "operations manual" instead of renegotiating the contract.
**The surprise:** The real lever of power wasn't the signed contract — it was a manual the franchisor could edit alone, adding new financial obligations without ever reopening negotiations.
**The idea:** Shadow contract — whoever controls the editable manual behind a formal contract can change the real deal without ever technically breaking it.
