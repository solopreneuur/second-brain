# A $199,000 house sells for more

**The find:** A study of more than 370,000 Virginia home listings found that "just below" pricing — $199,000 instead of $200,000 — let sellers set a higher starting ask without scaring off buyers. Those homes closed 2.5-3% higher than identical homes listed at the round number.
**The surprise:** It's the same house either way. The single leftmost digit on the listing moved the final sale price by thousands of dollars, even though the round-priced homes sold faster.
**The idea:** [Anchoring](../../../concepts/anchoring.md) at work on the biggest single purchase most people ever make: the first number a buyer sees recalibrates what the whole house feels worth, before anyone has seen a room.
