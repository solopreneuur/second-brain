# Barbie's company started by selling picture frames

**The find:** Mattel started in 1945 as a Los Angeles garage business making picture frames from scrap wood and plastic. Co-founder Elliot Handler began using leftover frame scraps to build dollhouse furniture, which outsold the frames outright — so Mattel dropped picture frames entirely to become a toy maker, 14 years before Barbie.
**The surprise:** The world's biggest doll and toy company's founding product line was literally made from its real business's factory waste.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): the byproduct of your actual business is sometimes worth testing as a business of its own.
