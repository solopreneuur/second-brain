# The speed bump that skips fire trucks

**The find:** Many split "speed cushions" have their gaps sized to about 1.85 meters, the wheel-track width of fire trucks and buses, so emergency vehicles straddle the gap and pass almost untouched while ordinary cars still get slowed down hard.
**The surprise:** The bump isn't randomly gapped — it's engineered to filter by vehicle width, quietly letting one class of vehicle through while still catching everyone else.
**The idea:** none
