# A label chairman swore Spotify would never be licensed

**The find:** Daniel Ek built Spotify on a free, ad-supported tier meant to pull users off piracy. In February 2010, Warner Music chairman Edgar Bronfman Jr. told analysts free streaming was "clearly not a net positive for the music industry" and that, as far as Warner was concerned, Spotify "will not be licensed."
**The surprise:** Warner didn't just license Spotify, it became a shareholder in it; by 2024 Spotify was paying the music industry more than $10 billion a year.
**The idea:** [Contrarian conviction](../../../concepts/contrarian-conviction.md): the free tier industry insiders call a giveaway is sometimes the acquisition funnel that kills the thing actually costing them money.
