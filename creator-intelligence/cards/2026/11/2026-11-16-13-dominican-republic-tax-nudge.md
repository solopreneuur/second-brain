# A 184 million dollar letter about prison time

**The find:** The Dominican Republic's tax authority mailed letters to over 56,000 firms and 28,000 self-employed workers reminding them that tax evasion could carry prison time. Recipients paid 44% more in taxes than a plain-reminder control group, and a separate disclosure-threat letter raised payments 18% — together pulling in $184 million in extra revenue from a free mailing.
**The surprise:** No new law, no new enforcement, no new audits — just making an existing, already-true consequence vivid moved more money than most actual enforcement budgets.
**The idea:** [Salience nudge](../../../concepts/salience-nudge.md): people don't under-respond to a known risk, they under-attend to it; making it vivid again is often cheaper than adding a new one.
