# The real reason behind wobbly cart wheels

**The find:** The popular story that stores deliberately make cart wheels wobbly to slow shoppers down is false. But a real 1968 patent (Elmer Isaacks) describes a magnet-triggered wheel lock meant to stop theft — the ancestor of today's buried-wire perimeter systems that freeze a cart's wheels if it's pushed past the lot line.
**The surprise:** The actual engineered "broken wheel" technology was built to catch thieves, not manipulate shoppers — while the manipulation story everyone believes turns out to be closer to myth than mechanism.
**The idea:** Embedding the enforcement in the product itself lets a store self-police without adding staff.
