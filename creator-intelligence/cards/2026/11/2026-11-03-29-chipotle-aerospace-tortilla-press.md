# Chipotle's tortilla warmer came from aerospace

**The find:** Chipotle founder Steve Ells hired an industrial engineering firm whose normal business is building machinery to lift jet engines off aircraft for maintenance, to spend over two years custom-engineering Chipotle's tortilla press and warmer — because off-the-shelf commercial presses couldn't heat evenly enough to make the tortilla puff with steam, which is what keeps it from tearing when a burrito is wrapped.
**The surprise:** The ten-second warming step before every burrito is wrapped — a detail almost no customer registers — got the same caliber of engineering as aircraft maintenance equipment.
**The idea:** [Quality signalling](../../../concepts/quality-signalling.md) can be invisible — the engineering effort doesn't need to be seen to be the thing that makes the product feel right.
