# "A team of agents" beats "an agent"

**The find:** Irrational Labs showed 1,014 software buyers identical AI product mockups, varying only the label: "an agent" vs. "a team of agents." The team framing raised purchase intent by 6.3 percentage points, even though it tested as harder to understand.
**The surprise:** Splitting one thing into several named parts made buyers want it more despite also finding it more confusing — clarity lost, desire won.
**The idea:** Breaking a single offering into labeled components makes it feel bigger and more valuable than the same thing presented whole ([the unpacking effect](../../../concepts/unpacking-effect.md)).
