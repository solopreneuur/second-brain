# Venmo's feed came from an admin email

**The find:** Venmo co-founder Iqram Magdon-Ismail has said the app's famous public payment feed wasn't planned as a social feature — it came from him reading the company's internal daily activity email, which listed every user's payments like a newspaper, and thinking: what if everyone could see a feed like this?
**The surprise:** The feature that turned a payments app into an accidental social network — and later a privacy controversy, with strangers' payments searchable by anyone — started with a founder being entertained by his own company's admin emails.
**The idea:** An [accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): internal tooling built to monitor a system can leak straight into the system itself as a user-facing feature.
