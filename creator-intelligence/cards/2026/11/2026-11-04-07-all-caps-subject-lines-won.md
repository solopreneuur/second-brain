# All-caps subject lines won the test

**The find:** Spotler split 8.2 million delivered marketing emails by whether the subject line opened in caps lock; the capitalized group opened at 18.7% versus 13.2% for lowercase.
**The surprise:** Nearly every copywriting rule says all-caps reads as spam, but on this dataset it was the better-performing choice by a wide margin.
**The idea:** A rule treated as settled can fail a real test, which argues for testing defaults instead of inheriting them.
