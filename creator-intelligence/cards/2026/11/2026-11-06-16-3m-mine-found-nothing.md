# 3M's mine never found its mineral

**The find:** Five men founded Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing in 1902 to mine corundum for grindstones. The deposit turned out to be worthless anorthosite — the mine failed outright — and investors bailed the company out by pivoting it to manufacture sandpaper from abrasives bought elsewhere.
**The surprise:** "Mining" is still in the company's name, even though its own mine never produced anything usable.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): the thing that saves the company is never the thing it set out to do.
