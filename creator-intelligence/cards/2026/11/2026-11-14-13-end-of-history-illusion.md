# You think you're done changing

**The find:** Across 19,000 people aged 18 to 68, everyone reported having changed a lot over the past decade in personality, values and preferences — then predicted almost no change over the next decade, at every age tested.
**The surprise:** A 68-year-old makes the same "I'm basically done changing" prediction a teenager does, and both are provably wrong based on their own past.
**The idea:** The end-of-history illusion — people treat their current self as the finished version, no matter how much evidence says otherwise ([end-of-history illusion](../../../concepts/end-of-history-illusion.md)).
