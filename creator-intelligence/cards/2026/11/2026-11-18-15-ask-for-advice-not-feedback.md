# Ask for advice, not feedback

**The find:** Researchers, including behavioural scientists from the UK's Behavioural Insights Team, ran a field experiment swapping a single word in requests for input on someone's work: "feedback" became "advice."
**The surprise:** Changing that one word measurably shifted reviewers toward giving more concrete, forward-looking, actionable suggestions instead of vague praise or vague criticism.
**The idea:** "Feedback" invites a verdict on the past; "advice" invites a plan for the future — the same request, reframed around a different tense, changes what people actually say ([Advice vs. feedback framing](../../../concepts/advice-vs-feedback-framing.md)).
