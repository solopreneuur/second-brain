# CEOs hide bad news by moving the meeting

**The find:** NYU researchers found that CEOs sitting on undisclosed bad news deliberately relocate the annual shareholder meeting far from headquarters to suppress attendance and dodge questions. Meetings held over 1,000 miles from HQ saw average abnormal stock returns of -3.7% over the following six months.
**The surprise:** It's a sophisticated, public, searchable signal — anyone could check where the meeting was held — and the market still failed to price it in at the time.
**The idea:** [Information avoidance](../../../concepts/information-avoidance.md) — sophistication itself can be the tell, since hiding bad news in plain sight is still a choice that leaves a trace.
