# Robinhood deleted its own confetti

**The find:** Robinhood's trade-completion confetti animation, long one of its signature touches, was deliberately pulled in 2021. Product director Madhu Muthukumar said it was "getting really misconstrued"; design head Rich Bessel said it could "take away from the text" that a user just traded.
**The surprise:** The company removed its own most-loved, most-recognized micro-interaction on purpose, because it worked too well at making trading feel like winning rather than informing.
**The idea:** A delight mechanic that successfully drives behavior can become a liability the moment regulators or your own team notice what behavior it's driving ([gamification liability](../../../concepts/gamification-liability.md)).
