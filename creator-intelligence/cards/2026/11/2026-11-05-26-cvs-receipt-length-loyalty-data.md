# CVS receipts measure your data

**The find:** A CVS receipt for one item is 10.5 inches without a loyalty card, but over 4 feet long if you scan your ExtraCare card for the identical purchase — the item list never grows, only the coupon and offer footer does.
**The surprise:** The receipt's length is a direct physical readout of how much the store's purchase-history database has on you.
**The idea:** Loyalty programs turn receipts into personalized marketing surfaces; since printing is nearly free, there's no cost pressure to ever stop.
