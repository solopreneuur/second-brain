# A Data Broker Became the Messenger

**The find:** In the Broiler Chicken antitrust case, producers including Tyson, Sanderson Farms and Pilgrim's Pride allegedly used a third-party benchmarking firm, Agri Stats, to signal and monitor supply cuts without emailing each other directly — one Tyson exec allegedly wrote asking if they could get Agri Stats "to say the things we want everyone to know." Tyson paid over $221.5M and Pilgrim's Pride $75M in settlements.
**The surprise:** The alleged cartel didn't need a smoking-gun agreement between rivals — they outsourced the coordination to a neutral-sounding data vendor who became the actual messenger of production cuts.
**The idea:** [Price leadership](../../../concepts/price-leadership.md): a shared "neutral" information intermediary can functionally replace a conspiracy while giving everyone legal deniability.
