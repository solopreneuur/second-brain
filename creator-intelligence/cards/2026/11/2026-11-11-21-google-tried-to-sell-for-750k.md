# Google tried to sell itself for $750,000

**The find:** In 1999, Larry Page and Sergey Brin tried to sell their search engine — soon to be named Google — to Excite CEO George Bell for roughly $750,000 cash plus about 1% of Excite stock. Bell turned it down.
**The surprise:** Bell says the deal didn't die over price — it died because Page demanded Excite rip out its own search technology entirely and run on Google's, a demand Bell now calls the worst call of his career to have refused.
**The idea:** [Refused to be a feature](../../../concepts/refused-to-be-a-feature.md) — founders sometimes reject a buyout specifically because the buyer wants to absorb the technology as a feature rather than let it be the whole product.
