# Oatly printed its own lawsuit on the carton

**The find:** In 2014 Sweden's dairy lobby sued Oatly over its slogan "It's like milk, but made for humans." Oatly lost the case in 2015, then published the entire legal complaint on its cartons and in Swedish newspapers. Sales rose sharply.
**The surprise:** The company that lost the lawsuit used its own defeat as the ad campaign, turning a courtroom loss into free, sustained coverage no ad budget could buy.
**The idea:** [Streisand effect](../../../concepts/streisand-effect.md): an attacker's attempt to suppress a message can amplify it instead.
