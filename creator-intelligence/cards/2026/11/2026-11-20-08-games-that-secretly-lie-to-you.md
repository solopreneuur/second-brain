# Games secretly lie to protect you

**The find:** Game developers collected hidden mechanics that quietly protect players without telling them: Far Cry 4 lowers enemy accuracy as more enemies cluster around you, Assassin's Creed and Doom weight your last sliver of health to survive hits the math says shouldn't land, and Arkham Asylum's guards are scripted to never fully turn around so they don't "notice" a player sneaking behind them.
**The surprise:** The moments that feel the most dangerous and unfair are often the ones most heavily rigged in the player's favor.
**The idea:** [Hidden-mechanic steering](../../../concepts/hidden-mechanic-steering.md) — a system can manufacture the feeling of risk or chaos while secretly protecting the person inside it from the real consequences.
