# One Verb Beat Another, Twice

**The find:** On MatchOffice.com, changing a button from "Order Information and Prices" to "Get Information and Prices" lifted conversions 14.79% on 14,230 visitors — then the same one-word swap, translated, produced a 38.26% lift on a Danish sister site.
**The surprise:** The exact same experiment, run twice in two languages, both times with a double-digit lift, from changing a single verb.
**The idea:** [Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md): "order" frames a cost (what you must do), while "get" frames a benefit (what you receive) — the fact doesn't change, but what it means to the reader does.
