# A learning scientist says grades aren't the point

**The find:** Dr. Mary Helen Immordino-Yang, a USC professor of education and psychology and director of USC's Center for Affective Neuroscience, Development, Learning and Education, tells Huberman: "Learning's not the goal. It's not the outcome. It needs to be the development of the person."
**The surprise:** An academic whose entire career lives inside the grading and credentialing system argues that the measurable "learning" schools chase isn't actually the point of education.
**The idea:** Optimizing for the metric (grades, test scores, "knowing") can crowd out the actual goal: a developing, curious person ([performance vs. development](../../../concepts/performance-vs-development.md)).
