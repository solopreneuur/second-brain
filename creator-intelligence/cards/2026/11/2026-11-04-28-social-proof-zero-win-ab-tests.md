# Social proof lost every single test

**The find:** A conversion-optimization shop ran three separate live A/B tests adding social proof (customer counts, comparison-page testimonials, star ratings); all three had a 0% win rate, and the testimonial test on a comparison page measurably hurt conversion.
**The surprise:** The most universally recommended CRO tactic lost, or actively backfired, in every single test this operator published.
**The idea:** [Social proof](../../../concepts/social-proof.md) only helps when it resolves real uncertainty; on a page where the visitor is already reasoning analytically, it just interrupts that process.
