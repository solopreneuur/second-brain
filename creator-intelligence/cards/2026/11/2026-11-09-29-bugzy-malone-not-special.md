# A grime star denies his own talent

**The find:** Bugzy Malone — the Manchester grime artist with five UK Top 10 albums and a MOBO Award for Best Grime Act — says that after years of thinking he was special for making it, he came to the opposite conclusion: "I guess I learned that the work paid off, the consistency paid off, the standing back up after getting knocked down paid off, but I'm nobody special."
**The surprise:** A platinum-charting rapper with a rags-to-riches backstory flatly denies having any special talent at all, crediting only repetition and resilience — the opposite of the "chosen one" narrative his own story invites.
**The idea:** No named concept — a credentialed success story, told by the person inside it, with the "specialness" removed on purpose.
