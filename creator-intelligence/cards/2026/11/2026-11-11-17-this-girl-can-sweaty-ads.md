# Fitness ads that showed sweat, not polish

**The find:** Sport England's 2015 "This Girl Can" campaign deliberately showed women sweating, wobbling, and imperfect while exercising — breaking fitness advertising's airbrushed convention on purpose.
**The surprise:** Removing the aspirational polish the entire category treats as essential is what worked: Sport England's own tracking found 2.8 million women aged 14-40 reported exercising more because of the campaign.
**The idea:** [Radical honesty](../../../concepts/radical-honesty.md) — showing the unflattering, ordinary version of an activity can motivate people a polished version never reached.
