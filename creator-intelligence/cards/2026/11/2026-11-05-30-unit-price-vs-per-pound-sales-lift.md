# Same price, new label, 90% more sales

**The find:** Across 16 studies including live grocery-store field tests, switching a price tag's displayed format — not the price — changed sales. For a half-pound item, switching to "per unit" pricing lifted sales 90%; for a two-pound item, switching to "per pound" pricing lifted sales 39%. The total cost never changed.
**The surprise:** A 90% sales swing came from relabeling a price tag, with the actual amount a shopper pays never moving.
**The idea:** [Anchoring](../../../concepts/anchoring.md) on the nominal number — shoppers fixate on the raw digit they see first as a value signal, even when it's mathematically meaningless once weight differs.
