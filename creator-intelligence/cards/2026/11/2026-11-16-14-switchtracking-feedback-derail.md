# The conversation that secretly splits in two

**The find:** Douglas Stone and Sheila Heen, authors of "Thanks for the Feedback," named "switchtracking" for the moment one person's reaction to a piece of feedback quietly swaps the topic: "you need to be on time" becomes "don't talk to me like that," and the two people end up arguing about different things while thinking it's the same fight.
**The surprise:** Neither person notices the subject changed — it just feels like the same conversation got heated, when the actual topic silently switched tracks.
**The idea:** [Switchtracking](../../../concepts/switchtracking.md): most unresolved arguments aren't disagreements, they're two separate conversations running on parallel tracks.
