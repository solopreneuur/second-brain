# Louis Vuitton blacklisted its best spenders

**The find:** Three Louis Vuitton customers who spent over $500,000 a year were secretly flagged internally as suspected "drug dealers" and banned from every LV store and the website, per a September 2024 federal lawsuit.
**The surprise:** The more someone spends, the more their buying pattern can look like money laundering to an internal fraud system built to protect the brand from itself.
**The idea:** Loyalty ceiling — anti-fraud systems built to catch outliers can misfire exactly on the customers a loyalty program is designed to reward, because an extreme-but-legitimate pattern looks the same as abuse.
