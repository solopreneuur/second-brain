# Pricing page lists the biggest number first

**The find:** A wine subscription service broke the universal cheap-to-expensive, left-to-right pricing convention, listing its highest price first on a pricing page — the opposite of nearly every pricing page on the internet.
**The surprise:** The reversed order, A/B tested against normal ascending order, lifted conversion rate by 23%.
**The idea:** [Anchoring](../../../concepts/anchoring.md) — the first number you see recalibrates how "cheap" every number after it feels.
