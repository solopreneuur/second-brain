# Nvidia's board nearly fired Huang

**The find:** In 2013, activist fund Starboard Value sent Nvidia's board a letter questioning the CUDA bet; Jensen Huang flew to Boston to beg Fidelity, Nvidia's largest shareholder, not to force him out. A board member called the meeting "brutal" after a decade of flat stock. Huang killed Nvidia's mobile-modem business to appease investors, while protecting CUDA.
**The surprise:** The near-ouster wasn't over a failed bet — it was over the one that became the AI boom.
