# Three words doubled a travel app's trial signups

**The find:** Travel-deals company Going tested its homepage button copy and found that changing "Sign up for free" to "Trial for free" lifted trial starts 104% month over month — nothing else about the offer, design, or price changed.
**The surprise:** "Sign up" and "trial" describe the exact same action, but one sounds like a commitment and the other sounds reversible, and that framing alone more than doubled the result.
**The idea:** The same action can feel binding or tentative depending purely on the word used to label it, and people respond to the label, not just the substance ([Commitment framing](../../../concepts/commitment-framing.md)).
