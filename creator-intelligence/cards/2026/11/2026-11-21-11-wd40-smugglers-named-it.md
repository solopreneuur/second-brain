# WD-40's First Fans Were Thieves

**The find:** San Diego's Rocket Chemical Co. formulated WD-40 in 1953 (their 40th attempt) purely as an anti-corrosion coating for Convair's Atlas missile skins.
**The surprise:** Convair workers started sneaking cans home for rusty tools and squeaky doors — that bootlegged household use, not the aerospace contract, is what the company renamed itself after in 1969.
**The idea:** [Emergent use](../../../concepts/emergent-use.md) — the product's real market can be discovered by the people stealing it, not the customer it was built for.
