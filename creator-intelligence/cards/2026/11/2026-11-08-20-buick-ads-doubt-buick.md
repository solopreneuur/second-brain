# Buick's Ads Doubted Buick

**The find:** Starting in 2014, Leo Burnett built Buick's national campaign around strangers refusing to believe a modern, good-looking car in front of them was really a Buick — making "nobody trusts our brand" the joke and the message for three-plus years in showrooms and on TV.
**The surprise:** US sales rose 11.4% in the launch year and Buick's average buyer age dropped three years, built on voicing the audience's worst assumption about the brand rather than countering it with feature lists.
**The idea:** [Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md): naming the audience's outdated assumption out loud disarms it faster than new evidence does, because the joke itself proves the brand knows it changed.
