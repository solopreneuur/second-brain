# The man who starved saving seeds

**The find:** Soviet geneticist Nikolai Vavilov built the world's first global seed bank to end famine, but was denounced by rival agronomist Trofim Lysenko, arrested by Stalin's police in 1940, and died of starvation in prison in 1943.
**The surprise:** The irony is exact — a scientist dedicated to ending hunger was killed by hunger, inside the very system his downfall helped starve.
**The idea:** none
