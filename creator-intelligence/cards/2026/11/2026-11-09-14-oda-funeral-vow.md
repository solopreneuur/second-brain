# Oda vowed to skip his parents' funeral

**The find:** *One Piece* creator Eiichiro Oda said at a 2018 awards ceremony that he had once vowed never to attend his own parents' funerals if it meant missing a manga deadline — then, in the same breath, called that vow "a misguided idea of what hard work means."
**The surprise:** The most disciplined man in manga publicly disavowed the exact discipline that made him famous, in front of the audience celebrating him for it.
**The idea:** A [self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md) taken to its logical extreme, and then renounced by the person who set it.
