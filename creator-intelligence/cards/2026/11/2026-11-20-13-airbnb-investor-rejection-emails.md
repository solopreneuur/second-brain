# Airbnb's rejections read fine in hindsight

**The find:** In 2008, seven prominent investors turned down Airbnb's seed round, writing back things like "not in our area of focus" and "we've not been able to get excited about travel related businesses." Airbnb later crossed a $20 billion-plus valuation.
**The surprise:** Fortune published the actual rejection emails years later, and the reasoning — travel is an unexciting category, wrong fund focus — reads as completely sound business logic in isolation, which is what makes the miss sting.
**The idea:** A [pattern-matching blindspot](../../../concepts/pattern-matching-blindspot.md): investors were judging the category Airbnb seemed to belong to, not the specific mechanism of the business in front of them.
