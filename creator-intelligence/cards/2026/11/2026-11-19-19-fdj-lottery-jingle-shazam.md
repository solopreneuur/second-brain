# A lottery jingle gets Shazamed 5x the norm

**The find:** Sound-branding agency Sixième Son's sonic identity for FDJ, the French national lottery, got Shazamed by 70.3% of the brand film's audience — four to five times the average Shazam rate for brand films.
**The surprise:** A lottery's sound hook outperforms almost every other brand film on a real, unprompted engagement metric: people reaching for their phones mid-ad because the sound itself was worth identifying.
**The idea:** Sonic distinctiveness can be measured by unplanned consumer behavior, not just recall surveys — if people Shazam your ad, the sound did the branding's job before the visuals got a chance.
