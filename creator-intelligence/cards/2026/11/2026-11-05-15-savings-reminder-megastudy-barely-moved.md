# Two million nudges, barely moved

**The find:** A megastudy sent seven different savings-reminder email campaigns to 1.93 million real bank customers. The average effect across all seven: a 0.05 percentage point increase in making a savings deposit — a 0.51% relative lift.
**The surprise:** Run on nearly two million real customers, the best behavioral-science-designed savings nudge barely moved anyone — an effect invisible without a sample size in the millions.
**The idea:** Reminders are the cheapest nudge to deploy and also one of the weakest at scale, independently confirmed in a brand-new, enormous real-world dataset.
