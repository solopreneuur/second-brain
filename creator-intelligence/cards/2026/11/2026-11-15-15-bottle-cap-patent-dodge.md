# Bottle caps dodged one patent

**The find:** The now-universal continuous-thread bottle cap exists because early-1900s glass and cap makers, trying to avoid royalties on a patented 1906 quarter-turn closure, converged on a rival thread design that Britain standardized in 1918 and the US followed in 1924.
**The surprise:** The interchangeable cap-and-bottle fit everyone assumes is just engineering sense was really a coordinated industry workaround to dodge paying one company's patent.
**The idea:** none
