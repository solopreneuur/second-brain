# The 3.4oz airport liquid limit is real bomb math

**The find:** After the 2006 transatlantic liquid-explosive plot, explosives experts calculated the maximum volume of liquid explosive that could NOT breach an aircraft's hull if detonated — about 100 ml. The US converted that figure and rounded it to 3.4oz specifically to match the international 100 ml standard, instead of using a cleaner 3oz.
**The surprise:** The number everyone assumes is an arbitrary, faintly absurd piece of security theater is actually the opposite — a precise, calculated figure most travelers have never heard explained.
**The idea:** A rule that reads as arbitrary bureaucracy can be hiding exact, deliberate calculation nobody bothered to publicize ([Concealed precision](../../../concepts/concealed-precision.md)).
