# Bees don't vote, they swarm

**The find:** When a honeybee colony needs a new home, scout bees "waggle dance" for candidate sites with vigor proportional to quality. The swarm moves the instant roughly 15-20 scouts are simultaneously present at one site — a quorum — even while other scouts are still dancing for a rival option.
**The surprise:** The rule isn't "everyone agrees" — it's "enough independent, undecided observers happen to converge physically at the same time," which is faster than consensus and just as accurate.
**The idea:** [Quorum sensing](../../../concepts/quorum-sensing.md) beats either a premature top-down call or exhaustive consensus-seeking — a few independent signals converging outperforms committee agreement.
