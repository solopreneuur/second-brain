# Honda's team rejected its biggest hit

**The find:** Sears and Montgomery Ward both offered to carry Honda's small 50cc Super Cub in the early 1960s; American Honda's own executives, committed to competing with Harley on big bikes, turned them down.
**The surprise:** The bike that made Honda America's dominant motorcycle brand is the one Honda's own team tried to keep off the shelves.
**The idea:** [Idea-value blindness](../../../concepts/idea-value-blindness.md) — the people with the most domain expertise are often the worst predictors of a shift, because their model of "how this business works" is the thing about to be broken.
