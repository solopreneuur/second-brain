# One form field was quietly costing 12 million a year

**The find:** Expedia's VP of analytics, Joe Megibow, found a single optional "Company Name" field on the booking form, placed right under "Name," was causing confused customers to type their bank's name instead — which triggered address-mismatch errors with their credit card and silently killed the transaction. Deleting the one field produced an immediate $12 million increase in annual profit.
**The surprise:** The field wasn't annoying people into leaving, the usual friction story — it was quietly sabotaging payments in a way invisible in support tickets and visible only once someone looked at the conversion data.
**The idea:** none
