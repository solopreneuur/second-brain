# One word doubled a beauty site

**The find:** NYX Cosmetics tested the social-proof line under its buy button: "71 beauties have viewed this product today" versus "71 beauties have purchased this product today." Swapping one word lifted click-through from 32% to 43% and doubled the transaction rate.
**The surprise:** Both lines reported the exact same underlying fact — 71 people engaged with the page — but one verb change turned ambiguous curiosity into decisive social proof.
**The idea:** [Social proof](../../../concepts/social-proof.md) is sensitive to the specificity of the proof claim, not just its presence — "purchased" signals committed behavior, "viewed" signals nothing.
