# Amex ran freight wagons for a century before cards

**The find:** American Express was founded in 1850 by Henry Wells, William Fargo, and John Butterfield as a freight-forwarding business, moving packages, cash, and securities by horse and rail across the country. It didn't introduce money orders until 1882, traveler's cheques until 1891, and its first charge card until 1958.
**The surprise:** A company whose entire modern identity is financial services and credit operated as a literal delivery and logistics company for over a hundred years first — longer than it's existed as the brand people know.
**The idea:** A company's current identity can be a late addition bolted onto a much older, completely unrelated core business, drifting category by category rather than pivoting once ([Category drift](../../../concepts/category-drift.md)).
