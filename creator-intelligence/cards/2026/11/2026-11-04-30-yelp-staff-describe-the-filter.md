# Yelp's own staff can't explain the filter

**The find:** Several self-identified former Yelp employees, across separate Reddit threads, independently describe a review-filtering algorithm that frequently moves genuine five-star reviews into a "not recommended" bucket that doesn't count toward a business's score, while one says they personally watched reviews flip to "unrecommended" shortly after a business declined an ad pitch.
**The surprise:** The people who worked inside the system can't fully explain why it suppresses good reviews either, and some describe watching it happen right after a sales call.
**The idea:** A platform's credibility can rest on an algorithm that is opaque even to the staff who sell against it.
