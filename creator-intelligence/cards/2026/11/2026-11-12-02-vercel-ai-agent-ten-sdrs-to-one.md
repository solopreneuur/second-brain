# Vercel cut its SDR team from 10 to 1

**The find:** Vercel COO Jeanne Grosser says a single GTM engineer, working on it part-time, built an AI lead-qualification agent in six weeks that let Vercel shrink its inbound sales-development team from 10 people to 1, with lead-to-opportunity conversion staying flat.
**The surprise:** It isn't a story of AI helping reps do more. It's a 10-to-1 headcount collapse on one specific function, built by one part-time engineer, with the output quality unchanged.
**The idea:** When a narrow, repetitive judgment task gets automated well, the team doesn't get faster, it gets smaller.

2 · Shocking number · Marketing · [Tom Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/vercel-ai-sales-agents-jeanne-grosser/)

## In depth

Vercel, the company behind the Next.js framework and a cloud platform for frontend deployment, had a conventional inbound sales-development setup: a team of 10 SDRs working leads generated by marketing, qualifying them before handing them to account executives. According to COO Jeanne Grosser, one "GTM engineer" — spending only about 25-30% of his time on the project — built an AI agent to do the qualification work instead. The build took roughly six weeks before the team felt confident moving from 10 humans to 1.

The nine redeployed SDRs moved to outbound work rather than being laid off, and Grosser reports lead-to-opportunity conversion held steady rather than degrading, which is the detail that makes the story more than a cost-cutting anecdote: the replaced function's output quality didn't drop. Grosser's own framing, as reported, was blunt: "For us, we had 10 SDRs doing this inbound workflow, and now we just have one," and "the agent is as good as our humans were." Vercel expects the remaining reps' customer-facing time to rise from about 30% to 70% of their day, since the mechanical qualification work is gone.

The broader pattern worth watching: this is a case where a single engineer, working part-time, produced a 10x headcount compression on a defined, repeatable judgment task (does this lead meet criteria X, Y, Z) rather than a creative or relationship-dependent one. It's a useful marker for which jobs are vulnerable to this kind of agent-replacement first: narrow, rule-governed, measurable, high-volume — not necessarily "junior" in title, but mechanical in function.
