# Showing the price first only helps a real bargain

**The find:** Uma Karmarkar's fMRI study found that showing a price before the product shifts shoppers into "is this worth it" evaluation mode, which boosts sales when the discount is genuinely strong, but for a weak or unimpressive discount, showing the price first actually sold worse than showing the product first, because the early price triggered scrutiny the product couldn't survive.
**The surprise:** The same "show the price first" tactic that strengthens a great deal actively damages a mediocre one, it isn't a universally good or bad ordering, its effect flips depending on how good the underlying offer actually is.
**The idea:** An anchor doesn't just shift a number, it changes which mental mode a person evaluates the whole offer in, and that mode can help or hurt depending on what's being evaluated ([Anchoring](../../../concepts/anchoring.md)).
