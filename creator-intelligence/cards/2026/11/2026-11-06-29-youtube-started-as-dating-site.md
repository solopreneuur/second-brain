# YouTube began as a dating site

**The find:** Before it was a video platform, YouTube launched in February 2005 as a video-dating site called "Tune In, Hook Up," with its founders offering women $100 on Craigslist to post dating videos of themselves. Almost nobody did, so they opened it to any video instead.
**The surprise:** The world's second-largest search engine began as a failed dating site, rescued by giving up on its own idea.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): the use people actually wanted was discovered only after the intended one failed.
