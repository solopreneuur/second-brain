# Why airlines obsess over paint chemistry

**The find:** Southwest Airlines paint engineer Tony Brown explains in aviation maintenance trade press that switching to a modern basecoat-clearcoat paint system extends the interval between repaints from roughly five years to seven — and a full Airbus A380 paint job weighs about 1,170 pounds.
**The surprise:** An airline's paint department isn't chasing looks — it's running a fuel-burn and downtime calculation, where every extra year a paint job lasts is a maintenance slot and a weight penalty the airline doesn't have to pay for.
**The idea:** Behind an everyday cosmetic choice, an industry's real decision is almost always a cost or weight trade-off most customers never see.
