# Panasonic's 250-year founding plan

**The find:** In 1932, founder Konosuke Matsushita told staff the company's true mission would take 250 years to fulfil, broken into ten phases of 25 years each: 10 years building, 10 years active work, 5 years giving back.
**The surprise:** A founder set a roadmap that wouldn't complete until the year 2182, explicitly planning past his own life, his children's lives, and several CEO successions.
**The idea:** [Multigenerational planning](../../../concepts/multigenerational-planning.md): a horizon no single leader will live to see forces short-term decisions to be judged against something bigger than quarterly results.
