# Casket shoppers always pick the middle

**The find:** The FTC's own funeral-industry guidance states that casket buyers almost always choose one of the first three models a funeral director shows them, usually the middle-priced one, which is why directors are advised to open with costlier models first.
**The surprise:** A government consumer-protection document openly explains the sales psychology being used against the grieving shopper, rather than just warning about price gouging.
**The idea:** [The decoy effect](../../../concepts/decoy-effect.md) works even in the most emotionally loaded purchase a person makes, because grief suppresses comparison shopping.
