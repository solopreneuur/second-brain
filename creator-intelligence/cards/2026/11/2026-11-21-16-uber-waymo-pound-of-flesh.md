# Uber's Pre-Deal Wishlist Included Theft

**The find:** Trial exhibits in Waymo v. Uber show Travis Kalanick's pre-acquisition notes listing "source, all of their data, Tagging, road map, pound of flesh, IP" — and Uber's own due-diligence found engineer Anthony Levandowski still holding roughly 14,000 stolen Google files before Uber closed the $680 million Otto deal anyway.
**The surprise:** This wasn't an oversight discovered after the fact — Uber's own investigators documented the theft in writing, and the deal closed regardless.
**The idea:** [Concealed precision](../../../concepts/concealed-precision.md) — the paper trail proving a company knew exactly what it was doing, in its own words.
