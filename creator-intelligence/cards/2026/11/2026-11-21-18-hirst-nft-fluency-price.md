# Same Art, Easier Title, Pricier NFT

**The find:** Economists analyzed 4,041 resale transactions of Damien Hirst's 10,000-piece "The Currency" NFT project — identical algorithm-generated dots, each given a unique title drawn from song lyrics.
**The surprise:** Tokens with shorter, easier-to-read titles sold for significantly more on resale, even though the art, the artist and the provenance were all held constant.
**The idea:** [Cognitive fluency](../../../concepts/cognitive-fluency.md) — a project designed by one of the world's most famous living artists specifically to interrogate what gives art its value still fell to the same bias.
