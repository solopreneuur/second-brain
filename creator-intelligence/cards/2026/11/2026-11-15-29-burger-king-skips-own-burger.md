# Burger King sent customers to McDonald's

**The find:** On McDonald's Argentina's 2017 charity "McHappy Day," Burger King pulled the Whopper from all 107 of its Argentina stores and told its own customers to go buy a Big Mac instead, generating 206 million impressions.
**The surprise:** A challenger brand didn't just tolerate a rival's charity day, it actively sent its own paying customers across the street to the competitor, and came out ahead for it.
