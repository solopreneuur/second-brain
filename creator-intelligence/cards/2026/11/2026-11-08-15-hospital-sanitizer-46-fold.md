# Three Tweaks, a 46-Fold Jump

**The find:** A Danish field experiment on 46,435 hospital visitors found baseline hand-sanitizer use was 0.43% — moving the station earlier in the entry path, adding a pink sign, and a duct-tape floor line (no persuasion at all) pushed compliance to 19.66%, a 46-fold jump from three placement tweaks.
**The surprise:** The national hand-hygiene ad campaign, pure persuasion, barely moved the needle; silent positioning and salience cues that cost nothing did nine times better.
**The idea:** [Friction](../../../concepts/friction.md): where a healthy default sits and how visible it is matters more than any message about why you should use it.
