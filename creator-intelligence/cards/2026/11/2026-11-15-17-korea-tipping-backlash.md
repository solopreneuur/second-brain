# Korea's tip jars got sent back

**The find:** When a Seoul ride-hailing app and a Seoul bakery both tried adding an optional tip in 2023, both drew immediate public backlash — the bakery pulled its tip jar within days — and a 12,106-person survey found over 70% of Koreans call tipping "unacceptable."
**The surprise:** A gesture Americans treat as baseline politeness reads in Korea as an insult — offering money implies the worker needs charity, or that you're flaunting status over them.
**The idea:** [Chemyon](../../../concepts/chemyon.md): the social standing a person is owed, which money can threaten as easily as it can help.
