# A chatbot beat a text at raising shots

**The find:** Argentina's health ministry and the Behavioural Insights Team randomized about 250,000 people in Chaco province into a two-way WhatsApp chatbot, a one-way SMS reminder, or nothing, ahead of COVID booster eligibility. The chatbot more than tripled vaccine uptake compared to no message, nearly doubling the plain text's effect.
**The surprise:** The content of the reminder barely mattered — letting people "talk back" to a bot outperformed just telling them what to do.
**The idea:** [Interactive nudge](../../../concepts/interactive-nudge.md) — a message that can respond to you changes behavior more than the same information delivered one-way.
