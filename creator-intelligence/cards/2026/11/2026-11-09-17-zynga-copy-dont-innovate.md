# Zynga's CEO ordered "copy, don't innovate"

**The find:** A former Zynga employee told SF Weekly in 2010 that CEO Mark Pincus said in an internal meeting: "I don't fucking want innovation. I want you to copy what they do and do it until you get their numbers." Zynga's FarmVille-era strategy of cloning rival games followed that instruction almost literally.
**The surprise:** A founder stated the anti-innovation doctrine out loud as deliberate policy, and it worked brilliantly for years before competitors caught on.
**The idea:** No named concept — explicit imitation, stated as strategy rather than hidden as embarrassment.
