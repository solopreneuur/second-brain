# McDonald's Menu Board Is a Salesman

**The find:** McDonald's AI-personalized Dynamic Yield menu boards, now running in more than 11,000 of about 14,000 US stores, are publicly credited with cutting drive-thru time by nearly 29 seconds year-over-year.
**The surprise:** The stated reason is "menu simplification" — the real mechanism is an upsell algorithm quietly deciding what you see based on weather, time of day and your own order history.
**The idea:** [Disguised function](../../../concepts/disguised-function.md) — a convenience upgrade that's actually a sales algorithm wearing a menu costume.
