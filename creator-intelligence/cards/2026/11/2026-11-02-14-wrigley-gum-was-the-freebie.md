# Wrigley's gum was a free gift twice over

**The find:** William Wrigley Jr. started in 1891 selling scouring soap, gave away baking powder as a bonus; customers liked the bonus more, so he started selling baking powder — and gave away gum as the new bonus. Customers liked that more too, so by 1893 he was selling gum.
**The surprise:** It took two full rounds of "the free gift outsold the thing I was selling" before Wrigley built a company around the bonus instead of the product.
**The idea:** [Accidental product](../../../concepts/accidental-product.md): watch what customers hoard unprompted, the giveaway may be worth more than what you charge for.
