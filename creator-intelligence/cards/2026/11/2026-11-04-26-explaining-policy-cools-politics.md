# Explaining a policy cools your politics

**The find:** People rated their political positions (cap-and-trade, a flat tax) less extreme after being asked to explain, mechanism by mechanism, how the policy would produce its effect, than after simply listing reasons they held the view; those who explained also donated less afterward to a matching advocacy group.
**The surprise:** Arguing for a side hardens it, but being asked how it actually works, not why you believe it, is what softens it.
**The idea:** The [illusion of explanatory depth](../../../concepts/illusion-of-explanatory-depth.md) extends past gadgets and zippers into political conviction: confidence in a policy opinion often rests on a mechanism you've never actually spelled out.
