# Steam hid ratings, reviews changed

**The find:** When Steam quietly reduced how prominently a game's average rating was shown to reviewers in 2019, a 10% higher average rating's effect on the next review being positive dropped from 5.4 percentage points to 2.8 — and the bias was asymmetric, with low ratings dragging reviews down harder than high ratings lifted them.
**The surprise:** It wasn't a lab experiment or a survey — a quiet UI tweak on a real platform became an accidental randomized experiment proving exactly how much a visible star rating warps the next person's opinion.
**The idea:** Social proof isn't just a nudge, it's measurable as a real swing in how people vote — and a design choice as small as showing or hiding a number can turn that swing up or down ([Social proof](../../../concepts/social-proof.md)).
