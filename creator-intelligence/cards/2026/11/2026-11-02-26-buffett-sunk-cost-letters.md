# Buffett called his own purchase a mistake, the same year

**The find:** In Berkshire's 1979 letter Buffett writes "the purchase was a mistake" about a second ailing textile mill he'd just bought, and in 1980 admits he was too slow to face the textile wind-down — in the same letter where regulators forced him to sell a bank he loved, one earning 2.3% on assets.
**The surprise:** He repeats a mistake he's already on record regretting, in real time, while simultaneously being forced to exit his best asset.
**The idea:** [Sunk-cost fallacy](../../../concepts/sunk-cost-fallacy.md): people cling to both bad and good positions past the rational point, just for different reasons.
