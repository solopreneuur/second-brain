# "Claim your kit" beat "join the program"

**The find:** Livongo emailed over 15,000 people eligible for its diabetes program. Swapping "Join the Program" for "Claim Your Welcome Kit" across the subject line, headline, button and body raised open rates 25%, clicks 88%, and registrations more than 120% within a week.
**The surprise:** The offer and the kit never changed. Only the frame did, and it more than doubled signups.
**The idea:** [Endowed progress effect](../../../concepts/endowed-progress-effect.md): framing something as already yours to claim beats framing the identical thing as something to go join.
