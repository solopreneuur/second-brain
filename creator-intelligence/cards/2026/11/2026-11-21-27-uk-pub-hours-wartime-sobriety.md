# Britain's Pub Hours Were a War Tactic

**The find:** Under the Defence of the Realm Act 1914, UK pub hours were cut from a pre-war norm of around 16-17 hours a day to about 5.5 hours on weekdays, with the stated goal of keeping munitions workers sober enough to maximize weapons output.
**The surprise:** The resulting licensing-hours gap — Britain's whole "last orders" ritual — persisted until the Licensing Act 1988, outliving the war it was designed for by 70 years.
**The idea:** [Ritual without understanding](../../../concepts/ritual-without-understanding.md) — a wartime productivity intervention became permanent custom long after anyone remembered why.
