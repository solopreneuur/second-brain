# 'Women and children first' is mostly myth

**The find:** A 2012 study of 18 shipwrecks and over 15,000 people found men survived at nearly double the rate of women, and crew outsurvived passengers. "Women and children first" actually held in only 5 of the 18 sinkings studied.
**The surprise:** The one disaster everyone cites as proof the rule works, the Titanic, is the statistical outlier — researchers found it was driven by one captain's specific order, misheard by many as "women and children only."
**The idea:** [Crisis self-preservation](../../../concepts/crisis-self-preservation.md) — the behavior people assume is the default in a crisis is usually the exception, not the rule; real crisis behavior defaults to self-preservation by whoever holds the most power in the room.
