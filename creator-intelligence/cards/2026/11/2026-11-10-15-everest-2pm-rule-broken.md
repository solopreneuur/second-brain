# Everest's 2pm rule, broken once

**The find:** Guide Rob Hall set a 2pm turnaround time before his 1996 Everest climb — reach the summit by 2pm or turn back, no matter how close — but on summit day it wasn't enforced, and climbers were still pushing upward after 3:45pm when a storm hit; eight people died, including Hall.
**The surprise:** The rule that could have prevented the deaths already existed and had been agreed to in advance — it failed only because nobody followed it once the summit was in sight.
**The idea:** [Precommitment](../../../concepts/precommitment.md) — deciding your exit condition in advance, while calm, because you can't trust the decision you'll make under pressure with the goal in sight.
