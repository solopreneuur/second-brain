# BioShock's hidden push toward its shop

**The find:** BioShock's designers built a hidden system that watches what resource a player is low on and quietly weights drops toward money instead, nudging players toward the game's vending machines.
**The surprise:** The game is secretly managing your scarcity to steer you toward its own in-game stores, and the "difficulty" you feel is partly a shopping cue in disguise.
**The idea:** Hidden mechanic steering — a system can create a felt experience (scarcity, urgency) on purpose to steer behavior toward a built-in objective, without the person ever seeing the lever.
