# Pixar's shelved "jerk" Woody

**The find:** On November 19, 1993 — "Black Friday" inside Pixar — Disney executives screened a rough cut of *Toy Story* with a sarcastic, mean-spirited Woody, shaped by notes pushing for "edgier," and halted production entirely. Tom Hanks reportedly broke character mid-recording to say "this guy is a jerk"; John Lasseter's team spent months rewriting Woody into the insecure leader that shipped in 1995.
**The surprise:** Disney's own creative notes nearly produced a *Toy Story* whose hero test audiences would have despised, forcing the studio to shut down the film it had greenlit to undo its own input.
**The idea:** No named concept — the hidden reason behind Woody's final personality is a studio halting itself mid-production to reverse its own notes.
