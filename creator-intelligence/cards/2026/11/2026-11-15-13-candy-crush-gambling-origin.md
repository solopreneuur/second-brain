# Candy Crush's gambling-site past

**The find:** King.com began in 2003 as Midasplayer.com, a UK real-money skill-tournament site where players paid entry fees and winners split 75% of the pot; the tournament mechanic survived King's 2011 pivot to Facebook and casual games.
**The surprise:** The maker of a cheerful, kid-friendly mobile game spent its first eight years running what was functionally a gambling operation.
**The idea:** none
