# The bike shed gets the real debate

**The find:** The "law of triviality," also called bike-shedding, names why a committee can approve a £10 million nuclear reactor in minutes but spend 45 minutes arguing over a £350 bicycle shed. Everyone understands the bike shed well enough to have an opinion, so it invites endless debate, while the reactor is too complex to challenge.
**The surprise:** The more expensive and consequential a decision is, the less scrutiny it tends to get, because nobody feels qualified to argue with it.
**The idea:** [Law of triviality](../../../concepts/law-of-triviality.md): debate time tracks how confident people feel giving an opinion, not how much is actually at stake.
