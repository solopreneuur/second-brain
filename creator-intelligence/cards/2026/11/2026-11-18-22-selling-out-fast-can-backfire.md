# Selling out fast can backfire

**The find:** A September 2025 study of limited-edition sneakers, pens and electronics accessories found that immediate sellouts don't boost desire for next time — they trigger feelings of exclusion and unfairness that lower repurchase intent, especially for ordinary, everyday products.
**The surprise:** Marketers treat "sold out in minutes" as free hype, but the data shows it mostly angers the majority who missed out and quietly erodes long-term loyalty.
**The idea:** Scarcity is usually taught as pure upside; this shows the same tactic can corrode the brand it was meant to help once the product isn't a luxury good people expect to chase ([Scarcity backfire](../../../concepts/scarcity-backfire.md)).
