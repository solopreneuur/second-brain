# Nvidia's CEO wouldn't build it again

**The find:** Asked on How I Built This whether, knowing everything now, he would start Nvidia again, Jensen Huang — the CEO of the world's most valuable company — says no, citing years of "pain and suffering" and "embarrassment and humiliation" along the way.
**The surprise:** The builder of the most valuable company on Earth tells his own origin story as a warning, not a pitch, saying he'd tell his 30-year-old self not to do it.
**The idea:** Survivors of a brutal founding story often can't honestly recommend the path that worked for them — success doesn't erase the cost of getting there.
