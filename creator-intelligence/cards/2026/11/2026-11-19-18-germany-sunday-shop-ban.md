# Germany makes it illegal to shop on Sunday

**The find:** Under Germany's Ladenschlussgesetz (Shop Closing Law), almost all retail stores are legally required to stay shut on Sundays and public holidays nationwide, a rule that dates back to 1956 and is still enforced today, with only narrow exceptions like bakeries, pharmacies and gas stations.
**The surprise:** The law isn't a relic nobody follows — German retailers that try to open on Sunday can be fined, and the rule survives repeated legal challenges because it's framed as protecting workers' rest and religious observance, not as an economic restriction.
**The idea:** A rule that looks purely economic to outsiders can be legally untouchable once it's reframed as protecting a social or religious right instead.
