# Netflix's founding myth was invented later

**The find:** Netflix co-founder Marc Randolph says the famous story — Reed Hastings got a $40 Blockbuster late fee for Apollo 13 and started Netflix out of spite — is false. In his 2019 book he calls it "a convenient fiction" invented years after launch, since Netflix charged late fees itself at the start; the real genesis was a cold, analytical 1997 bet to become "the Amazon.com of something," landing on DVDs because they were cheap to ship and durable.
**The surprise:** The company most often cited as proof that founders need a great origin myth admits its own myth was fabricated after the fact, to make a mundane logistics decision sound inevitable.
**The idea:** The [narrative fallacy](../../../concepts/narrative-fallacy.md) — a tidy founding story gets constructed retroactively to explain a decision that was really just careful, boring analysis.
