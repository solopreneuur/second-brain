# "Get a Free Quote" beat "Book a Free Survey" by 60%

**The find:** Home security company ADT tested three button labels for the same home-visit appointment: "Book a Free Survey," "Book An ADT Survey," and "Get a Free Quote." The "quote" version wasn't just a little better, it beat both others outright and lifted conversions by 60% within two weeks.
**The surprise:** "Survey," the accurate word for what the appointment actually was, tested worse than "quote," a word that wasn't literally true of what would happen; people respond to the benefit they want, not the accurate description of the process.
**The idea:** Name a call to action after the outcome the user wants, not the process you're actually running.
