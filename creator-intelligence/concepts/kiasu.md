# Kiasu

What it is: A named, culturally mainstream Singaporean personality trait (Hokkien for "afraid to lose") describing the anxious competitiveness Western marketers vaguely call FOMO.
Also known as: fear of missing out (Western equivalent, usually unnamed as a trait)
